## Snapshot
Wolfspeed is a US-listed, **vertically integrated, pure-play silicon-carbide (SiC) company** — it grows its own SiC crystal/substrates and sells **(1) SiC + GaN materials** (wafers/substrates) and **(2) SiC power devices** (MOSFETs/modules). Two product lines: **Power Products ~67% of revenue, Materials Products ~33%** (10-Q, 2026-05-07; Power $218.4M vs Materials $100.3M over the Successor period 9/30/25–3/29/26). End-markets are **Automotive/EV + fast charging** and **Industrial & Energy** (AI data centers, grid modernization, renewables/storage) (10-Q, 2026-05-07). **The headline is the balance sheet, not the end-markets:** Wolfspeed filed a **prepackaged Chapter 11** (RSA 2025-06-22, confirmed 2025-09-08) and **emerged 2025-09-29**, cutting total debt ~70% and adopting **fresh-start accounting** — old equity was nearly wiped (existing holders kept ~1.3M of 25.8M new shares, ~5%; convertible noteholders took the rest) (10-Q, 2026-05-07). It is a sub-$200M-quarter, gross-loss, cash-burning turnaround with a SiC-800V optionality call layered on top — own the restructuring before you own the theme.

## Position in the value chain
Wolfspeed is **vertically integrated in SiC** — it grows its own SiC boules/crystal and substrates (assuring supply, controlling cost), then fabs/packages SiC power devices, and also sells bare SiC/GaN materials to third parties (10-Q, 2026-05-07). It sells into **EV/automotive, industrial & energy (grid, renewables/storage), and the emerging 800V AI-datacenter power** stack — the latter the only real growth vector right now (datacenter rev +50% QoQ in Q2 FY26, +30% QoQ in Q3 FY26; 10kV MOSFET launched; customers migrating 400V→800V) (Q3/Q2 FY26 calls). **But the operative fact is the post-Chapter-11 balance sheet**: ~70% debt cut, fresh-start basis, near-total equity wipeout, still running negative gross margin on Mohawk Valley underutilization. SiC sits at infrastructure voltages (>1,000V) in the 800V debate; JPM names WOLF among 800V beneficiaries but is **Underweight the equity** (JPM, 2025-12-15) — a small-cap, levered, going-concern-recovered name with a distressed-equity profile, not a clean compounder. BofA's "Watts to Tokens" full model reinforces the SiC-at-infrastructure angle: it sizes the **SiC device pool at $183m (CY25) → $2,098m (CY30), a 63% CAGR**, and lists Wolfspeed among the key SiC vendors, with SiC named **"the primary material winner"** in solid-state transformers (SSTs) and the best device type in HVDC solid-state circuit breakers (SSCBs, 750V/1200V/1700V-class); WOLF's high-voltage edge is explicitly BofA's SST illustration — **"Wolfspeed's 10kV die can enable a simple two-level cell"** vs the series-stacked multi-level architectures MV-rated SiC requires (BofA "Watts to Tokens" full model, 2026-05-25). BofA also sources its **"medium-voltage transformer lead times up to 3 years"** SST-tailwind datapoint from Wolfspeed. But WOLF is inside the model's "Other" bucket — no named revenue/share line among the eight tracked suppliers — and BofA flags SST scaling challenges (reliability/thermal/electrical stress) that push the "end-state" SST inflection to CY28-30, so this is optionality sizing, not near-term WOLF revenue. See [800v-dc-power](themes/800v-dc-power.md).
