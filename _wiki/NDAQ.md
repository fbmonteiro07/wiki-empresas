## Snapshot

Nasdaq is now mostly a recurring-revenue market-infrastructure company rather than a trading venue: Solutions (Capital Access Platforms plus Financial Technology) were 76% of FY2025 net revenue and 79% in Q3 2026 (Capstone arithmetic on company figures). The thesis is that three engines compound: Index licensing on the Nasdaq-100 franchise (ETPs, CME futures, structured and insurance products), bank software (Verafin financial-crime, surveillance, and AxiomSL and Calypso from the 2023 Adenza deal) cross-sold into the same clients, and a listings and data franchise. Market Services adds volume-linked cash flow. The open tests are whether Verafin finally reaches the mid-20s growth management keeps underwriting, how much of the 39% Index growth is Nasdaq-100 market beta, and whether 23/5 trading, tokenization and the IPO revival extend the exchange franchise or fragment it while the expense guide keeps rising. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-22](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-10-22_earningsrelease3q26ex-991.htm); [Adena Friedman / Sarah Youngwood, Q3 call, 2026-10-22](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-22.md); [Q4 2025 call, 2026-01-29](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-01-29.md); Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

**FinTech: record ARR growth, and Verafin is finally accelerating.** Financial Crime revenue grew 23% and its ARR 19% (17% in Q2), with net revenue retention of 111%. Management attributes the pickup to enterprise clients signed in 2H 2025 going live after roughly 12-month implementations. Verafin signed 45 SMB clients and 3 enterprise deals in Q3, taking 2026 enterprise signings to 12 against 9 in all of 2025. More than 850 clients use its Agentic AI Workforce, and over 70 have signed paying contracts under a "try before you buy" model. Cloud solutions were 85% of FinTech ACV bookings; Friedman puts the ACV uplift on a cloud migration at about 1.5x. Capital Markets Technology was helped by a trade-management price increase effective January 1 and by episodic paid testing that "starts to trail off" in Q1; Calypso upfront revenue in Q4 2025 makes a tough Q4 comparison. [Release](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-10-22_earningsrelease3q26ex-991.htm); [Friedman / Youngwood, Q3 call, 2026-10-22](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-22.md); [Youngwood, Q2 call, 2026-07-23](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q2-2026-earnings_2026-07-23.md).

**Index: Nasdaq-100 volume and price, plus some alpha.** Youngwood attributed 15 points of the 39% Index growth to alpha, which leaves roughly 24 points to market factors (Capstone arithmetic). Quarterly ETP net inflows were lumpy: $35bn in Q4 2025, $6bn in Q1, $51bn in Q2 and $49bn in Q3 2026 (company releases). Licensed derivatives volumes rose 70% with a mix shift from E-mini to lower-priced micro E-mini futures; Index ARR covers "a very small portion" of the business. New Nasdaq-100 ETFs from BlackRock and State Street carry licence terms "consistent with the QQQ pricing terms" and exceed $1bn of combined AUM. Half of the 32 Q3 product launches were outside the U.S., and products launched in the last five years drew 35% of TTM inflows. [Q4 2025](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-01-29_d103698dex991.htm), [Q1](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-04-23_d148002dex991.htm), [Q2](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-07-23_earningsrelease2q26ex-991.htm) and [Q3 releases](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-10-22_earningsrelease3q26ex-991.htm); [Friedman, Q1 call, 2026-04-23](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-04-23.md); [Q3 call](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-22.md).

**Costs, capital and market structure.** The 2026 opex guide has been raised at every print, from $2.455–2.535bn in January to $2.550–2.580bn: +$70m (+2.8%) at the midpoint. The latest rise reflects revenue-linked compensation, marketing for a stronger IPO market, technology spend and the Dasseti and LeveL deals. The new range implies Q4 opex of $663–693m against $609m in Q4 2025 (Capstone arithmetic on 9M opex of $1,887m), yet the Q3 operating margin still rose a point. Capital return has pivoted from deleveraging to buybacks: $1.2bn repurchased in 9M 2026 against $616m in all of 2025, plus a $315–385m ASR launched in October, with gross leverage at 2.5x against the "mid to high 2x" target set at the February Investor Day. Market Services grew only 2%. Equities capture was strong, but U.S. options capture fell as order flow shifted toward consolidators, and European retail pricing was cut to defend share. LeveL (the third-largest U.S. ATS, closed October 15) should add $7–8m of revenue in Q4. Separately, Nasdaq bought $100m of Payward (Kraken) Series E preferred stock in September. [Releases, 2026-01-29 to 2026-10-22](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-10-22_earningsrelease3q26ex-991.htm); [Youngwood, Q3 call](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-22.md); [Q1 call, 2026-04-23](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-04-23.md); [Q3 10-Q](../NDAQ/NDAQ_10-Q_2026-10-22_0001120193-26-000020.html).

**Bull case — recurring software and index annuities now carry the growth.** Solutions organic growth of 14%, 17% and 18% in the first three quarters of 2026 runs well above the 9–12% medium-term outlook raised at the February Investor Day (as relayed by Jefferies). Even after removing the roughly 24 points of Index growth not attributed to alpha, Q3 Solutions growth is about 12%, at the top of that range (Capstone arithmetic: ($169m − ~$49m) / $1,003m). FinTech is selling cloud migrations at a higher ACV and adding AI modules to a captive bank base. The Nasdaq-100 brand is spreading across sponsors, geographies and derivatives. Strong free cash flow (management cites $2.3bn TTM at 98% conversion) funds buybacks at 2.5x leverage. Redburn rates the stock Buy and sees AI fears, "most acute around ICE and Nasdaq", as over-discounted. [Q1](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-04-23_d148002dex991.htm)–[Q3 releases](../NDAQ/earnings/2026-10-22_earningsrelease3q26ex-991.htm); [Q3 call](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-22.md); [Redburn / Clinch, 2026-06-11](../NDAQ/commentary/2026-06-11_Exchanges-deep-dive-CME-u-g-to-Buy-top-pick-ICE-Nasdaq-DB1-all-Buy-MarketAxess-d-g-to-Neutral-CBOE-T_a03cddca.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — beta, a slipping FinCrime target and rising costs.** A large part of the 2026 upside is the Nasdaq-100: Index revenue and licensed futures volumes ride tech-heavy market levels and retail activity, and inflows can stall (Q1). The promised FinCrime acceleration slipped from "starting in the fourth quarter" of 2025 to the second half of 2026, and LLM entrants in bank software (the FIS/Anthropic tool) raise the question of whether Verafin's consortium data and agents stay differentiated. Opex guidance has risen every quarter. Capital Markets Technology faces a tough Q4 comparison and fading test revenue. Market Services is flat in a lower-volatility tape, and new venues (token venues, ATSs, perps) compete for the same order flow. [Q2 2025 call, 2025-07-24](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q2-2025-earnings_2025-07-24.md); [Q2 2026 call](../NDAQ/transcripts/NDAQ_Q2-2026-earnings_2026-07-23.md); [Jefferies sales, 2026-05-05](../NDAQ/commentary/2026-05-05_First-Look-KKR-PYPL-FISV-SHOP-Quick-Thought-FIS-and-NDAQ_3cfc2a86.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** Financial Crime growth of 25% or more with ARR above 20% in Q4/1Q, plus a 2027 expense guide that preserves margin expansion, would support the bull case. On the downside, watch for an Index print that stalls with the Nasdaq-100, Financial Crime ARR slipping back below 17%, or 23/5 and token venues moving share without new revenue.
