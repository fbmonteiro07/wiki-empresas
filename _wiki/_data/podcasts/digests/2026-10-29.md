### Satya Nadella on Open vs Closed Models: Who Wins | Why Companies Need to Own Their Own Intelligence
_00:23:48 · [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=24XUcWhqY_4) · transcript: YouTube auto-generated (ASR) · relevance: high_

_Picked up from exec-scan: Satya Nadella (Microsoft)_

**TL;DR**

- **MAI weights will be "licensed", not open, and Microsoft wants a software business, not a network-effects one** (Nadella, [00:05:41]–[00:06:43]): *"even with the MAI models uh that will be not open weight… think of it more as licensed weights… I want to turn this into just a simple straightforward software business as opposed to a network effects intelligence business."*
- **A named customer runs an MAI model in production** (Nadella, [00:13:03]): Unilever built an agentic order-to-cash system. On trade claims it started on a frontier model and then substituted *"currently an MAI model… because it's sort of a lower cost and… they control it… they're able to train on their traces."* Unilever's own IT team now runs it after a Microsoft FDE (forward-deployed engineer) engagement.
- **Most tokens will be commodity tokens** (Nadella, [00:07:20]–[00:08:36]): *"there will always be a market for frontier… but I think predominantly token usage will be uh commodity token usage."* That could be open weights or *"cheap closed weights… there'll be lots of price competition."* His evidence is Copilot: *"we use a lot of closed source cheaper models along with uh open weight models."* He gave no percentage, even though Stebbings asked for one over 24 months.
- **Firms must own their intelligence: the "reverse information paradox"** (Nadella, [00:09:11]–[00:11:02]). Building on Hayek and Kenneth Arrow, he says *"the buyer needs the protection"*, because the user's data and *"exhaust"* (thumbs up/down signals) create value for the model company. The surplus should *"remain at the firm"* through weights, context or evals. *"every firm will have a private e-val benchmark, which is their core IP"*, and models will *"hill climb"* on it.
- **Zero-trust containment outside the model** (Nadella, [00:01:32]–[00:04:01]): treat models as possibly compromised. Agent 365 and Project Perception [transcribed "perception"] give every agent an identity and keep tamper-proof logs of tool calls, model calls and the CoT. Policy is set on top and behaviour is monitored. CoT should be *"in English and not in"* neuralese [transcribed "New Release"]. Chain of thought *"can be unfaithful"*, and hiding it makes that worse. This pressure *"may even drive more open weights"* [00:04:45].
- **No single vertically integrated winner** (Nadella, [00:14:25]–[00:15:52]): *"every day you wake up and you say wow there's more competition in every layer of the stack"*, including silicon. He expects *"five, six"* closed and *"five, six"* open-weight models competing, with a router picking among them. His hope is *"horizontal specialization with multiple winners in each layer."*
- **Training budgets are bounded by revenue** (Nadella, [00:15:52]–[00:17:54]). Asked about the $100bn training runs that Stebbings attributes to Mustafa Suleyman, Nadella answered: *"training run, it's just a percentage of R&D… companies that are heavy R&D spend what? 15, 20%?"* On supply: *"we have power shortages, we have silicon shortages… everyone's building… When will all of this come online? What will be the demand supply? No one knows."*
- **Changed his mind:** *"I used to always think that… alignment will just fall out. I no longer believe that"* [00:21:28]. Alignment *"has to show up in training… maybe even in pre-training. I don't think you can even RL your way to alignment"* [00:02:49].
