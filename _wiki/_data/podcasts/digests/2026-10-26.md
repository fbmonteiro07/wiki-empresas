### The New Startup Playbook | Box CEO Aaron Levie
_00:34:24 · [episode](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/the-new-startup-playbook-box-ceo-aaron-levie-I0FN5_nv) · transcript: podscripts.co (machine transcript, no speaker labels) · relevance: medium_

**TL;DR**

- **Agents give 3-, 5-, 10-person startups "the output of a 50 or 100 or a 200-person team" (Levie [00:03:37]).** The 2025 startup vs 2005 is "the most different that I've ever seen"; founders become editors/orchestrators of background agents, reviewing "thousands of lines of code a day" [00:06:30].
- **Leverage from top founders is now "a three to five to 10x form of leverage", vs "10 or 20 percent" a year ago (copilot/autocomplete era) (Levie [00:16:32–00:17:16]).** Incumbents can't port it quickly: codebases lack agent-ready documentation, tooling and methodology — a structural window for startups [00:17:45–00:18:02].
- **AI coding: "20, 30, 40 billion dollars of market cap" created in ~3 years (Replit, Cursor, Windsurf, Lovable, Cognition), and Levie is "perfectly comfortable underwriting a couple hundred billion dollars of market cap in the AI coding space that don't go to incumbents" [00:15:00–00:15:21].** Same thesis "for every single field" — life sciences, healthcare, legal, consulting — outrunning both software and services incumbents [00:15:50].
- **"Hundreds of companies" at $1bn/$5bn/$10bn+ market cap, one per job-function × industry cell — "literally 5,000 cells" (Levie [00:19:58–00:20:43]).**
- **Jevons for labor: legal review at "$2,000 per hour or $1,000 per hour" vs an agent at "$5 or $10 an hour" → demand could be "50x" (Levie [00:22:40–00:22:58]).** "To hire a person costs $100,000, $300,000 … To bring in an agent might cost $1,000" → used in functions that were never staffed [00:23:45].
- **Model progress now amortises into enterprise, not consumer (Levie relaying Dario Amodei's point "on a recent podcast") [00:27:06–00:29:05].** Undergrad → grad-student chemistry gains go unnoticed by consumers but matter to Pfizer; his mother "would be fine if GPT-5 [transcribed "GPD5"] was just … the last thing"; a law-firm contract still comes back with "like 70 mistakes".
- **Distribution does not change: "If you build it, they will not come" (Levie [00:30:30–00:31:32]).** New channels (an OpenAI app/agent store), but no reinvention of go-to-market; own a community.
