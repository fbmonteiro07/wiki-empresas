### Amazon AWS CEO: AI boom is just getting started
_00:52:51 · [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1C_MErgk2Z0) · relevance: high · found by: manual: transcript pasted by Felipe in chat 2026-11-10_

**Lands on:** AMZN, CRWV, SPCX, themes/hyperscaler-capex, themes/custom-asic-tpu, themes/tokenmaxxing

**What they said (TL;DR)**

- **Capacity stays short "for the next couple years".** Garman: *"right now the demand is far out stripping the supply. And so even spending as much capex as we are… I anticipate that we'll be short of capacity um for the next couple years"* [00:12:00]. Most large customers sign *"three, four, five, six year commitments"*, some *"8, nine, 10 year"*, and *"oftentimes those customers frankly will outspend their commitments"* [00:11:22]–[00:12:00].
- **2027 is deliberately NOT sold out to the labs.** *"I don't want to sell out 100% of my capacity to just the large labs or the large technology companies"* [00:38:26]; *"I could choose to sell out every single accelerator that I have that I know is landing in 2027 and just be done with it… we choose not to"*. A *"good chunk"* is committed long-term, some is held back for enterprises and *"a new startup that hasn't even formed yet"*, and *"a bunch"* is saved for Bedrock [00:39:29]. Allocation is a regular (not daily) meeting; customers get re-offered other dates or regions [00:38:26].
- **Capex is staged by lead time, which is how he defends the spend.** Land, power contracts and permits are bought years out (*"I might be buying land today that I plan to use in 2030"*); networking and generators closer in; *"we're not buying servers"* for 2030 yet. *"If we're wrong, I could probably resell land"* [00:12:00]–[00:13:35].
- **The neocloud risk is "localized", not systemic, in his view.** The risk sits with builders that are *"completely debt financed at a… relatively high rate"*, with *"high concentration of customers"*, mostly *"really large training clusters"* bought by *"a small number of customers"* [00:14:10]–[00:15:15]. The host had named CoreWeave [transcribed "Cororeweave"] and the neoclouds [00:14:10]. AWS's AI workloads are *"the majority… inference today"* [00:15:15]; training is *"the minority of our business"* [00:17:57]. If a model provider fails, inference *"will fall over to another"* [00:15:52].
- **Inference is "not even 1%" of its end state, with a Jevons claim attached.** *"I don't think it's even 1%"*; *"every time we see inference get you know 50% cheaper we see an order of magnitude more usage… and yet the revenue is higher every single year"* [00:18:38].
- **Data centres in space: "highly unlikely to be economically feasible or practical in the next decade or two"** [00:28:37]. AWS is *"landing in a data center today, one GPU every 14 seconds"* while the world does fewer than one launch a day [00:29:19]. Of space, ocean and desert, deserts are *"by far the most likely"* [00:30:32].
- **External Trainium: the rationale, not the date.** Customers want Trainium on-prem and in sovereign settings; *"only you know 85% of workloads are still on prem"* [00:37:36]. *"It's one that we will be investing in and… doing more and more over time"*. No timing here (the Q3 call said *"late in 2027"*).
- **Nova: "more of a reset", frontier ambition unchanged.** *"Just as big of an investment"*; *"customers are going to want a first-party frontier model from AWS"*; *"stay tuned"* [00:40:10]–[00:40:47]. "Reset" is the host's word, which Garman accepts (*"It was more of a reset. That's right"*). It covered talent, data and infrastructure [00:40:47]. The host's framing of layoffs and Nova models being *"sunseted"* [00:40:10] was not confirmed or denied in detail.
