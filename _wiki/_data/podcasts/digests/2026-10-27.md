### OpenAI: When AI Agents Use Computers Faster Than You
_00:45:19 · [episode](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/openai-when-ai-agents-use-computers-faster-than-you-QLF23qpt) · transcript: podscripts.co (machine transcript, no speaker labels) · relevance: medium_

**TL;DR**

- **OpenAI computer use is "faster than the average human at using a computer" and "really close" to superhuman, meaning faster than an expert user (likely Ari Weinstein, speaker UNCONFIRMED [00:09:21]).** They call it a coming "sea change" [transcribed "C change"]. They also claim "the fastest, cheapest, and most reliable computer use product" [00:08:46]. No benchmark was cited.
- **GPT-6 Astra is faster because it "uses fewer thinking tokens to do the same task" (Ari Weinstein [00:02:38]).** He also called it more reliable, framed as the gap between "99% reliable and 97% reliable" (an illustration, not a measured figure). The other guest (James Sun, likely) called Astra OpenAI's "most aligned model", with better prompt-injection resistance per its system card [00:03:08–00:03:29].
- **Enterprise controls are the adoption gate (James Sun, likely [00:10:48–00:11:20], [00:18:02–00:19:19]).** The controls are a deterministic sandbox (which apps and websites the agent may use), human-in-the-loop dials (e.g. approve every upload or download, or every new website) and an auto-approve "Guardian" model that reviews "every single action".
- **Agent traffic breaks the ad model of the open web.** "Most places, an agent's ad impression does not actually count towards real revenue" (James Sun, likely [00:12:43]). OpenAI works through this with its partnerships team, and supports **WebMCP** in its in-app browser for sites that want to work with agents [00:13:01].
- **Use cases are mainly systems of record: "ERPs, CRMs… every single system of record" (James Sun [00:21:31]).** Examples are procurement (spreadsheet → procurement software → vendor outreach), month-end ledger reconciliation in finance software with no APIs, and sales prospecting into a CRM. Also mentioned: market research and creative work (architecture, hardware engineering, game design) [00:21:12–00:24:12].
- **Legacy software lives longer, but must become agent-friendly (Ari Weinstein [00:26:34–00:27:16]).** Software "written 10, 20, 30, 40 years ago" won't go away overnight, but computer use "may put more pressure on companies to make software work better with agents". James Sun: cheap software forces better software, but not "everything will have an MCP and an API" [00:29:43].
- **Adoption: the product is "growing like crazy" in the past couple of months, but "only some portion of our user base is using computer use" (Ari Weinstein [00:41:30–00:42:16]).** James Sun calls the growth "YC level… week over week" since browser use was folded into all products after Atlas ("rip Atlas"), whose agentic mode "wasn't actually like a big selling point" [00:42:16–00:42:43]. He says the next inflection is cloud computers for phone-first ChatGPT users [00:43:00].
