### Ep. 039 - [EMERGENCY] Our Numbers Are "Insane." Here's the Proof. (Datacenters) | Jeremie Ontiveros, Reyk Knuhtsen, Jordan Nanos
_00:34:46 · [episode](https://podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/jordan-nanos/episodes/Ep--039---EMERGENCY-Our-Numbers-Are-Insane--Heres-the-Proof--Datacenters--Jeremie-Ontiveros--Reyk-Knuhtsen--Jordan-Nanos-e3qpvpg) · transcript: YouTube uploaded captions · relevance: high_

**TL;DR**

- **SemiAnalysis is RAISING its 2027 datacenter build forecast. It does not accept the cut.** The previous number was **43 GW in 2027**. The article's framing is now **"fifty gigawatts in a single year"** (Jordan Nanos, [00:01:11]). The pushback consensus sits **near 25 GW** (show notes). **The basis is never stated** (IT load or facility; global scope implied).
- **The trigger was Brad Gerstner (Altimeter).** He showed SemiAnalysis's chart on All-In and in BG2's Jensen interview and called 43 GW too aggressive. Jensen "acknowledged it and moved on" (Nanos, [00:28:31]). The team calls the result "cut our forecast in half" (speaker UNCONFIRMED, [00:04:33]).
- **Moratoria are mostly noise in their count.** They mapped **400+ moratoriums** against every datacenter in the model and found **~10% of sites affected** (Reyk Knuhtsen, [00:06:55]).
- **The model already builds in delays.** It adds **1-3 quarters of delay by default**. A timeline under 12 months is treated as a red flag. They argue that cutting the number again would double-count the risk (Jeremie Ontiveros [00:12:05]; Knuhtsen [00:09:20]).
- **Oracle New Mexico gets a deliberately slow ramp.** More than **1 GW of IT capacity** is under construction there, but only a small share of it counts in 2027. They say Oracle is **"calling force majeure"** on power (Ontiveros, [00:13:32]).
- **Leading indicators point up.** Construction starts are rising every quarter. Pre-leasing (binding contracts) totals **"over one trillion" dollars** of datacenter leases (Nanos, [00:18:35]). Modular builds take **~9 months at DayOne** and **~6 months at "Bright Ray"** in Southeast Asia. AWS Project Houdini targets **~6 GW/yr** (Knuhtsen, [00:21:02]-[00:21:48]).
- **The 2028 call is the new one.** Today GPUs are easier to get than datacenter megawatts, i.e. chips are in oversupply. The **main risk in 2028 is the reverse: DC oversupply relative to chips**, which is bullish for fab and equipment capex. Ontiveros says this is a risk, **"not formally"** a call ([00:26:38]-[00:27:18], [00:30:57]).

**What was different**

- 🔭 **DIVERGES from the wiki's canonical facility-GW anchor.** The ledger `ai-dc-power-gw` carries **~20 GW built in 2027**, from **~40-45 GW proposed** after a 50% haircut (Jefferies satellite tracking, 2026-06-22). SemiAnalysis is at **43 → ~50 GW**, more than 2x that. It argues explicitly *against* haircutting under-construction sites. ⚠️ **Basis: SemiAnalysis never says IT-load or facility in this episode.** Carry it as an UNLABELLED basis and never net it against the Jefferies facility figure, the JPM North-America installed series, or the IT-load marks.
- 🔭 **DIVERGES from UBS's powered-shell bottleneck.** UBS (Arcuri, call 2026-09-30) has **~50 GW of compute-shipment GW in 2027** running into a **~20 GW powered-shell gap**, so shell is the constraint. SemiAnalysis agrees shell is the constraint *today*. But it expects the shell to arrive (~50 GW in 2027) and flags **2028 DC OVERSUPPLY vs chips** as the risk. That is the opposite sign to UBS's 2028 gap (20-25 GW), and to Jefferies' 2H27-28 behind-the-meter feasibility gap (~56 GW feasible vs ~90 GW needed).
- 🆕 **NOT ON THE WIKI: the "consensus ~25 GW" counter and the Gerstner-vs-SemiAnalysis fight.** BofA's Vivek Arya already referenced "the SemiAnalysis report … 50 gigawatts" next to **TSMC's 30-40 GW** on his 2026-10-19 SEMICON/OCP call (`_equity_calls/Semis/2026-10-19_BofA_...`). So the 50 GW figure is already circulating on the sell side.
- 🆕 **ORCL New Mexico "force majeure".** The ORCL page has management ring-fencing FY27 ("neither of these sites will have any impact into … FY27 guidance"), construction "definitely on track", and Barclays (2026-09-09) flagging New Mexico as the FY28+ risk. "Force majeure" does not appear on the page. It is SemiAnalysis's claim here and must be verified against a primary (press or filing) before it reaches the page.
- ✅ **CONFIRMS the modular / Project Houdini thesis already on AMZN** (SemiAnalysis "LEGO Datacenters", 2026-07-29). New in this episode: the **6 GW/yr Houdini goal**, which Knuhtsen hedges as "I think … was the stated goal … maybe six months ago", plus the DayOne ~9-month and "Bright Ray" ~6-month build times.
- 🆕 **"Over one trillion" of signed DC leasing.** This links to the SemiAnalysis Capital Markets episode (Ep. 037, 2026-10-09, Dan Nishball). It is the host's figure and the basis is not given.
