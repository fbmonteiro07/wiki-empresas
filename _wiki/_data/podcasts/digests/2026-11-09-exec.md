### Zscaler (ZS) CEO on Why Wall Street is Undervaluing the Company
_00:28:00 · [episode](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1SkB8AbOrOM) · relevance: medium · found by: exec-scan: Jay Chaudhry (Zscaler)_

**Lands on:** themes/ai-cybersecurity, PANW, NET, CRWD, OKTA

**What they said (TL;DR)**

- **"$10 billion plus" stated as confidence, not as a scenario (Chaudhry [00:24:32]):** after the investor day and the guidance, *"we're very confident that with the platform we can get to $10 billion plus."* The host framed the plan as *"$3.8 billion in ARR to 10 billion by fiscal 2031"* [00:00:43]. The wiki's record of the investor day has >$8bn as the base case and $10bn as an AI scenario outside guidance.
- **Firewalls are the target (Chaudhry [00:05:20], [00:08:44]):** *"In the world of zero trust, there's … no room for firewalls"*; *"it's a matter of time when firewall-based security will go away or maybe they'll become like mainframes."* Legacy vendors *"try to confuse the market because they will get disrupted."*
- **The PHP week as proof of the hide-the-attack-surface pitch (Chaudhry [00:05:54]–[00:06:26]):** applications behind Zscaler *"are hidden"*; customers called to say their PHP applications *"did not have the same exposure"*. With frontier models *"any user … can do what the very sophisticated hacker did."* Anecdote only, no count.
- **Agents are monetised on traffic (Chaudhry [00:18:17]):** *"We monetize based on the amount of traffic that's flowing through us, by agents making requests to do work."* Zscaler does not build agent identity: it calls Microsoft Entra ID [transcribed "and TryID"] or Okta for identity, then enforces policy [00:17:19].
- **Data security rides on AI security (Chaudhry [00:19:45]):** *"in the recent quarters, 70% of the deals we closed for AI security also had data security as a part of the same purchase."* He adds that as a standalone business it *"would probably be the largest data security company"* [00:20:21]. The host put data-security ARR above *"500 million"*, *"up 30% year over year if I remember correctly"* [00:18:17] — host's figures, not confirmed by Chaudhry.
- **A direct shot at "platformization" (Chaudhry [00:14:09]–[00:15:22]):** *"the word platform has been hijacked"*; a large retailer's CTO told him a platform sold *"by a firewall company"* had *"eight separate products, eight policy engines underneath"* — the benefit was one invoice instead of eight. Vendor unnamed; the host had just attributed "platformization" to Palo Alto.
- **CrowdStrike is a partner, not a rival (Chaudhry [00:13:37], [00:23:46]):** *"CrowdStrike is a great company. They complement what we do"* — endpoint is the door lock, Zscaler the international airport. Customers want *"about half a dozen key platform providers"*, not one vendor for everything [00:22:19].
- **M&A stays tuck-in (Chaudhry [00:21:47]):** *"we don't buy big companies for revenues"*; three acquisitions in the past 12 months, bought to cut a product's delivery *"from nine or 12 months down to three months."* Large-customer cohort: customers spending over a million dollars growing *"over 20 some percent per year"* [00:26:22].
