### What It Takes to Win as a Founder | Ben Horowitz
_00:41:52 · [episode](https://a16z.simplecast.com/episodes/what-it-takes-to-win-as-a-founder-ben-horowitz-AjG_7MSY) · transcript: podscripts.co (machine transcript, no speaker labels) · relevance: medium_

**TL;DR**

- **AI is "the new computer", not a network, so there is no dot-com-style adoption gap (Horowitz [00:02:35-00:05:17]).** Netscape in 1996 had "90% browser share and… 55 million users", half on dial-up, so the internet "collapsed before it rebuilt". With AI the network already exists, so "almost instantly you can get to a giant number of users". Companies are growing "faster than any companies we've ever seen".
- **Pure LLMs have plateaued; RL is scaling (Horowitz [00:14:05-00:14:53]).** "The language models are plateaued quite a bit in terms of… just the pure LLM. And then the reinforcement learning side is scaling. So… code is getting way better, but the regular kind of… chatbot is not that different." Each future breakthrough is "an architectural change" that resets the application and infrastructure layers.
- **The application paradigm may fade the most (Horowitz [00:08:30-00:09:06]).** "You organize your data, and then you have agents that you ask to do stuff against the data", for business and personal apps alike. He also floats "fast fashion SaaS" (constant clones) and runtime-generated bespoke software.
- **The "GPT wrapper" critique is "a bit of a dumb idea" (Horowitz [00:09:28-00:11:26]).** It is the RDBMS-wrapper mistake again (SAP, Salesforce). Cursor runs "14 models now" on top of the foundation model, and its data from "super high-end programmers" is differentiation. Incumbency matters: ChatGPT vs Grok [transcribed "GROC"] is "kind of marginal", but switching needs "a 10 times better product".
- **a16z no longer invests in consumer internet, mobile apps or SaaS (Horowitz [00:35:31-00:36:13]).** "We don't invest in any consumer internet companies anymore. you know, like mobile apps or, you know, SaaS company." Whole categories of in-house expertise are "no longer relevant".
- **Crypto as the "money network for AI" (Horowitz [00:12:26]).** "AIs aren't allowed to apply for credit cards", so agent-to-agent payments may run on crypto, with network effects from value exchange.
- **AI video is a new medium (Horowitz [00:26:46-00:28:38]).** It will be "as different from film as film was from plays". It is a mistake to just replicate film/TV.
- **Founder psychology (Horowitz [00:29:22-00:32:04]).** Loudcloud "went public on $2 million in trailing revenue… going to $75" (as said; the unit is presumably $75 million) at 18 months old; the alternative was bankruptcy. CEO failure "always comes down to a crisis in confidence". Zuckerberg at 22 had "tremendous doubt".

**What was different**

- **"Pure LLM plateaued; RL scaling; chatbot not that different" (Horowitz [00:14:29]).** → tokenmaxxing carries FundaAI's "not hit a scaling wall, but does not yet have enough compute to reach one", and the lab/analyst view that scaling continues. 🔭 **DIVERGES (mildly) from the "no wall" framing on the pre-training leg**, while ✅ CONFIRMING the code/RL leg that drives agentic token demand. It is a VC's opinion, not measured, and he gives no benchmark.
- **a16z exiting consumer internet, mobile and SaaS investing (Horowitz [00:35:31]).** → tokenmaxxing's 10-23 block carries the UBS "SaaSpocalypse" framing and the "non-binary SaaS" view. 🆕 **NOT ON THE WIKI: a top-tier VC stating it has stopped funding SaaS entirely.** It is a signal for the SaaS-disruption debate (CRM, NOW, etc. through private-market capital allocation), but it is a firm's own strategy statement, not evidence of public-SaaS revenue erosion.
- **"Application paradigm fades; agents against your data" (Horowitz [00:08:49]).** → ✅ CONFIRMS the systems-of-record-vs-agents debate on ai-cybersecurity / PANW (Arora: system of record "decays into an unstructured database", 09-14) and Krishna's SaaS consolidation (10-21 All-In). Not new.
- **Cursor at "14 models" (Horowitz [00:10:13]).** → 🆕 single datapoint on a private company. Context for the "apps build their own models" leg of tokenmaxxing (small/specialist models in the 10-23 block).
- **Crypto as agent payment rails [00:12:26].** → agentic-commerce theme. ✅ a familiar a16z position (crypto fund), not new.
- Compared with the prior a16z episodes (10-22 Frontier fund with Andreessen/Horowitz/Dixon; 10-23 Jassy), this is a founder-craft talk with no infrastructure, capex or token numbers. Horowitz's Loudcloud bandwidth-collapse analogy from the 10-16 All-In episode does not recur.
