How to read it. Each project sits under its end user where one is disclosed or credibly reported. For example, Riot Rockdale's AI-lab lease is under Anthropic, and Nebius Vineland is under Microsoft. The landlord is named in the detail. "Miners & developers" holds capacity with no named tenant.

MW are headline figures in mixed units. Some are IT, some are power or utility load, and some are full-build or permit capacity. Power runs about 1.2–1.4× IT. Cell totals show where capacity is concentrated; they are not additive forecasts. "n/d" means not disclosed and adds nothing to the totals.

Rank is our post-election data center friendliness ranking (1 = friendliest), from the DC Regulation knowledge base. The 2028 GW figure is SemiAnalysis net additions (Nov 1).

Sources: company filings and releases, utility and PSC filings, local press, DCD, Epoch AI, Baxtel, Cleanview, and the knowledge base (FUNDA, Wells Fargo, SemiAnalysis). Wells Fargo and FUNDA material is internal only. Q3 prints for CoreWeave (Nov 10), Nebius (Nov 12), TeraWulf, IREN, Core Scientific and MARA may change several cells.
