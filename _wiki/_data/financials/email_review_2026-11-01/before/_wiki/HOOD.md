# HOOD — Robinhood Markets, Inc.

_Wiki · created 2026-10-31 · research cutoff 2026-10-31 · [Financials](themes/financials.md) · [Company index](00_INDEX.md). Source archive: [HOOD](_dashboards/financials-sources.html#HOOD)._

## Snapshot

Robinhood is expanding from a retail trading application into a broader financial platform spanning brokerage, crypto, retirement, banking, cards, advisory and market infrastructure. The central thesis is greater wallet share and product adoption within the customer base. The challenge is whether this creates durable earnings after pricing concessions, credit losses, acquisition costs and regulatory constraints, rather than simply more activity during favorable markets. [Company Q3 release/call, 2026-10-27](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm); Capstone synthesis, 2026-10-31.

_Source: [company Q3 release, 2026-10-27](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm). Options and event contracts grew while crypto revenue fell 55%; net interest revenue fell 9% despite higher interest-earning assets._

**Adjusted EPS is a new definition this quarter.** The company introduced adjusted net income and EPS to exclude specified non-operating items. GAAP net income included $67m, or $0.07 per share, of unrealized losses primarily related to Robinhood Ventures Fund I. Management said normal-course stock compensation remains in adjusted EPS. Do not compare the new adjusted number with historical GAAP EPS without the reconciliation. [Q3 release](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm); Shiv Verma, [Q3 call, 2026-10-27](../HOOD/transcripts/HOOD_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md).

**Costs and deposits require careful boundaries.** The expense outlook excludes credit-loss provisions and other specified items, so it is not an all-in GAAP cost ceiling. October month-to-date net deposits exceeded $13bn at the announcement, including $6bn from the Dell family contribution to Trump Accounts. That discrete contribution should remain visible when judging ordinary customer acquisition and deposit momentum. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-27](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm).

**New products are scaling, but their economics differ.** The company reported 14 business lines at $100m or more in annualized revenue, including Rothera. Event-contract volume, options and margin balances reached records, while crypto activity was weaker. These product milestones do not by themselves establish stable revenue or returns on the associated investment. [Company release/call, 2026-10-27](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm).

## Debate / thesis

**Bull case — customers consolidate more of their financial lives on the platform.** A broader offering can increase assets, engagement and monetization per customer; Gold and retirement products may deepen relationships, while exchange infrastructure can retain more economics. The case is stronger if deposits and product adoption persist across trading cycles. [Morgan Stanley / Michael Cyprys and Barron Thomas, 2026-09-01 report](../relatórios%20bons/ROBINHOOD_20260901_0411.html); company Q3 release, 2026-10-27; Capstone interpretation, 2026-10-31.

**Bear case — a broader product set adds exposures as well as revenue.** Lower take rates, product incentives, credit-card losses and regulatory costs can offset volume growth. Prediction markets, perpetual futures and tokenized products bring distinct rules and operational risks. A large platform-asset balance can fall with markets, and annualized new-product revenue can be volatile. [Company Q3 release](../HOOD/earnings/2026-10-27_q32026robinhoodexhibit991.htm); [Q3 10-Q, filed 2026-10-28](../HOOD/HOOD_10-Q_2026-10-28_0001783879-26-000167.html); Capstone interpretation, 2026-10-31.

**What would change the view:** continued organic net deposits beyond special contributions, higher multi-product adoption with disciplined acquisition costs, and improving earnings after credit losses and dilution; or persistent monetization erosion while product and regulatory expenses rise.
