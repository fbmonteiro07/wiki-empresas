Reconciliation to the national forecast

Five individually quantified states: 18.0 + 5.1 + 2.6 + 2.4 + 2.1 = 30.2 GW.
Other eight states in the report: published 13-state subtotal 43.5 − 30.2 = 13.3 GW.
Remaining US, including Virginia and Mississippi: published national total 62.2 − 43.5 = 18.7 GW.
Check: 30.2 + 13.3 + 18.7 = 62.2 GW.

The two residuals are Capstone arithmetic from one forecast vintage, not published individual-state estimates. Missing values are not zeros. The unstarted ranges are subsets of total additions and cannot fill the missing totals. Planned capacity exposed to policy changes is not an estimate of capacity delayed.

Georgia's 3.2 GW Camellia power commitment spans 2028–2032 and is not used as a 2028 state total. The 2027 top-20-state aggregate of 33.8 GW is a different year and population. The September regional installed-capacity stock is also excluded from this bridge. No forecast haircut or current political-outcome assessment is made.
