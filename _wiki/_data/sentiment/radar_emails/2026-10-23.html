Desk read

Funding fear turns the AI unwind into a de-gross: SOX -6.7% on META's raise and LRCX's WFE; desks see rotation into low-beta NVDA/AVGO and software, not capitulation.

Thursday was the worst AI day since DeepSeek: SOX -6.7%, NDX about -2.6% and GS's HF Popular Pair basket -4.2%, its worst move since 2011 (GS Bartlett, 23 Oct). Global fundamental L/S, multi-strat and systematic funds all fell more than 1% on the same day for the first time since Covid. The desks blame META's equity-raise overhang and LRCX's sub-whisper 2027 WFE, and say even good prints (WDC, BE, FIX) get faded (Jefferies Favuzza, Barclays Rand, UBS Ruple, 22-23 Oct). Positioning has reset fast: UBS PB has US long exposure down from the 90th to the 15th percentile and shorts up from the 15th to the 80th since end-September. BofA's Villani says HFs are the only persistent sellers while long-onlys turned net buyers and retail bought the dip. Barclays' Rand sees money moving into lower-beta NVDA/AVGO and out of AMD/MRVL/LITE, while Wells Fargo's Kwon has software sentiment at +1.2 SD, near a contrarian sell. CNN Fear & Greed closed 22 Oct at 19.8 (extreme fear), from 31.4.
