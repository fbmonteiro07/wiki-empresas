Friday's mail is pre-print positioning rather than new flow: UBS (Dwyer, 16 Oct) scores AMZN 9/10 and META 8/10 with hedge funds long both, and GOOGL 4/10 with hedge funds short, while the META 2027 capex bar is scattered from the $201bn consensus (Vital Knowledge, 17 Oct) through $230-240bn (DB investor conversations, 16 Oct) and $250bn (UBS) to $250-300bn (Bernstein, 16 Oct), so the print reaction depends on which base the holder is using.
