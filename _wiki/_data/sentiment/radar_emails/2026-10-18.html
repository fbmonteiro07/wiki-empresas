Themes and quality-source notes

De-grossing is the story; nobody changed a number. Jefferies' Favuzza (18 Oct) had the largest tech sell skew in over five years (about $5.5B net for sale by Friday morning, more by the close) with a 57/43 HF/LO split, the worst week ever for AI winners vs losers and tech momentum down ~20%; UBS's Ruple (17 Oct) says net exposure fell fast while gross stays high, so more de-grossing is possible; JPM's Schlegel (via See, 18 Oct) has multi-strat de-grossing near a two-year worst, US positioning at the 40th percentile and a 4Q rally as base case, likening the tape to October 2023. 22V's Visser (18 Oct) calls it an LTCM-style leverage unwind rather than an AI-bubble pop, and the Vanda note relayed by Jefferies (18 Oct) counts the seventh >9% SOX drawdown since June with tech positioning 'middle of the road'. The dissent is Chris Wood (via Jefferies, 17 Oct): the easy money in semis is made, diversify. No source changed a 2026-27 estimate; GS (Chang, 18 Oct) raised its server TAM 31%/45% and Thill (via Jefferies, 18 Oct) models hyperscaler+ORCL+META capex of ~$818B CY26 and ~$1.17T CY27.

CXMT scare vs Hynix HBM execution: two different bear legs. The research desks spent the weekend on China: Jefferies Korea (Jeff Kim, 18 Oct) puts the SEC-CXMT DRAM gap at 5-7 years and UBS's Gaudois (17 Oct) says the 4F2 VCAT ramp fears are overdone, both calling the Korea selloff a buying opportunity at ~3x P/E for Samsung. The positioning evidence points the other way for now (Korea's largest foreign outflows since June per Vanda via Jefferies; Hynix -27% and Samsung -17.7% on the week per UBS), and the live bear leg for Hynix specifically is not China but its own HBM: Jefferies tees up 'potential Rubin delays on HBM cuts at Hynix' for 22 Oct, after Friday's DB note on 12-high MR-MUF. UBS's tracker has MU on the long, short and debated lists simultaneously, which is the honest summary of memory positioning. Jefferies' memory expert call (Harold Lee, 22 Oct 9am ET) is the next venue.

Anthropic's $1.5T and the frontier-lab growth scare. Jefferies' Saturday note (17 Oct) cites the Reuters report that some Anthropic IPO investors are weighing ~$1.5T versus the ~$2T previously expected as a fuel for Friday's selloff, and UBS (17 Oct) opens its recap with 'the lukewarm reception to frontier model revenue growth projections'. Tech Fund (Substack, 18 Oct) attributes the scare to Anthropic's sequential growth slowing below 7% month-on-month for two months while OpenAI's accelerated to 16-19%, argues August seasonality and OpenAI's Astra launch explain it, and counts $124B of combined lab ARR ($142B grossed for cloud resale) heading to ~$187B by year end; those are the author's figures and the wiki's Anthropic ARR is conflict-flagged with bases unknown pre-S1. The same author's claim that AI added ~$65B of ARR to Meta since 2023 is an estimate, not a disclosure. Treat this as a narrative with no desk positioning voice yet; the hyperscaler prints (META 21 Oct, GOOG 27 Oct, MSFT 28 Oct, AMZN 29 Oct) are where it meets numbers.

Optics and power keep their post-OCP bid while compute is sold. Blayne Curtis's OCP takes (via Jefferies Favuzza, 18 Oct) repeat Friday's verdict: 1.6T demand strong, NPO gaining traction ahead of meaningful CPO, LITE and MTSI most levered, CRDO's ALC/optical backplane/OmniConnect under-appreciated and pluggables more durable (USFP, XPO). UBS's tracker (17 Oct) has LITE, COHR, ANET, CIEN, HPE, DELL, CLS, FLEX and JBL long-biased, so this is the crowded corner of hardware, while Bloomberg X shows CIEN tone falling and ALAB attention dropping. On power, UBS's Fisher (17 Oct) returns from Data Center World Expo Singapore with demand accelerating into 2027, supply the constraint and the industry converging on 800VDC for 2027-2029 deployment (read positive for PWR, CAT, CMI); a facility/architecture claim, no GW base attached. GS's server model (Chang, 18 Oct) implies ~60% ASIC unit penetration in 2028, a unit share that should not be compared with the wiki's dollar-based custom-ASIC share framing.
