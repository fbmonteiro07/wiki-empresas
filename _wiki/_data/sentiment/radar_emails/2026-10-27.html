Semis rose 2.9% on 26 Oct, and ETFs took a record $12.7bn (JPM Delta-One). Desks disagree on how real the buying is. Jefferies (Favuzza, Beavington) sees a relief bounce with HFs and LOs not rushing back. UBS's desk was 'largely better for sale in Tech', with buyers in defensives. Vanda (forwarded by Rodrigo Nascimento) says institutions are rotating out of semis rather than de-risking broadly. 22V (DeBusschere) says the Anthropic S-1 makes it 'hard to short the buildout'.

GW debate: what the market discounts vs what satellites see (different bases). Arcuri (UBS semis call, 26 Oct) back-solves the SMH price to ~$25 of 2028 EPS vs a ~$42 Street (-40%). He maps that to ~50-52 GW of 2028 compute shipments vs his ~70 GW, i.e. ~20 GW discounted, on a compute-shipment basis. Jefferies' JDS (27 Oct) raises facility deployments to ~27 GW in 2027 (+23%). For 2028 he sees >30 GW, up to ~40 GW, vs semi supply implying +90 GW, and more than half of 2028's sites are not yet land-cleared. These are different bases (compute shipments vs facility deployments) and should not be compared one-for-one.

DAMNANG (independent author, not a broker; 27 Oct, preview only) relays a Jukan X post that NVIDIA lowered the official Rubin HBM4 pin speed from 11.7 to 9.3 Gbps.
