The AI earnings night turned into a de-gross: INTC's crowded long breaks, AMZN is sold but defended, memory falls on good prints, and META/MSFT become the megacap hide.

Desks moved from yesterday's single-name split to a broad tech de-gross. Jefferies (Favuzza, 29 Oct) says it has been net for sale in tech for five weeks, BofA (Villani, 29 Oct) sees long-only funds' biggest monthly selling of 2026, and UBS PB (Ruple, 30 Oct) says de-grossing hit both sides of the book while short exposure stays above the 80th percentile, which leaves room for a squeeze. Memory (SNDK, Kioxia, Hynix) and the MRVL-LITE optics debate add to semis pressure, with META, MSFT and ORCL's funding relief named as the places to hide.
