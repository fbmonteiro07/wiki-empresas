Themes and quality-source notes

Covering, not buying: the short book tells the story. UBS's equity-finance data (Ruple, 23 Oct) shows US Tech net covered (-4.9% WoW) with memory/storage/optics leading (MU, NBIS, SNDK, SKHY, LITE, WDC, STX), while new shorts went into software and IT services (NOW, CRM, ADSK, ACN) and TSM/INTC. MS PB adds HFs unwound longs at -2.6z while also covering (-0.6z). The bounce in AI hardware names is therefore covering-led; BofA Short Analytics (via Villani) sees no obvious re-risking yet.

NOW is a live disagreement into its print. BofA's Liani raises his PO to $180 and JPM's Schilsky (23 Oct, prior ledger) has sentiment near neutral, but UBS's book shows NOW among the top-10 notional shorts WoW (+37%). Software is the rotation destination, and shorts are building there at the same time.

SemiAnalysis: Trainium3 moves into training. SemiAnalysis (Chen/Patel, 23 Oct) hands-on: native PyTorch eager/torch.compile on Trainium3, a profiler it rates above Nvidia's and AMD's, and Anthropic running production inference and RL rollouts on Trn2/Trn3. It is still far from CUDA's maturity, so this is medium-term AMZN custom-silicon support and a slow NVDA-moat erosion, not a near-term share call. Independent research, no ratings.

SPCX's capex: bullish for semicap, bearish for neocloud financing. The same SPCX datapoint (13 GW by YE27, 4Q capex $35-40B) is read as a semicap tailwind (ASML/KLAC, BofA Villani; NVDA/CRDO, Jefferies) and as a neocloud headwind through capacity and financing competition (Villani's inbounds; MS on CRWV). Independent voices lean toward a clearing event still ahead: 22V's Dauvin Peterson (23 Oct) sees a desire for capitulation and says Anthropic disclosure matters, and Irrational Analysis (24 Oct) puts the market 70-80% of the way to peak fear.
