Desk read

Day two after the blue sweep turned the AI rotation into capitulation: SOX -12/13%, memory and AI-power liquidated, retail and systematics dumping, and only APP, NET and the hyperscalers' existing capacity drawing buyers.

5 Nov was the second-worst SOX day on record; desks describe forced de-grossing rather than a fundamental verdict. Jefferies' ADR desk (6 Nov) counts nine straight sessions of TMT net selling by both LOs and HFs, and Vanda (6 Nov, forwarded internally) saw systematics cut $74bn and retail sell single stocks at the fastest pace since 2020. The 4 Nov hyperscaler bid (UBS PB: 100% long buying) did not survive day two, though AMZN held up best; memory (MU, SNDK, Hynix) went from crowded long to the leading unwind. GS (Armbrust, 6 Nov) leans '1998 not 2000' and the 6 Nov pre-market bounce is led by the AI complex (LRCX buyback, MU, CRDO), but UBS (Cheong via Dwyer, 5 Nov) and Fubon (6 Nov) both say the low may not be in yet.

Themes and quality-source notes

Capitulation or more to come? Desks disagree on who sold and whether the low is in. Jefferies (Massey, 6 Nov) says LOs outsold HFs 55/45 on 5 Nov and TMT has seen nine straight days of LO and HF selling; TMTB (5 Nov) notes GS's desk instead saw LOs 'frozen out'. BofA's Villani (6 Nov) saw flows flip to net buying late in the day (retail's largest ETF buy day on record) while LOs and HFs kept selling semis and industrials. UBS (Cheong via Dwyer, 5 Nov) expects $100bn+ of risk-control selling and a possible Monday washout; Vanda (6 Nov) says retail capitulations usually come near bottoms but a few more days of pain are possible. GS (Armbrust, 6 Nov) leans '1998 liquidity event, not 2000'. All of these are flow and desk opinions, not forecasts.

2027 builds look intact; 2028 GW is what is being repriced. JPM's Meyers (5 Nov), citing SemiAnalysis, says 50+ GW being built in 2027 should be mostly on track but 62+ GW of 2028 capacity is in question; GS's Callahan (5 Nov) says the market is trying to price 2028+ and SOX multiples have compressed ~50% from June/July. UBS's Dwyer (5 Nov) hears both 'pull-forward / 2027 peak' and 'cycle extended' readings. The GW figures are as the sources state them, without a facility vs IT-load basis, so they are not reconciled to the wiki's canonical GW framing. META's President (via Jefferies) says new US sites will be harder.

Semis valuation floor: numbers up, multiples down. Barclays' Rand (6 Nov): CY28 semi EPS is up ~24% since the late-June peak while multiples are down 19.5 turns, to ~13x CY28, with few long-term investors stepping in yet. BofA sales relaying Arya (6 Nov): SOX -37% from peak vs a 32% average drawdown, 13.7x forward, at most ~12% further downside to a ~12x trough; a 20% CY27 capex cut would mean a 35-40%+ EPS headwind for AI semis. Both say EPS cuts, not only cheap multiples, may be needed. Buybacks (LRCX $25bn, MU from 9 Dec) are the near-term support.

SemiAnalysis: TPU is a credible inference alternative to CUDA. SemiAnalysis (5 Nov, independent author, not a broker) says Google's Ironwood TPU now stakes a claim on inference, its external-TPU call has been proven with Oracle, OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta as customers, and NVIDIA is responding under pressure. This is a narrative input for GOOG vs NVDA rather than positioning evidence; no desk in today's mail relayed it.
