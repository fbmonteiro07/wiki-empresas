Themes and quality-source notes

Rotation inside AI: hyperscalers bought, picks-and-shovels and neoclouds sold. UBS's TMT desk (Ruple, 7 Nov) and Bernstein's desk (Doran via Seidman, 7 Nov) describe the same flow from both sides: buying hyperscalers, selling neoclouds, semis and hardware, with short-sale tickets rising. GS's Pasquariello (7 Nov, desk commentary) frames the new fact set as better for the hyperscalers and worse for the rest of the AI infrastructure complex, with less upside convexity in picks-and-shovels. The read-across: a slower buildout is being treated as FCF-positive for the spenders and negative for the suppliers.

Washout or capitulation? Desks disagree on timing. Late-stage camp: Vanda (Liu, 7 Nov) says Thursday's retail selling was the largest in its dataset and such episodes nearly always mark a drawdown's final stage, with CTA selling near complete; UBS (7 Nov) has PB net AI exposure at summer lows. Not-yet camp: JPM's Schlegel (via See, 8 Nov) says positioning reset but the TPM flow trigger for capitulation has not fired; JPM's Silver (8 Nov) expects HFs to run low gross into year-end; Jefferies Europe (7 Nov) says it is too early to bottom-fish semis while Jefferies US says build a holiday shopping list. Weighting: these are desk and flow-strategy views, not rated research.

SemiAnalysis in Inner Mongolia: the China contrast to the Texas backlash. SemiAnalysis (Tan, Ontiveros, Laoban, Patel, 7 Nov; preview read) visited Envision's Galaxy campus in Ulanqab, planned for up to 800 MW of IT capacity, whose first 120 MW phase was delivered within a year at about a fifth of US cost, with a 5 MW hall running SST-native 800V DC. It contrasts China's accelerating build with Texas's anti-datacenter sentiment. 800V is a separate debate; these MW are IT capacity at one campus and should not be mixed with US facility-GW figures. No universe-company observation logged.

WFE: desk says too hot, research says beat/raise. Bernstein's spec sales (7 Nov) lists 'WFE expectations running too hot' among the negative themes of the cycle, while Jefferies' US team (via Beavington, 7 Nov) expects AMAT to beat/raise with revenue guided HSD+ vs consensus +5%, and peers point to 30%+ WFE growth next year. AMAT (Thu) and ONTO (Mon) are the tests.
