Themes and quality-source notes

Neocloud funding shock vs hyperscaler re-gross. The weekend's new catalyst was Musk's claim that debt markets have 'almost entirely shut' for neoclouds (GS Kamshad, UBS Ruple, Jefferies Favuzza, 26 Oct). Note Bloomberg reports the SpaceX $40bn debt deal is not yet concluded (Jefferies Beavington, 26 Oct). JPM's Tyler (26 Oct) says credit is becoming more discriminating on AI infra but sees no broader funding crisis. FundaAI (independent, 26 Oct) says GPU rental pricing, not lab ARR, is the real test. Meanwhile UBS PB (Dwyer, 26 Oct) saw HFs fully re-gross hyperscalers on Friday, funded by hardware and memory sales.

META equity raise: overhang or clearing event?. UBS (Dwyer, Ruple) and Bernstein (Seidman: supply as clearing events) read META's $95bn package as clearing an overhang that was expected in January. The same UBS specialist's weekend note said the Muse honeymoon is over, and Jefferies (Beavington, 26 Oct, written before the details) still called issuance a near-term overhang. The desks disagree, and this week's pricing settles it.

Is the AI-hardware de-gross done?. Bernstein crowding (26 Oct) still has 7/12 semis in the top decile, led by AVGO/AMD/NVDA (QCOM the outlier), and Jefferies/Vanda say the washout isn't complete. On the other side, JPM's Meyers (relaying Schlegel, 25 Oct) sees selling exhaustion, with AI core semis down to the 94th %-ile from the 99th, and 22V's Visser (25 Oct) calls the deleveraging done or in its final innings. Survey bars stay high in places (STX +5, AMAT/SNDK +3).

Software/cyber rotation meets valuation sensitivity. Money rotated from AI semis into software (IGV +6% last week, per Jefferies; TMTB independent sees tech generalists hunting in software). Jefferies' Favuzza (25 Oct) sees long-side cyber holders turning valuation-sensitive, while BofA's Liani (26 Oct) raises POs for CRWD/ZS on AI-driven cyber risk: research and desk lean different ways.
