Issuance of $10 Billion Aggregate Principal Amount of Notes On November 4, 2026, Oracle consummated the issuance and sale of $2,000,000,000 principal amount of its 7.000% Notes due 2029, $1,750,000,000 principal amount of its 7.450% Notes due 2031, $2,250,000,000 principal amount of its 7.850% Notes due 2033, $2,500,000,000 principal amount of its 8.000% Notes due 2037 and $1,500,000,000 principal amount of its 8.650% Notes due 2046 (collectively, the “Notes”), pursuant to an underwriting agreement dated November 2, 2026 by and between Oracle and BofA Securities, Inc., as representative of the several underwriters named therein (such offering, the “Notes Offering” and, together with the Common Stock Offering, the “Offerings”). The material terms and conditions of the Notes are set forth in the Officers’ Certificate filed herewith as Exhibit 4.1 and incorporated by reference herein, in the Indenture filed as Exhibit 10.34 to the Current Report on Form 8-K filed by Oracle Systems Corporation on January 20, 2006, and in the First Supplemental Indenture filed as Exhibit 4.3 to the Registration Statement on Form S-3 filed by Oracle Corporation on May 10, 2007.
