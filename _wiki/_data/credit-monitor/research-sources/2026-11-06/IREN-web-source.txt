L15: NEW YORK, Nov. 05, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- IREN (NASDAQ: 【1†IREN†www.globenewswire.com】) today reported financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on its AI Cloud business and growth plans.
L16: “This quarter Horizon 1 went into service for Microsoft with NVIDIA Exemplar status, AI Cloud revenue nearly tripled to $200 million, and operating ARR doubled to a billion,” said Daniel Roberts, Co-Founder and Co-CEO of IREN. “The more than $4 billion of contracted ARR is funded and now targeted for January, after a customer-requested design change and supplier timing moved it from year-end; it may extend to the end of February.
L17: Since quarter-end we have signed a five-year contract at about $22 million per megawatt with 90% of the GPU capex prepaid by the customer, and a short-term contract at $40 million per megawatt. We own the land, the power and the data centers under all of it, which gives us more ways to fund growth and lets us set its pace.”

L76: Assumptions and Notes
L77:   1. Total AI Cloud Revenue comprises AI Cloud Services Revenue of $135m and AI Cloud Lease Revenue of $65m (prior quarter: $71m and nil, respectively), per our unaudited condensed consolidated statements of operations.
L78:   2. Adjusted EBITDA and Total segment gross profit are non-GAAP financial measures. AI Cloud segment gross margin is AI Cloud segment gross profit (Total AI Cloud revenue less AI Cloud cost of revenue, excluding depreciation and amortization and before site-level costs) divided by Total AI Cloud revenue. Refer to the reconciliations at the end of this press release to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measures.
L79:   3. Revenue backlog includes remaining performance obligations plus remaining lease arrangements plus other amounts we estimate may be recognized as revenue in future periods under committed customer contracts, in each case, subject to the satisfaction of delivery and availability of service requirements. Investment-grade share is based on the credit ratings of counterparties or their guarantors as of September 30, 2026.
L80:   4. Annualized-run-rate revenue (“ARR”) is calculated as GPU/hour pricing for commissioned GPUs under contract as of the relevant date multiplied by 8,760 hours per year and includes annualized revenue for storage and ancillaries.
L81: More than $4 billion of ARR is targeted to be operational by January 31, 2027, and may extend to the end of February 2027, based on internal company assumptions regarding utilization and pricing, with revenue expected to ramp upon, and being subject to, commissioning, testing and customer acceptance in the months following each data center’s delivery. ARR is not derived from or an adjustment to GAAP revenue, and excludes GAAP recognition and measurement effects. ARR is an operating metric, not a U.S.
L82: GAAP measure, and differs from, and is not a substitute for, GAAP revenue; recognized revenue may be materially lower.
L83:   5. Cash and committed GPU financing and prepayments comprises cash and cash equivalents, restricted cash, undrawn committed GPU financing facilities and contracted customer prepayments not yet received, as of September 30, 2026.
L84: “Fully funded” means these sources cover the remaining capex from September 30, 2026, including data centers and GPUs, for the 0.3GW (IT) program that supports the more than $4 billion ARR target, subject to satisfaction of the conditions to funding under committed financing facilities, including conditions to the release of escrowed proceeds and applicable availability periods.
L85:   6. Estimated average annual revenue per MW (IT) for 3- and 5-year contracts signed during Q1 FY27 and Q4 FY26 based on internal assumptions on power consumption for each contracted deployment, weighted by ARR.
L86:   7. Reflects contracts signed after Q1 FY27. Revenue per IT-MW is estimated annual revenue per MW (IT). Customer prepayment coverage reflects customer prepayments as a proportion of associated estimated GPU capex for the five-year contract. Customer prepayments represent amounts contractually payable by customers in advance of service delivery, expressed as a percentage of the estimated capital expenditure attributable to the associated deployments.
L87: Prepayment terms vary by contract and there can be no assurance that future contracts will include prepayments or similar terms. GPU capex excludes provincial sales tax and goods and services tax. H100 pricing increase is relative to pricing under the prior H100 contract at Prince George.
L88:   8. Includes land secured in freehold and under option, as of October 31, 2026.
L89:   9. >35GW development pipeline represents the potential gross power capacity, as of November 5, 2026, of projects at various stages of development prior to execution of grid connection agreements, letters of agreement or equivalents. These projects are speculative, and we do not currently expect a significant portion of them to result in secured power. There can be no assurance that any project will proceed or that the indicated capacity will ultimately be secured or developed.
L90:   10. Data center assets comprise buildings, construction in progress and land, net of depreciation, as of September 30, 2026.
L91:   11. Remaining capex is from September 30, 2026 and includes data centers and GPUs.
L92:   12. Future capacity targets include further capacity across the platform’s secured power and development pipeline, including the 0.8GW of data center capacity by the end of calendar 2027, which may require additional capex in FY27.
