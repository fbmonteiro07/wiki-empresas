Pre-3Q Earnings Estimate Revisions; Raising Google Cloud on TPUs & AWS on AI/Pricing; Slight Increase to Meta 2027 Expenses; Reiterate Hyperscaler Overweights
 
    
 
   
 
   
 
    
 
     
 
    
 
    
 
Read full disclosures
 
    
 
   
 
   
 
    
 
     
 
    
 
    
 
     
 
    
 
    
 
     
 
    
 
    
 
   
 
   
Ahead of 3Q earnings & in conjunction with our 3Q preview, we’re updating estimates across hyperscalers (GOOGL, AMZN, & META), rideshare (UBER & LYFT), and small-cap FVRR. Please see an overview of the key changes and summary Old/New tables for each company below, along w/ our updated investment thesis, valuation, and risks for each company herein. 

   
 
    
 
GOOG/L: 
The biggest change is our Google Cloud growth rate, which increases from +88% Y/Y to +100% in 3Q, from +90% to +105% in 4Q, from +70% to +107% in 2027, and from +55% to +63% in 2028. 
The primary driver of the increase is greater TPU sale inclusion, & we could still be conservative.
 We also include the $5B debt raised by Waymo in early October, assume $80B of new debt raises in 2027, introduce quarterly estimates for 2027, and update for FX. 
These changes resulted in a 3% increase in our 2027E GAAP EPS to $14.05 and a 4% increase in our 2028E GAAP EPS to $17.29. 
We reiterate our Overweight rating & raise our Dec ‘27 PT from $420 to $425 based on ~25x our 2028E GAAP EPS.
 
 
    
 
AMZN: 
We are increasing our AWS growth estimates and now model acceleration in 2027 to +40%, which could still be conservative. 
Key drivers to upside for AWS growth include scaling of its OpenAI partnership and deployment of an incremental 2M GPUs across 2027-2028, as well as pricing power. 
We note that AWS recently announced a 15% increase to GPU reserve pricing starting Oct 7
th
, its fourth consecutive quarterly price increase for reserved AI compute capacity across GPU classes, with H100/H200 & B200/B300 prices up 40-75% YTD.
 In addition, demand is broad-based and AWS’ compute allocation is diversified, with the largest customer accounting for only a single-digit percentage. We’re also rolling out 2027 quarters, updating for the recent £4.25B debt raise, as well as FX. 
Overall, we are raising our 2027/2028 estimates across Sales by 1-2%, OI by 3-5%, and GAAP EPS by 2-3%.
 We reiterate our Overweight rating & our $365 PT is based on 27.5x ‘28E GAAP EPS of $13.34.
 
 
    
 
META: 
We are updating our numbers to reflect the $10B legal expense accrual in 3Q26 related to Meta’s settlement with 52 state attorneys general, which was not contemplated in the $165B-$169B full year expense guidance provided at 2Q earnings. We are also updating FX & 
raising our 2027E total GAAP expenses to $210B (+18% Y/Y, +25% Y/Y ex-$10B legal expense)
, partially offset by slightly higher revenue.
 Overall, our 2026 & 2027 GAAP EPS come down -10% & -2%, respectively. 
We reiterate our Overweight rating & our $920 PT is based on ~26x ‘28E GAAP EPS of $35.45.
 
 
    
 
UBER: 
Uber recently 
announced
 a restructuring of ~10% of its headcount, or ~3,300 people, with a focus on removing layers, simplifying team structures, refining global location strategy, and focusing people and investments against the biggest opportunities. As part of the restructuring, Uber reduced the number of micro-teams with one or two members by 50%, and also reduced staff more than seven layers down from the CEO by 20%. We calculate the restructuring-related annual cost savings at ~$600M+, with severance costs of ~$90M to be realized in 3Q. 
We are currently not modeling any cost savings from the restructuring as Uber looks to reinvest the savings in the business through lowering prices, improving selection, as well as continuing to invest in the growth areas. 
We are also updating #s for the recent 
€4.5B debt raise & FX.
 Overall, our 3Q GAAP OI & FCF come down by -4%/-3%, while Adj. OI is mostly unchanged.
 Due to a research restriction, we currently do not have a rating or PT for Uber. 
 
    
 
LYFT: 
Lyft recently disclosed in an 
8-K
 that it had reached a settlement with the State of California, the Labor Commissioner for CA, and two private plaintiffs to resolve claims of driver misclassification in California between Apr 5, 2016 and December 15, 2020. The total settlement amount is $272.5 million, and Lyft can elect to make settlement payments over four years, with 5% simple interest accruing after year one & capped at $12.4M. 
Notably, Lyft reaffirmed its 3Q26 guide with Gross Bookings / Adj. EBITDA of $5.50B – $5.67B / Adj. EBITDA of $183M – $203M. 
We note that Lyft had accrued $210M for the matter in 4Q25, w/the charge split between contra revenue and G&A. 
We expect the incremental $62.5M above the prior accrual to hit 4Q G&A, which will impact 4Q GAAP OI/NI but won’t impact Adj. EBITDA. 
We currently assume Lyft will make the $272.5M payment over four years, which will be reflected in cash outflow. Also, Lyft’s CEO David Risher recently called out at an investor conference that 2H Rides growth will be stable vs. 1H, w/the lapping of Freenow (July 31
st)
 offset by acceleration in the US. 
We’re currently modeling 2H Rides growth of 10.4% vs. 10.2% in 1H.
 We’re Neutral on Lyft shares & our $19 PT is based on ~4.5x 2028E FCF of $1.0B.
 
 
    
 
FVRR: 
We are lowering our Fiverr estimates and now expect 2027 revenue growth in-line with 2026 of -15%, and 2027 EBITDA margins to be more in-line with our 4Q26 expectations of 10.5%. On 2Q earnings, Fiverr highlighted a noticeable deceleration in marketplace traffic and demand which had carried into 3Q. We think customer traffic has further deteriorated in 3Q. Based on SimilarWeb data, US visits to Fiverr.com decelerated from -10% Y/Y in 2Q to -22% Y/Y in 3QTD (-19% in July & -24% in August), while unique visitor traffic to Fiverr.com in the US also decelerated from -17% Y/Y in 2Q to -21% Y/Y in 3QTD (-21% in July & -22% in August). Recent changes in Google Search, including AI/LLM search summaries, are increasingly pushing users away from paid or organic search links, 
and while Fiverr is investing in GEO, current LLM CTRs remain below those of search, creating a significant headwind for top of the funnel traffic. Fiverr remains early in its transition from a transaction-oriented marketplace focused on low-value projects to a trusted work platform for higher-end & higher-value work. Mgmt expects the transition will take at least six quarters given high-value work currently represents only 15% of GMV, and some portion of the remaining 85% of the business will be disrupted by AI, while some will be more defensible. 
We are lowering our 2027/2028 revenue by 6%, while our 2027/2028 Adj. EBITDA is coming down 21% & 14%. 
Our Dec-27 PT comes down to $10 (from $11) based on 8.0x ‘28E non-GAAP EPS of $1.23, and we remain Neutral rated.

Alphabet Inc. (Overweight; Price Target: $425.00)

We believe Alphabet’s fundamentals are solid and the company will remain both a driver of and primary beneficiary of an increasingly digital economy & advances in Generative AI. Google remains focused on innovation, and we continue to believe there is a healthy runway across Search and YouTube ads as AI drives higher ROI & TV dollars shift online. Non-Ad businesses, incl. Cloud & Subscriptions, have significant headroom, and companies within Other Bets, notably Waymo, provide option value. We remain confident in the company’s ability to innovate around Generative AI, control costs, & generate solid top-line growth.
 
   
Valuation

   
Our December 2027 price target of $425, up from prior $420, is based on ~25x our ‘28E GAAP EPS of $17.29, which ex-cash equates to ~22x our ‘28E GAAP EPS ex-Other Bets of $18.37. We believe Alphabet shares should trade at a premium to the S&P 500 as Alphabet is one of the few companies in the S&P 500 with DD % revenue and EPS growth off of a very large base and a strong 34%+ GAAP Operating Income margin.

Downside risks include: 1) 
Competition ramping across AI, Ads, Cloud, and for engineering/tech talent; 
2) 
potential for a return to heavy investment spending and margin compression; 
3) 
regulatory scrutiny resulting in meaningful change to operations or limiting innovation; and

growth slowing more than anticipated with the next major revenue drivers, incl. Cloud, Hardware, and Waymo, not materializing as expected. 
 
   
 
   
Alphabet (Overweight; Price Target: $425.00)

Cloud & YouTube subscription services, have significant headroom & momentum, and companies within Other Bets, incl. Waymo, provide option value. We remain confident in Google's AI full stack advantage & in the company’s ability to innovate around Generative AI, control costs, & generate solid top-line growth.
 
   
Valuation

   
Our December 2027 price target of $425, up from prior $420, is based on ~25x our ‘28E GAAP EPS of $17.29, which ex-cash equates to ~22x our ‘28E GAAP EPS ex-Other Bets of $18.37. We believe Alphabet shares should trade at a premium to the S&P 500 as Alphabet is one of the few companies in the S&P 500 with DD % revenue and EPS growth off of a very large base and a strong 34%+ GAAP Operating Income margin.
 
   
Risks to Rating and Price Target

   
 
Downside risks include: 1) 
Competition ramping across AI, Ads, Cloud, and for engineering/tech talent; 
2) 
potential for a return to heavy investment spending and margin compression;

Amazon.com (Overweight; Price Target: $365.00)
 
   
Investment Thesis

   
We believe Amazon is well positioned as the market leader in both e-commerce public cloud, and the secular shifts in each have meaningful headroom as US e-comm represents <25% of adjusted retail sales and we estimate <20% of IT spend is in the cloud. We believe Amazon’s flexibility across first party vs. third-party inventory, and the Prime membership serve as major advantages in its Stores business, and its multi-year head start in the cloud has led to ~30% AWS market share in ’26e. We believe AMZN is on track for multi-year margin expansion led by N.America operating efficiencies. High-growth AWS and Advertising revenue streams are AMZN’s most profitable businesses, further supporting margin expansion.
 
   
Valuation

   
Our December 2027 PT of $365 is based on ~27.5x our 2028E GAAP EPS of $13.34. This is a premium to GOOGL & META trading at ~21.0x on average, given AMZN’s faster bottom-line growth profile.
 
   
Risks to Rating and Price Target

   
 
Downside risks include: 
1)
 Pace of AWS reacceleration & normalized growth trajectory; 
2) 
State of US consumer, with macro pressures impacting spending levels; 
3) 
AMZN’s margin path is not always linear, with AMZN historically investing in growth; 
4)
 GenAI monetization is early & visibility is limited; 
5) 
Heavy competition across Stores & Cloud; 
6)
 Increased regulatory scrutiny could become a multi-year overhang; & 
7)
 Less traditional valuation support vs mega-cap peers. 
 
   
Meta Platforms Inc (Overweight; Price Target: $920.00)
 
   
Investment Thesis

   
We believe Meta’s virtual ownership of the social graph, strong competitive moat, and focus on the user experience position it to become an enduring blue-chip company built for the long term. Meta's reach and engagement continue to drive network effects, and its targeting abilities provide significant value to advertisers. META is delivering significant AI-driven ad ranking & recommendation gains, supporting greater engagement, ROAS, & revenue growth. More recently, we believe the company has laid out a credible path to monetizing its AI driven capex spend via consumer/business agents and model API access. We remain Overweight rated on Meta.
 
   
Valuation

   
 
Our December 2027 PT of $920 is based on ~26x our 2028E GAAP EPS of $35.44, further supported by our DCF model. We apply a premium to the SPX (16x) as we have greater confidence in Meta’s strong top-line growth, ongoing cost efficiencies, and path to monetizing its AI initiatives. We use this valuation approach as we believe it appropriately balances Meta’s valuation relative to its growth and industry peers.
 
 
   
Risks to Rating and Price Target

   
 
Downside risks include: 1)
 AI investments are heavy & monetization lags, which weighs on GAAP OI, GAAP EPS, & FCF; 
2)
 slower than expected revenue growth limits AI monetization upside; 
3)
 AI product & model launches do not resonate, weighing on engagement, revenue, & margins; 
4) 
competition for online and mobile ad dollars from Google, TikTok, OpenAI, and other online advertising companies; &
 5)
 the core ad business could be negatively impacted by litigation-related product changes 
 
   
Lyft (Neutral; Price Target: $19.00)
 
   
Investment Thesis

   
Lyft is a leading multimodal technology platform for personal consumer transportation, operating a consumer ridesharing marketplace at scale and building out networks of alternate transportation options. While underlying trends are healthier, there is concern around share loss to Waymo in markets like SF, and LA, and Lyft is behind Uber in terms of building AV partnerships. We remain on the sidelines and want to see a few quarters of consistent performance and margin improvement, along with the new long-term margin targets before getting more constructive. 
 
   
Valuation

   
 Our December 2027 price target of $19 is based on ~4.5x our 2028E FCF of $1.0B. This reflects a discount to marketplace peers trading at ~13x on average. 
We believe this discount is appropriate given our view that 1) shares remain a show-me story with sound execution required ahead, and 2) category position and scale lag those of its peer Uber.
 
 
   
Risks to Rating and Price Target

   
 
Risks to Upside:
 

   
1) Lyft gains market share against Uber without hurting unit economics; 2) Lyft is able to manage insurance risks in a better way, allowing for contribution margin upside; and 3) Valuation remains muted with re-rating possible on a sustained strong execution.

   
 
Risks to Downside:
 

   
 
1) Uber applies pricing pressure; 2) Loss of market share; 3) Regulatory risk pertaining to worker classification plays out unfavourably; 4) Margins fail to scale w/additional volume; and 5) Autonomous vehicle (AV) technology increases direct competition.
 
 
   
Fiverr (Neutral; Price Target: $10.00)
 
   
Investment Thesis

   
Fiverr is a two-sided global online marketplace that changes how people work together. Unlike hiring or staffing platforms, Fiverr operates a transactional marketplace where freelancers’ services, or Gigs, are listed as products in a catalogue that buyers can search, browse, and purchase. The freelancing market is large, at $247B in the US alone, and still early in shifting online, with the majority of freelancing work still done offline. Fiverr is facing AI and macro headwinds and is undergoing a multi-year transformation plan to move upmarket while deprioritizing low-end transactions creating revenue volatility. We think Fiverr’s focus on AI and moving upmarket should remove some of the SMB weakness, but the current transformation creates significant execution risk.
 
   
Valuation

   
Our Dec 2027 price target of $10 is based on ~8.0x 2028E Adj. EPS of $1.23, a discount to peers (Upwork, Recruit Holdings, and Seek Limited) trading at ~17.5x‘28E Adj.EPS on lower visibility of revenue getting back to growth amidst AI disruption. 
 
   
Risks to Rating and Price Target

   
 
Risks to Downside Include:
 1) 
Top of the funnel traffic continues to deteriorate tied to LLM-integrated search; 
2)
 Ongoing compression of low-value works continues to outweigh gains in higher-value categories; 
3)
 Macro continues to remain challenging, especially for SMBs; and
 4) 
Fiverr is headquartered in Israel, potentially presenting geopolitical and FX risks that are different from other companies in our coverage universe. 

   
 
Risks to Upside Include:
1) 
Fiverr is able to transition to high-end value projects smoothly and is able to offset disruption to the low-end gigs. 
2)
 Valuation remains cheap at <1.0x ‘28E EBITDA.
