Pre-3Q Earnings Estimate Revisions; Raising Google Cloud on TPUs & AWS on AI/Pricing; Slight Increase to Meta 2027 Expenses; Reiterate Hyperscaler Overweights

 	 
By Doug Anmuth AC <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=67wTafV&sig=QnoCky7n6C3eMK52f60EFujSTkC&v=2&url=https://markets.jpmorgan.com/research/analyst/I466794> , Dae K Lee, CFA <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=tib_A4R&sig=JCXzQ2lB6MDbffr2zQpQxDGCHh3&v=2&url=https://markets.jpmorgan.com/research/analyst/R544600> , Neeraj S Kookada, CFA <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=1IWBzaD&sig=MLuMNhAiHauNCmgYAaMWoQM1VHT&v=2&url=https://markets.jpmorgan.com/research/analyst/I613874> , Daniel Pfeiffer <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=oKSWmJ3&sig=JXMyS8M75yV3H5f0h71HBOhKPLx&v=2&url=https://markets.jpmorgan.com/research/analyst/W599953> , Margaret Hoffman <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=VeCvmvi&sig=FRch2IiCgMWQiD1QjdiuqSkqn3j&v=2&url=https://markets.jpmorgan.com/research/analyst/F741290>

Ahead of 3Q earnings & in conjunction with our 3Q preview, we’re updating estimates across hyperscalers (GOOGL, AMZN, & META), rideshare (UBER & LYFT), and small-cap FVRR. Please see an overview of the key changes and summary Old/New tables for each company below, along w/ our updated investment thesis, valuation, and risks for each company herein. 

*	GOOG/L: The biggest change is our Google Cloud growth rate, which increases from +88% Y/Y to +100% in 3Q, from +90% to +105% in 4Q, from +70% to +107% in 2027, and from +55% to +63% in 2028. The primary driver of the increase is greater TPU sale inclusion, & we could still be conservative. We also include the $5B debt raised by Waymo in early October, assume $80B of new debt raises in 2027, introduce quarterly estimates for 2027, and update for FX. These changes resulted in a 3% increase in our 2027E GAAP EPS to $14.05 and a 4% increase in our 2028E GAAP EPS to $17.29. We reiterate our Overweight rating & raise our Dec ‘27 PT from $420 to $425 based on ~25x our 2028E GAAP EPS. 
*	AMZN: We are increasing our AWS growth estimates and now model acceleration in 2027 to +40%, which could still be conservative. Key drivers to upside for AWS growth include scaling of its OpenAI partnership and deployment of an incremental 2M GPUs across 2027-2028, as well as pricing power. We note that AWS recently announced a 15% increase to GPU reserve pricing starting Oct 7th, its fourth consecutive quarterly price increase for reserved AI compute capacity across GPU classes, with H100/H200 & B200/B300 prices up 40-75% YTD. In addition, demand is broad-based and AWS’ compute allocation is diversified, with the largest customer accounting for only a single-digit percentage. We’re also rolling out 2027 quarters, updating for the recent £4.25B debt raise, as well as FX. Overall, we are raising our 2027/2028 estimates across Sales by 1-2%, OI by 3-5%, and GAAP EPS by 2-3%. We reiterate our Overweight rating & our $365 PT is based on 27.5x ‘28E GAAP EPS of $13.34. 
*	META: We are updating our numbers to reflect the $10B legal expense accrual in 3Q26 related to Meta’s settlement with 52 state attorneys general, which was not contemplated in the $165B-$169B full year expense guidance provided at 2Q earnings. We are also updating FX & raising our 2027E total GAAP expenses to $210B (+18% Y/Y, +25% Y/Y ex-$10B legal expense), partially offset by slightly higher revenue. Overall, our 2026 & 2027 GAAP EPS come down -10% & -2%, respectively. We reiterate our Overweight rating & our $920 PT is based on ~26x ‘28E GAAP EPS of $35.45. 
*	UBER: Uber recently announced <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=SeJVbZt&sig=NJKuTcFD7IewqjiUl3seqYNwFSS&v=2&url=https://www.uber.com/us/en/newsroom/simplerfasteruber/>  a restructuring of ~10% of its headcount, or ~3,300 people, with a focus on removing layers, simplifying team structures, refining global location strategy, and focusing people and investments against the biggest opportunities. As part of the restructuring, Uber reduced the number of micro-teams with one or two members by 50%, and also reduced staff more than seven layers down from the CEO by 20%. We calculate the restructuring-related annual cost savings at ~$600M+, with severance costs of ~$90M to be realized in 3Q. We are currently not modeling any cost savings from the restructuring as Uber looks to reinvest the savings in the business through lowering prices, improving selection, as well as continuing to invest in the growth areas. We are also updating #s for the recent €4.5B debt raise & FX. Overall, our 3Q GAAP OI & FCF come down by -4%/-3%, while Adj. OI is mostly unchanged. Due to a research restriction, we currently do not have a rating or PT for Uber. 
*	LYFT: Lyft recently disclosed in an 8-K <https://jpmorgan.email.streetcontxt.net/platform/al?a=14206483&ad=5006020275&h=wFDS_pr&sig=CVqBMTsy4OqspGackVXDZqOBRJN&v=2&url=https://investor.lyft.com/financials/sec-filings/content/0001628280-26-064095/lyft-20260930.htm>  that it had reached a settlement with the State of California, the Labor Commissioner for CA, and two private plaintiffs to resolve claims of driver misclassification in California between Apr 5, 2016 and December 15, 2020. The total settlement amount is $272.5 million, and Lyft can elect to make settlement payments over four years, with 5% simple interest accruing after year one & capped at $12.4M. Notably, Lyft reaffirmed its 3Q26 guide with Gross Bookings / Adj. EBITDA of $5.50B – $5.67B / Adj. EBITDA of $183M – $203M. We note that Lyft had accrued $210M for the matter in 4Q25, w/the charge split between contra revenue and G&A. We expect the incremental $62.5M above the prior accrual to hit 4Q G&A, which will impact 4Q GAAP OI/NI but won’t impact Adj. EBITDA. We currently assume Lyft will make the $272.5M payment over four years, which will be reflected in cash outflow. Also, Lyft’s CEO David Risher recently called out at an investor conference that 2H Rides growth will be stable vs. 1H, w/the lapping of Freenow (July 31st) offset by acceleration in the US. We’re currently modeling 2H Rides growth of 10.4% vs. 10.2% in 1H. We’re Neutral on Lyft shares & our $19 PT is based on ~4.5x 2028E FCF of $1.0B. 
*	FVRR: We are lowering our Fiverr estimates and now expect 2027 revenue growth in-line with 2026 of -15%, and 2027 EBITDA margins to be more in-line with our 4Q26 expectations of 10.5%. On 2Q earnings, Fiverr highlighted a noticeable deceleration in marketplace traffic and demand which had carried into 3Q. We think customer traffic has further deteriorated in 3Q. Based on SimilarWeb data, US visits to Fiverr.com decelerated from -10% Y/Y in 2Q to -22% Y/Y in 3QTD (-19% in July & -24% in August), while unique visitor traffic to Fiverr.com in the US also decelerated from -17% Y/Y in 2Q to -21% Y/Y in 3QTD (-21% in July & -22% in August). Recent changes in Google Search, including AI/LLM search summaries, are increasingly pushing users away from paid or organic search links, and while Fiverr is investing in GEO, current LLM CTRs remain below those of search, creating a significant headwind for top of the funnel traffic. Fiverr remains early in its transition from a transaction-oriented marketplace focused on low-value projects to a trusted work platform for higher-end & higher-value work. Mgmt expects the transition will take at least six quarters given high-value work currently represents only 15% of GMV, and some portion of the remaining 85% of the business will be disrupted by AI, while some will be more defensible. We are lowering our 2027/2028 revenue by 6%, while our 2027/2028 Adj. EBITDA is coming down 21% & 14%. Our Dec-27 PT comes down to $10 (from $11) based on 8.0x ‘28E non-GAAP EPS of $1.23, and we remain Neutral rated.
