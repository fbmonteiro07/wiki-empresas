Hi Felipe

Our commodities team has published a note on lithium.

Key Takeaways:
• Near-term lithium price risks are skewed higher, with further delays to CATL’s Jianxiawo restart and Zimbabwe’s January concentrate export ban capable of shifting our 88kt 2027 surplus toward a small deficit in 1H27. 
• ESS demand could provide further upside through year-end, with shipments up 94% YTD, tracking ahead of our 887 GWh 2026 estimate, and China’s battery export VAT rebate removal potentially pulling volumes forward into 4Q26. 
• Beyond the near-term squeeze, supply growth should reassert itself, as elevated lithium prices support mine restarts and expansions; we continue to expect a looser market and a downward price trajectory into the end of the decade. 

MS Outlook: Lithium carbonate is trading in line with our US$20k/t Q4 forecast, with near-term risks skewed to the upside from delayed Chinese mine restarts, Zimbabwe export restrictions, low inventories and potential battery shipment pull-forward into year-end. 

We forecast US$18.5-19k/t in 1H27, before prices trend lower as additional supply returns and the market moves into surplus. 

Equity View: Within our coverage, PLS (EW) remains our preferred exposure on relative valuation and expansion optionality. EW IGO having upgraded the stock (from UW) in July as valuation corrected.
