QuantumScape Corp. (QS): Research agreement at Honda and opportunities in non-auto markets, but potential reduced opportunity at VW; 2Q26 wrap 
	
24 July 2026 | 2:13PM EDT | Research | Equity 	

QuantumScape reported 2Q26 non-GAAP EPS of -$0.16, compared to GSe at -$0.17 and the Street (FactSet) at -$0.18. Management reiterated its 2026 adjusted EBITDA guidance of -$275 mn to -$250 mn compared to GS prior at -$263 mn and the Street at -$256 mn.

We believe this was a mixed report. The company made progress on business development, with the shipment of cells to an American defense prime. In addition, QuantumScape highlighted its recent research agreement with Honda, and is now engaged with four top-10 automotive OEMs. However, we see a potentially reduced opportunity at VW/PowerCo given the challenges that VW is facing in the market, coupled with the restructured milestone-based agreement. Overall, we maintain our Sell rating on the stock as several fundamental risks remain in our opinion that we don't believe are reflected in the current stock price.
