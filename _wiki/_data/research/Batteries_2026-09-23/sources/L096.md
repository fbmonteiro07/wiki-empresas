Global Metals & Mining: Albemarle (ALB) call feedback: ESS-led lithium market tightness; Australian JVs and Chile DLE lead medium-term growth pipeline, with capital-allocation flexibility/buyback under consideration 
	
17 August 2026 | 10:55AM AEST | Research | Equity 	

Following the 2Q CY26 result, we hosted the Albemarle (NYSE: ALB; not covered) Investor Relations team to discuss lithium market conditions, the operating outlook across its Energy Storage portfolio, and longer-dated growth and capital-allocation priorities (including at the Australian hard-rock joint ventures).

ALB expects to sell ~225-235kt LCE from its Energy Storage business in CY26, supported by integrated Chilean brine production, spodumene production at Wodgina, equity earnings from Greenbushes/Talison and downstream conversion. Near-term growth remains centred on the Australian JVs, where Wodgina continues to outperform on ore availability and recoveries, while Greenbushes CGP3 continues to ramp-up and is expected to reach full run-rate in 1Q27. Longer term, management views Salar de Atacama DLE as its highest-priority organic growth option, leveraging its existing brine resource, pond system and La Negra conversion infrastructure.

ALB highlighted a physically tight lithium market as ESS remains the fastest-growing demand segment, where with ESS demand being predominantly LFP-based and therefore carbonate-intensive, this has contributed to particularly tight lithium carbonate inventories. ALB also enters this period with increased financial flexibility following divestments, debt reduction and lower capex, ending June with ~US$1.6bn of cash, ~US$3.2bn of liquidity and ~0.5x net debt/adjusted EBITDA (vs. historical ~2-2.5x target range). With ALB's more capital-intensive growth projects still subject to permitting, study work and/or FID, ALB noted that it has flexibility to fund Australian brownfield growth while retaining optionality for longer-dated development and potential shareholder returns with a buyback also under consideration.

We outline our broader takeaways within.
