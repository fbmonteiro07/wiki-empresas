Smartphone testing to be completed by end of 2026

This morning ENVX announced that CEO Talluri has resigned effective Aug 13th, to pursue another opportunity. Mr. Ryan Benton, CFO will serve as interim CEO while the company conducts a CEO search evaluating both internal and external candidates. Chairman T.J. Rodgers was also appointed as executive chairman, effective immediately. Management does not expect a change in strategy and reaffirmed its F3Q guidance given during the earnings call. While we see Honor (ENVX's lead smartphone customer) confirming that ENVX's battery passes more than 1,000 cycles on the 0.2C discharge cycle test as a positive, we see the resignation of CEO Talluri as an overhang on the stock that adds incremental uncertainty as Mr. Talluri had good relationships with the smartphone OEMs and was actively engaged in helping get the battery qualified. We reiterate our Neutral rating as positives including revenue potential and large TAM are balanced by early manufacturing hurdles, smartphone qualification periods, uncertainty from the CEO transition, and expected negative margins & cash flow. 

F2Q came in strong, F3Q guide slightly lower than our ests

ENVX recently reported F2Q26 rev of $9.0bn, at the high end of guide, and operating loss of $28.8mn that was better than expected. F3Q rev was guided to $9.5mn (mid-pt), below St at $10.3mn and our est. of $10.5mn. F3Q EPS was guided to -$0.15 (mid-pt), below St at -$0.14 and our est of -$0.13. The main gating item to smartphone qualification is the accelerated cycle-life test which is underway & mgmt. expects the testing to be completed by end of F26. We like that Honor is willing to consider multiple variations of the test which should help the process. 

Eyewear shipments ramp, Defense pipeline expands

ENVX shipped about 2,100 batteries to a tier 1 smart eyewear customer in F2Q, and expects total 50,000 packs in F26. We expect eyewear GM to remain negative through year-end, as the 50,000 unit volume is insufficient to absorb manufacturing overhead and certain costs shift from operating expenses into cost of rev during the ramp. The drone, defense and industrial pipeline increased 41% q/q to about $183mn.

Ests move lower; PO moves to $5.00 (from $8.00)

Our F27 rev/EPS move lower to $83mn/$(0.66), from $103mn/$(0.64). PO moves to $5.00 (from $8.00) on 17x (prior 20x) EV/C27E sales. We use a lower multiple on lower revenue growth and overhang from the CEO transition.
