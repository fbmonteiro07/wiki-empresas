Global xEV sales up 8% YoY in Jul'26

Global xEV sales were +8% YoY/-12% MoM in July. The mild YoY growth was mainly due to solid xEV sales in Europe (+26% YoY), given rising EV penetration on more competitive new EV model launches, partially offset by weak xEV sales growth in China (-6% YoY) and the US (-8% YoY). Global BEV sales (+10% YoY) outgrew PHEVs (-5% YoY), with BEV penetration at 19.1% in July (vs. 20.7% in Jun'26). 

Global EVB July installed capacity +22% YoY/-14% MoM 

According to SNE Research, global EV battery installed capacity in July reached 116GWh, up 22% YoY/down 14% MoM. CATL's (+32% YoY) share was 40%, ranking No.1 globally, followed by BYD (+21% YoY) with a share of 16%. LGES (-18% YoY) ranked No.3 with 7% share. By region, Europe outperformed the global market, with installed capacity +35% YoY in July, while the US underperformed, -40% YoY. By form factor, prismatic accounted for the largest share at 84%, vs. cylindrical/pouch at 7%/9% in July'26.

China EV battery installed capacity +57% YoY in July

According to RealLi, total China EV battery installed capacity (based on EVs produced in China, including exports) in July was 100GWh, up 57% YoY/down 4% MoM (more driven by exports). Battery installed capacity by vehicle type in July: passenger BEV: 62%; passenger PHEV: 19%; E-bus (BEV+PHEV): 1%; and special vehicle (BEV+PHEV): 18%. By EV battery chemistry, in July, NCM/LFP share came in at 16%/84%. CATL's planned production is estimated to be 117.5GWh in Sep, up 67% YoY/6% MoM.

Korea EVB shipments -5% YoY YTD; weak US, EU m/s loss 

Korea's EVB installations fell 22% YoY in July, lagging global EVB growth of 22% YoY. Regionally, Europe EVB market remained solid (+35% YoY), while US demand stayed weak (-39% YoY). Among K-battery makers, LGES (-18% YoY) outperformed SK/SDI (-25%/-32%), supported by stronger EU shipments (LGES/SK/SDI: +10%/-5%/+7% YoY), while US shipments remained weak across the board (-64%/-59%/-67% YoY). YTD, Korean EVB shipments are down 5% YoY vs global EVB shipments growth of 20%, reflecting weak US demand (-24% YoY) and continued share loss in Europe YTD (EU EVB demand +29% YoY vs LGES/SK/SDI +12%/-1%/flat YoY).

Accelerating sodium-ion battery commercialization

On 17 August 2026, CATL's 1GW/4GWh sodium-ion independent shared energy storage power station project in Mojiang, Yunnan received regulatory filing approval, with Phase-I comprising 200MW/800MWh. We view this as a step toward sodium-ion battery's large-scale commercialization. During its 1H26 results briefing on 24 July, CATL highlighted strong sodium-ion battery order momentum following its April Tech Day and indicated that capacity expansion is underway. CATL also noted its existing lithium-ion battery production line can be easily converted to produce sodium-ion batteries.
