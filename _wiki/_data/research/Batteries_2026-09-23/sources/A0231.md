SK On to supply 9GWh of LFP batteries to US ESS firm

According to local press, SK On signed an agreement to supply US ESS company NeoVolta Power with a total of 9GWh of LFP pouch-type battery cells for ESS applications from 2027 to 2031. The contract is estimated to be worth c.W1.5tn. SK On will reportedly produce the cells at its Georgia plant in the US, which currently manufactures high-nickel batteries for EVs. The press added that the company plans to sign a separate agreement to supply another 9GWh by the year-end.

Expanding ESS exposure should support valuation

SK On previously guided for 20GWh of LFP battery order intake for ESS applications in 2026. The 9GWh contract with NeoVolta represents 45% of this target, while a potential additional 9GWh of orders would bring fulfillment to 90%. The five-year supply agreement could help SK On raise utilization rates at its US production facilities, in our view. Furthermore, we believe that expanding ESS exposure could also narrow SKI's valuation gap with peers, considering that SK On's relatively limited presence in the ESS market had constrained its long-term growth outlook - The stock currently trades at a 45% discount to LGES/SDI. Of note, BofA forecasts global additional ESS installations to increase from c.300GWh in 2025 to 867GWh in 2030, implying a 24% CAGR.

Expect limited financial impact from SKIET absorption

We believe the financial impact of absorbing SKIET should remain manageable, considering estimated 4.6%/3.8% EPS dilution in 2027/28 post-merger (assuming no cost savings), management target of c.W60bn in annual EBITDA uplift, and further potential upside from lower financing costs (see our report on SKIET absorption <[original URL omitted; available in raw archive] ). Despite the investor concerns on (1) the risk of additional financial commitments to support SKIET's operations; (2) potential cash outflows related to the PRS/appraisal rights (BofAe: c.W140-150bn); and (3) near-term EPS dilution, we believe strong earnings from Refining/Lubricants and the restructuring of the Battery division and a rising exposure to the ESS market could support longer term earnings.

Strong near-term earnings; maintain Buy

We believe the strong earnings outlook over the next 12 months remains intact, supported by robust refining margins and solid lubricants performance. QTD GRM remains at US$36/bbl, up 3% QoQ, as refined-product supply stays tight. In addition, the OSP for Arab Light to Asia is set at US$2/bbl below the Oman/Dubai average for Sep. QTD Group III prices also rose 41% QoQ, reflecting a major outage at the Pearl gas-to-liquids (GTL) facility in Qatar (one year from March'26). We maintain Buy on SKI.
