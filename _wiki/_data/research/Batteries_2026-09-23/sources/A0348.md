SQM (NEUTRAL): Q2 BIG BEAT ON RECORD LITHIUM VOLS

RESULT HIGHLIGHTS

*	Q2 beat across the board: Sales of $2.47bn (+10% vs cons.), adj. EBITDA of $1.32bn (+16% vs cons.) and EPS of $2.31/sh (+24% vs cons.). The upside was driven primarily by record lithium volumes, with additional support from strong iodine and Specialty Plant Nutrition performance.
*	Cash flow the standout: Free cash flow of $768m vs cons. -$44m, driven by operating cash flow of $915m vs cons. $181m and capex of just $147m (-35% vs cons.).
*	Lithium delivered a significant beat: Sales of $1.78bn (+12% vs cons.) and gross profit of $998m (+15% vs cons.). Record sales volumes of 84.1kt LCE (+14% vs cons., +59% YoY) more than offset pricing that was merely in line at $21.2/kg LCE (+152% YoY).
*	Record lithium volumes the key driver: Volumes reached an all-time high of 84.1kt LCE, substantially ahead of expectations and demonstrating continued strength in SQM's operating performance.
*	Pricing remains supportive: Realised lithium pricing of $21.2/kg LCE was broadly in line with consensus and management expects stable pricing in Q3, providing reassurance following recent industry volatility.
*	Iodine business very strong: Sales of $302m (+8% vs cons.) and gross profit of $173m (+16% vs cons.). Volumes of 4.1kt (+7% vs cons.) and record realised pricing of $73.4/kg (+2% vs cons.) were supported by robust demand from x-ray contrast media and LCD applications.
*	Specialty Plant Nutrition beat expectations: Sales of $323m (+8% vs cons.) and gross profit of $73m (+36% vs cons.), driven by strength in both pricing and volumes.
*	FY26 lithium production guidance maintained: Management reiterated lithium production guidance of 280-290kt LCE despite the exceptionally strong first half performance.
*	Long-term capacity expansion remains on track: SQM continues to target more than 300kt LCE of lithium capacity by end-2027, supporting further volume growth over the medium term.
*	Constructive demand outlook: Management expects global lithium demand to exceed 2.1mt LCE, highlighting continued confidence in EV and energy storage market growth.
