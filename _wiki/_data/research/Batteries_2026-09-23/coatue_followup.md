Production remains larger than transmission/distribution in the latest bar; the caption should not be read as proof that delivery already constitutes a majority.

## Implication for the broader review

**Our interpretation:** these two posts combine rapid storage deployment with a thesis about relieving grid constraints through distributed batteries. This introduces an operator/software/customer-network exposure alongside manufacturers, integrators and AI backup suppliers.

Base's [April 6, 2026 business-model explanation](https://help.basepowercompany.com/en/articles/10194881), used as background outside the review window, describes using software to coordinate home batteries for grid balancing while retaining outage protection. This is the company's account of how it earns revenue, not independent verification of its profitability.

The Coatue posts establish thematic interest and backing for Base Power. They do not establish investment positions or recommendations in CATL, Panasonic, Samsung SDI, Fluence, Tesla or lithium producers. They also do not settle the supplier-margin and project-return debates in the broker emails.
