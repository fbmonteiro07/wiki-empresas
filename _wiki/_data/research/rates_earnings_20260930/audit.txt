- The identity P1/P0=(E1/E0)*(PE1/PE0) is valid with the same earnings definition at both endpoints. No fitted constant is required. It does not forecast E1 or PE1, prove a causal yield relationship, or describe intra-period drawdowns.

- model.guardrails contains manually assigned PASS descriptions, not automated validation logic. Existing assertions check reported revision rounding and selected identities. Do not call all guardrails executable or automatically validated.

- Treasury nominal-minus-real par-yield subtraction is a rough spread measure; do not treat it as a pure observed inflation-expectations series.

### 6. What the prior analyst MUST do before this work ships
- Label 6% yields and 16x P/E as a conditional stress assumption, never a calibrated prediction. The independent current-multiple check only validates today's approximate valuation range.
- Show terminal-multiple sensitivity. With +30% EPS, a 19.5x start and terminal 14/16/18x produce -6.6667%/+6.6667%/+20.0000%. With a 19.2x start they produce -5.2083%/+8.3333%/+21.8750%.
- Keep dates and denominators prominent: matched two-month fixed-target revisions; shifted three-month next-year revisions; Treasury through Sep29; FactSet Sep24 observation. Do not imply they form a single precisely synchronized causal regression.
- Retain forecast/actual, EPS/aggregate earnings, roll/revision, and earnings-quality caveats beside the model. Describe qualitative guardrails as reviewed disclosures, or implement actual checks.
- Confirmed arithmetic for presentation: breakeven EPS = 19.5/16-1 = 21.875%; 19.2/16-1 = 20%. At 19.5x ->16x, +30% EPS gives +6.6667%, +15.4% gives -5.3128%, unchanged EPS gives -17.9487%. At 19.2x ->16x, corresponding returns are +8.3333%, -3.8333%, and -16.6667%. Price returns exclude dividends.

### 7. Bottom line
The historical input work and arithmetic hold up. The study can ship as a sourced historical comparison and conditional valuation exercise after terminal-P/E sensitivity is added; it cannot support a forecast that 6% yields imply 16x or that earnings will prevent a 2022-style drawdown. The largest uncertainty is the terminal valuation and future NTM earnings path, not the arithmetic.
