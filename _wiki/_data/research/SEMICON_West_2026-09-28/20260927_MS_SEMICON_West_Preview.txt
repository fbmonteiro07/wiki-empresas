                                                         We expect AMAT could provide some guardrails around mid- to long-term GM%. Our
                                                         thoughts are as follows:

                                                               · AMAT vs. ASML. ASML discloses GM% for its Installed Base Management (IBM)
                                                                  business. While IBM and AMAT's AGS have a similar mix - both include services and
                                                                  upgrades, with AGS also including spares and software - IBM's GM% is significantly
                                                                  higher. Our understanding is that the economics of an ASML tool upgrade are
                                                                  particularly attractive for the customer, allowing ASML to generate margins closer
                                                                  to those of a system sale. A case in point was 2025, when ASML saw customers
                                                                  accelerate NXE:3800E field upgrades to benefit from increased throughput.

                                                               · AMAT vs. LAM. At its JunQ earnings, LAM said it intends to drive GM% to the mid-
                                                                  50% range and OPM% to the mid-40% range, with annual expansion expected to
                                                                  take several years. Intra-quarter, LAM highlighted the key levers as new
                                                                  differentiated tools, its close-to-customer strategy, and pricing. One notable
                                                                  difference between the two companies is manufacturing footprint: LAM's primary
                                                                  manufacturing hub is in Malaysia, while AMAT's hubs are in Texas and Singapore,
                                                                  likely providing LAM with a cost advantage.

                                                         Our view. We see GM% for SPE companies as largely controllable and intentional, so we
                                                         don't necessarily have expectations. That said, we would be surprised if AMAT discussed a
                                                         path materially beyond the mid-50% range.

   What if? At its JulQ earnings, AMAT commented that it had "nearly doubled our
   manufacturing capacity over the past several years" and that, "based on the longer-term
   demand signals from our customers, we are now doubling our quarterly system output
   from current levels by 2028." Assuming quarterly system shipments reach ~$14bn by mid-
   2028, we estimate revenue of ~$68bn and EPS of ~$31.70, implying AMAT is currently
   trading at ~15x this capacity-implied bull case. Since 2020, AMAT has troughed at ~10x in
   4Q22 and ~13x in April 2025. Given the GM% expansion we model this cycle, we think
   AMAT merits a higher trough multiple than it has historically.
