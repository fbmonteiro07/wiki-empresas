WHAT'S CHANGED?
Camtek (CAMT.O)
Price Target

From:   $165.00To: $152.00



We’re focused on their growth story, HBM, financial model, and China. Stock trades at a premium to US SPE, looking for reasons to justify that. 



Key takeaways
HBM: looking for color on share by customer and the technology roadmap. Expect $1bn/$6 model (rev/EPS) with possibly a $1.4bn/$8.5 model as well. 

2026 has been a transition year for CAMT. We estimate Advanced Packaging (AP) and WFE to grow 69%/39% in 2026. CAMT has materially under grown, as we estimate product sales growth of approx. 20%. At their JunQ earnings, the company disclosed YTD orders of $600mn+, implying a $1,030mn annual run rate, suggesting 2027 should see growth closer to AP/WFE, which we see growing 52%/37%.
4 Questions. Camtek is hosting an Investor & Analyst Breakfast on Oct 14th at SEMICON West. We have 4 areas we’re focused on: 1) Growth Story: We’re interested in how CAMT frames its share opportunity against the key 2027 growth drivers, including non-TSMC CoWoS , HBM, SoIC, and EMIB-T, particularly as competition in AP increases from front-end players such as KLA and AMAT. 2) HBM: CAMT’s 2025 regional revenue mix implies significantly higher revenue per kwpm at MU/CXMT than at Hynix/Samsung. We currently model CAMT’s HBM revenue per kwpm remaining roughly flat through our forecast horizon and are interested in whether the roadmap or share dynamics could influence our assumptions. 3) Financial Model: CAMT’s current financial model calls for 54–55% GM and 33–35% OPM at $750mn of revenue (CY26 MSe $594mn). We expect the company to introduce a financial model at $1bn of revenue and potentially at a higher revenue level ($1.3–1.5bn), with implied EPS of $6 at $1bn of revenue and $8.50 at $1.4bn. 4) China: Following two strong years of growth in China (CY25 +84%; CY26e +10%), we’re interested in how CAMT views the forward opportunity in China CoWoS and HBM, including domestic competition. For more see China Advanced Packaging.
Thoughts on the stock: CAMT trades at a premium to our US SPE coverage and OEM averages (CY27 P/E 29x vs 25x/27x). Do we agree? Not necessarily. Growth has tracked below our coverage average in 2026 (20% vs. 35%), and without a clear catalyst for outgrowth in 2027/28 or a meaningful path to margin expansion, we question the premium valuation. We fine-tune our CY27 estimates from $788mn/$5.01 to $802mn/$5.14, primarily reflecting higher margin assumptions. Our prior $165 PT was based on 26x CY28 EPS, a 1-turn premium to through-cycle average. We lower our target multiple to 23x, a 1-turn premium to historical cycle-trough multiples, resulting in a PT of $152. Read more...
