GPT-5.6 Luna: a live Jevons experiment

On Jul 30 OpenAI cut Luna's list price 80% — three days after a 50% discount appeared on its OpenRouter endpoint. Realized price fell ~93%. Two weeks on, Luna serves 13.5× the tokens for roughly the same realized revenue — an almost exactly unit-elastic response, measured live. The strict Jevons claim (total spend rising) survives only on a cache-off ceiling basis — and OpenAI's total implied take on the venue fell ~15% as its premium models collapsed into Luna.

Figure 3 · ElasticityUnit-elastic — not the elastic regime the Street coefficient implies

Arc elasticity = ln(Q₁/Q₀) ÷ ln(P₁/P₀). At exactly −1, a price cut is revenue-neutral; beyond it, revenue grows. The reference band is the only third-party coefficient in the wiki (Exponential View via 13D, Aug 6: “every 10% price cut → 12–18% more tokens”, i.e. 1.2–1.8) — logged there as uncorroborated. This is the first measured check: Luna lands below the band on every basis; only Terra's cache-off reading reaches it.

Migration probe — how much is re-routing?

Three reads on whether the ×13.5 is new demand or arriving traffic

67% of the entire PRE→POST level shift completed within 24–48h of the cut (77bn → 477bn/day by Jul 31). Induced demand needs build-and-adopt cycles; a two-day step looks like existing traffic re-routing to the promo.
82% of Luna's absolute weekly gain (+4,326bn) equals the w/w growth of one app — Hermes Agent (+3,542bn, OpenRouter's #1). Apps aren't broken out by model, so this shows concentration, not attribution — but the surge is not broad-based.
+13.2% is how much the rest of OpenRouter (ex-Luna) grew over the same two weeks — the venue itself is expanding, which bounds neither the migration term nor net-new creation.

Honesty boxWhat this does and doesn't prove

  • The ceiling is not a conservative proxy here. Tokens × listed price normally just scales revenue down ~3× (house rule). But when the cache mix shifts between windows — 76.8% → 86.0% hit rate, reads at 1/10th price — the ceiling flips the sign of the spend change (+23.6% vs −2.0%). Any Jevons claim built on cache-off token revenue should be treated as suspect, here and elsewhere.
  • Volume response is real but its composition is unresolved. The 48-hour step (67% of the shift), the Hermes Agent concentration (82% of the gain), and the 50%-off promo all point to a big re-routing/venue-shift component. Net-new token creation is strictly less than ×13.5. Unresolvable from public data.
  • Vendor-level, this was not revenue-accretive on this venue: OpenAI's implied weekly take fell $8.82M → $7.52M (−14.8%) as gpt-5.5 (×0.50), gpt-5.4 (×0.49), gpt-5 (×0.23), o4-mini (×0.21) and sol-pro (×0.46) collapsed; Luna's +$102k offset 1/14th of the −$1,402k ex-Luna decline. Basis: cache-off, each model at its own listed price — the only basis available across models.
  • Elasticity is a range, not a point: −0.99 on the best basis (OR effective), −1.09 cache-off, −0.83…−0.91 if Luna's own +24.5%/wk pre-trend is extrapolated as the counterfactual (aggressive — launch ramps decelerate; Sol's realized +15% is the family's actual organic path). Every basis sits at or below unity, i.e. below the Exponential View 1.2–1.8 band. Terra's −1.55 is cache-off only (no effective series pulled) — label it accordingly.
  • Prices are OpenRouter customer-paid under a 50%-off promo — implied spend ≠ OpenAI revenue (promo funding split unknown), ~2% of tokens bill on Azure/Bedrock at 5–10× the OpenAI-endpoint POST price, and Flex (0.5×)/Priority (2×) service tiers are unmodeled (bounded to a few % by the effective-output tie-out). If the discount lapses, effective price doubles overnight — a free second experiment; watch token stickiness.
  • Competitive trigger, not isolated: the cut landed the day DeepSeek shipped V4-Flash-0731 ($0.09/$0.18), which itself ×2.4'd to 15.1tn tokens/wk (#1, 21.4% share). The whole cheap tier is repricing at once.
