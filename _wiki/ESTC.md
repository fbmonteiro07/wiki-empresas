## Snapshot
Elastic sells one consumption-metered data platform (Elasticsearch) packaged as three solutions — Search/AI, Observability and Security — with no separate SKUs: "they're purchasing the platform… in cloud, the meters spin differently" (Kulkarni, Q4 FY25 call, 2025-05-29). Historic commitment mix disclosed once: ~40% observability, ~25% security, the rest search, with search the fastest-growing on GenAI (Prengel, Q3 FY25 call, 2025-02-27). Management now frames the company as "the leading data retrieval and context engineering platform for the AI era" (Kulkarni, Q1 FY26 call, 2025-08-28) — the "permission-aware enterprise context plane" in Citrini's AI-stack map (Citrini Semis, 2026-07-20, via theme page). Revenue is reported as total, subscription, Elastic Cloud, and since Q4 FY25 the management-preferred **sales-led subscription revenue** (subscription ex monthly self-serve cloud), guided from Q2 FY26. FY26: revenue +17%, sales-led subscription +20%, non-GAAP OM 16.4%, adj. FCF margin ~20% (Welihinda, Q4 FY26 call, 2026-05-28).

## Current state (Q1 FY27, call 2026-08-27)
Beat on all guided metrics and the first quarter of the promised constant-currency acceleration. Total revenue **$478M, +15%** (15% cc, up from 14% cc in Q4); sales-led subscription **$399M, +18%** (17% cc, up from 16% cc). Annual cloud +27% (from 26%); monthly cloud flat. **cRPO $1.2B, +21%** (20% cc — second straight quarter at 20% cc); **RPO $1.9B, +27%** (27% cc). **$100K+ customers >1,800**, +80 net q/q ("largest increase to date"), now **90% of sales-led subscription revenue** (87% a year ago). **>37% of $100K+ customers use Elastic for AI** (>670 customers, +70 q/q), from ~21% a year ago. NER slipped **112% → 111%** — a trailing-four-quarter artefact of last year's lower cc growth, expected to recover "within four quarters" (Welihinda). ## Debate / thesis
- **Bull — the context plane is cheap optionality on top of a stable compounder.** Citrini's scorecard: "ESTC monetizes the indexed copy of enterprise information… positive; inexpensive AI-search optionality against ~14% FY27 guide" (Citrini Semis, 2026-07-20, via theme page) — and the guide has since moved to +15.2% total / +17.5% cc sales-led (Q1 FY27 call, 2026-08-27). The mechanism is consumption: AI workloads "tend to be more compute intensive" (Kulkarni, Q1 FY26), the AI cohort expands "roughly 5%, a little over 5% faster than the rest" (Kulkarni, Q4 FY26), and penetration of the $100K+ base has gone 17% → 23% → 28% → >1/3 → >37% over five quarters. Management's own proof point for the context layer: agents using Elastic as context cut token usage 70% vs naive RAG (Kulkarni, Q4 FY26 blog reference). Commitments lead revenue — cRPO 20% cc for two quarters, RPO +27–28%, non-current RPO +43% — and ~70% of the FY27 sales-led guide was covered by cRPO at the start of the year (Welihinda, Q4 FY26). Security is the near-term engine: two $20M+ TCV cloud wins (Q2 FY26), CISA SIEM-as-a-service pulling civilian agencies onto Elastic Cloud, a Fortune 50 bank SecOps consolidation (Q4 FY26), a large agency migrated "in under one month" (Q1 FY27). Margins are now a second lever: FY27 OM ~19.4% and FY29 target ~25% after the AI-driven reorganization. UBS's Keirstead lists Elastic among the infra-data names that "ripped" this year (UBS, 2026-08-31, via theme page).
- **Bear — stable, not inflecting, and priced as if it might.** UBS's Roddy Sultan: "GitLab and Elastic… more than doubled off the bottom, but still trade at four to five times sales… could be under pressure over the next few months as fundamentally things sound stable but nothing is really inflecting" (UBS · Sultan, 2026-08-31, via theme page). The transcripts give him ammunition: total revenue growth has sat at 15–18% for six quarters, cc cRPO was stuck at 15% for two quarters (Thill, Q3 FY26: "why aren't we seeing a faster inflection?"), NER has drifted 112% → 111%, monthly cloud is flat by design and dilutes the headline, and the FY27 sales-led guide (17.5% cc) is below FY26's 20% (Hedberg, Q4 FY26) — the acceleration story rests on an H2 ramp that has to be delivered. The "AI customers" share has been redefined twice (see table note). Competition is coming from three directions raised on the calls: hyperscaler-native vector search ("they all have their own vector database functionality… beyond their own, we want to be number one" — Kulkarni, Q4 FY25), frontier-model harnesses (Hedberg, Q3 FY26: competition or partner?), and data-platform peers solving the same context problem (Singh, Q1 FY27, citing Palantir); RAG durability itself was questioned twice (Singh, Q4 FY25 and Q2 FY26). On the observability side the theme page's deflation risk — cheap lake storage vs premium log indexing (Arora's "a third of the cost", PANW Q1 FY26 call; Databricks LakeWatch, Octahedron 2026-07-15) — cuts at Elastic's logs-led land motion even as it wins on TCO today. Restructuring ($20M charges, $13M cash in Q1 FY27) shows the margin step-up is not free.
- **The observability + security stretch.** WFC's cloud expert grouped Elastic with Datadog and Dynatrace as observability platforms that have "stretched into security" with "AI baked into their platform", and called that broadening "the bigger threat to security vendors" (WFC cloud/MSFT expert call, 2026-04-14, via theme page). On the calls the stretch runs the other way too: security was the fastest grower in Q4 FY26 and Q1 FY27 while observability — "the third pillar of the stool" (Kulkarni, Q1 FY27) — is being rebuilt via the metrics relaunch and Deductive AI. Kulkarni reads Palo Alto/Chronosphere and CrowdStrike/Onum as "validation… that others are trying to emulate us" (Q2 FY26).
- **IBM-target optionality.** Jefferies' Thill: after Confluent there are four public infra-software assets left — "Datadog, Dynatrace, Elastic, Mongo, GitLab… the only ones they could really buy would be Dynatrace, Elastic" (Jefferies · Thill, AI call, 2026-06-01). Nothing on the calls addresses it; the $500M buyback (76% used by Q1 FY27) and a 50%-of-FCF return policy "unless we have attractive acquisition opportunities" (Welihinda, Q4 FY26) make Elastic an acquirer as much as a target.

## Risks
- **Consumption variability** — management repeatedly warns quarterly revenue "can naturally vary in a consumption model" (Welihinda, Q3 FY26); cloud commitments ramp ratably while self-managed recognizes part up front, so mix shifts (as in Q4 FY26) move in-quarter revenue.
- **Execution on the H2 ramp** — FY27 acceleration depends on ramped reps and pipeline conversion in the seasonally largest quarters; the sales-led guide is only ~70% covered by cRPO (Welihinda, Q4 FY26).
- **US public sector concentration** — top-5 vertical; federal-civilian constraints hurt Q4 FY25, the 2025 shutdown slipped renewals in Q2 FY26; CISA is now a large, ramping single relationship.
- **Hyperscaler and platform competition in vector/context** — native vector search at AWS/Azure/GCP, Postgres/pgvector and MongoDB (theme page §2), frontier-lab harnesses; OpenSearch remains the open-source fork Elastic benchmarks against (Kulkarni, Q3 FY26).
- **Log-economics deflation** — cheap lake storage undercutting premium log indexing (theme page §3.1) could compress the observability land motion.
- **Definitional drift in KPIs** — the AI-customer share and the sales-led metric were both introduced/widened by this management team; track the like-for-like series.
- **Pricing dependence** — annual price increases (~5% May 2025; 3%/5% FY27) support the growth floor; management refuses to disaggregate their contribution.
- **FX** — reported and cc growth have diverged by 1–3pts in most quarters; guidance is framed on cc.
- **M&A integration** — Keep, Jina AI, Deductive AI acquired in ~15 months.
