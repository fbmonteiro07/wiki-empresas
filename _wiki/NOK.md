# NOK — Nokia

_Wiki · initiated 2026-10-11 · EUR unless stated · [Master index](00_INDEX.md). Research scenarios below are not approved Capstone estimates or Bloomberg consensus._

## Snapshot

**The business is improving; the share price already requires execution.** Nokia combines Optical, IP and Fixed Networks in **Network Infrastructure (NI)** with radio, core software and patent licensing in **Mobile Infrastructure (MI)**. Portfolio Businesses sit separately. The model's strongest earnings driver is optical/IP growth, but MI remains the largest profit contributor. This is an AI-networking investment with material telecom exposure, rather than a pure optical company. Nokia's structure took effect on **2026-01-01**; Infinera closed on **2025-02-28**. (Nokia, **2026-01-29**, S3/S4.)

**Working view, 2026-10-11:** constructive on the optical opportunity, valuation-sensitive on the equity. The base research scenario produces **€0.37 normalized 2027 EPS**, implying **24.9x earnings** and **3.3% FCF yield after lease principal** at the **$10.36 ADR close on 2026-10-09**. Those are model outputs, not broker targets. The optical order book must become deliveries, margin and cash to justify a stronger conclusion. [Workbook](../outputs/nok-2026-10-11/NOK_simplified_model.xlsx) · [Worked model and limitations](../outputs/nok-2026-10-11/model-methodology.md).

## Current state (latest quarter)

**Use the July recast.** FWA CPE and Enterprise Campus Edge became discontinued operations; earlier segment allocations also changed. **FY2025 comparable sales €19,480m / EBIT €2,092m** and **NI EBIT €760m / MI €1,531m** are the matched historical base. Nokia's **2026 EBIT guide of €2.1–2.6bn** replaced **€2.0–2.5bn** solely for the perimeter change; it was not an operational upgrade. CEO Justin Hotard expected somewhat above the new midpoint. (Nokia, **2026-07-23**, S1/S2.)

**Demand is tangible, conversion is pending.** Q2 Optical and IP sales grew **20% and 16% at constant currency**, while Fixed fell **2%**. The workbook uses reported EUR assumptions and does not claim a precise organic-growth reconciliation. (Nokia / Hotard, **2026-07-23**, S1.)

## Debate / thesis

- **Bull:** more cloud optical demand, Infinera integration and added manufacturing capacity can lift NI's revenue and operating margin simultaneously. Nokia planned San Jose capacity to begin ramping in **Q4 2026** and Pennsylvania test/packaging expansion beginning **Q3 2026**. These are ramp plans, not evidence of delivered capacity. The original **2028 NI margin target of 13–17%** provides an external check on the model's **14% base assumption**, but predates July's perimeter change. (Nokia **2026-07-23**, S1; CMD **2025-11-19**, S5; research scenario **2026-10-11**.)
- **Bear:** orders may be supply-constrained, margin gains may arrive later, and the group still depends on telecom spending and software timing. MI contributes **€1.67bn of the model's €2.74bn 2027 group EBIT**, before corporate costs. Optical strength alone cannot erase an MI miss. H1 cash outflow makes the second-half cash recovery a separate test. (Research scenario **2026-10-11**, calibrated to Nokia S1/S2.)
- **Broker bull case:** Jefferies **William Beavington, relaying Janardan Menon, 2026-09-17**, called Nokia a **Buy / top pick** and forecast approximately **31% optical growth in both 2027 and 2028**. This comes from the archived [CIEN research synthesis](CIEN.md), not a newly verified full Nokia report. The model uses that growth only in its bull scenario; customer-equivalence and share-gain claims remain broker hypotheses.
- **Supply counterweight:** JPMorgan **Joseph Cardoso, 2026-09-28 ECOC call**, relaying meetings with Nokia/Ciena/Cisco, described pump-laser packaging constraints across suppliers. Supplier onboarding and ramp take time; vertical integration does not by itself prove unconstrained supply. [Archived call](../_equity_calls/Semis/2026-09-28_JPMorgan_Hardware-Networking-ECOC-AllStars-takeaways-Cardoso.md). Read-through: [CIEN](CIEN.md), [optical/CPO theme](themes/optical-cpo.md).

**NVIDIA is funded strategic support, not a new revenue order.** Nokia announced **$1bn / €0.86bn of equity for 166,389,351 shares on 2025-10-28** and confirmed completion on **2025-11-13** in its **2026-01-29** release. The cash and shares are already embedded in subsequent balances. No additional investment proceeds or separate AI-RAN revenue windfall are added to this model. See stable deal **NVDA-NOK-EQUITY-20251028** in the [deal ledger](themes/ai-compute-deals.md). (Nokia S8/S4.)

## Simplified model — research scenario, 2026-10-11

**H1 2026 is actual; H2 2026 is forecast from the matched H2 2025 base.** Later years use separate optical/IP/fixed/MI revenue drivers and segment margins. All forecast inputs are labelled HARD, PARTIAL or ESTIMATE on the workbook's Drivers/Actuals tabs.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Comparable revenue | 19.48 | 20.85 | 22.35 | 23.73 |
| Comparable EBIT | 2.09 | 2.40 | 2.74 | 3.15 |
| Normalized diluted EPS, EUR | n.a. | 0.330 | 0.372 | 0.424 |
| Company-definition FCF | 1.47 | 1.56 | 1.92 | 2.20 |
| FCF after lease principal | n.a. | 1.35 | 1.71 | 1.99 |

Actual sources: **Nokia 2026-07-23 recast / 2026-01-29 Q4 release**, S2/S4. Forecasts: **research model, 2026-10-11**. Historical EPS is omitted because the matched recast non-controlling-profit input was not verified.

The base assumes **2027 optical/IP growth of 20%/15%**, **NI/MI margins of 12.5%/14%**, **€185m corporate cost**, **€200m financial income**, **26.5% tax**, and **70% FCF conversion**. Growth and margins are estimates; 2026 financial/tax/conversion ranges are company-guided, but their extension into 2027 is an estimate. **2027 EPS = [(2,739.21 + 200 + 6) × (1 − 26.5%) − 8] / 5,801.886 = €0.3717.** Associates, minority profit and diluted shares are explicitly anchored to H1/Q2 actuals, then extrapolated. See the worked methodology for every intermediate step.

| Research scenario, 2026-10-11 | 2027 EBIT, €bn | 2027 EPS, € | 2028 EBIT, €bn | 2028 EPS, € |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Bear | 2.13 | 0.277 | 2.25 | 0.288 |
| Base | 2.74 | 0.372 | 3.15 | 0.424 |
| Bull | 3.25 | 0.451 | 4.07 | 0.558 |

**What the price requires:** **$10.36 ÷ 1.1206 USD/EUR ÷ 1 ordinary share per ADS = €9.245 per share**. Dividing by base EPS gives **24.9x 2027 earnings**. At an illustrative **25x** hurdle, the reverse calculation requires approximately **€2.72bn of 2027 EBIT**, close to the base. The multiple is a judgemental hurdle, not a price-target methodology. Sources: **Stock Analysis/S&P Global close 2026-10-09; ECB 2026-10-09; Nokia annual report published 2026-03-05**, S6/S7/S9; model calculation **2026-10-11**.

**Falsifiers and sensitivities:** a **100bp change in both 2027 segment margins** moves EPS approximately **€0.0278**. Removing **€200m financial income** lowers EPS **€0.0253**. Reducing FCF conversion from **70% to 60%** cuts FCF by **€274m**. The prior CNS **€37m provision reversal** could contaminate H2 margin calibration: assuming it all belonged to retained MI lowers modeled 2026 EBIT **€38.11m**, EPS **€0.00484**. Actual allocation is unresolved. (Research calculations **2026-10-11**; reversal disclosed by Nokia **2026-01-29**, S4 p.6.)

### Share anchor and assumptions

**Morgan Stanley / Terence Tsui, Meta Marshall and Antonio Jaramillo, 2026-07-09, pp.8–9**, reproduces Dell'Oro charts with **2025 Nokia share of 23% in metro DCI and 25% in long-haul DCI**, with **Infinera separately at 3% in each**. House aggregation gives **26% / 28%** and an unweighted midpoint of **27%**. Geographic basis, mix weights and consolidation treatment remain unresolved. Applying that midpoint to future AI-WAN is an **ESTIMATE**, not a measured combined Nokia share.

The research scenarios use **22% / 27% / 32%** share, **12% / 18% / 24%** whole-Optical EBIT margins and **$8.82bn / $11.11bn / $14.00bn** 2028 AI-WAN TAM. Bear shifts the interpolated ramp back one year; Bull brings the 2029 endpoint forward one year. All are house assumptions dated **2026-10-12**. Jefferies / William Beavington relaying Janardan Menon (**2026-09-17**) cites only a **double-digit Optical margin target**; it does not establish 18% or 24%.

Source: **research sensitivity, 2026-10-12**, using the source inputs above and the **2026-10-11** model. Optical operating contribution is EBIT after assumed tax per diluted share, before corporate costs and financing; it is not reported segment EPS. These are not the original workbook's full-company Bear/Bull cases.

**Worked Base path:** interpolate TAM as **7 × (14/7)^(2/3) = $11.1118bn**. At **27%** share and **1.1206 USD/EUR**, modeled AI-WAN sales are **€2.6773bn**. The original **€5.3720bn** Optical forecast less this amount leaves a **€2.6947bn remaining-Optical allocation**, held fixed across scenarios. This is a residual of the existing forecast, not observed non-DCI sales. Base Optical EBIT is **€5,371.995m × 18% = €966.959m**. Remove the **€752.079m** already allocated to Optical at the original **14% NI margin**. Incremental EPS is **(966.959 − 752.079) × (1 − 26.5%) / 5,801.886m shares = €0.02722**; add original group EPS **€0.423535** to obtain **€0.450757**. Tax, shares, FX and the baseline allocation remain estimates for 2028.

**What drives the result:** Base revenue is unchanged by construction, so its EPS uplift is entirely margin. Bull's **€0.1082** increase comprises **€0.0234** from revenue at the original margin and **€0.0848** from margin, including the interaction. At Base, **+1pp share adds €0.00226 EPS**, **+$1bn TAM adds €0.00549**, and **+1pp Optical margin adds €0.00681**, holding the reference residual fixed. A **+1pp higher baseline Optical margin allocation reduces the EPS bridge by €0.00681**; baseline segment profit is a material uncertainty.

**Cross-checks:** Jefferies' **~31% growth in 2027 and 2028** applied to the house **€3.89275bn 2026 Optical forecast** gives **€6.680bn** 2028 revenue, close to the sensitivity Bull's **€6.693bn**. This is a house bridge using broker growth, not Jefferies' absolute sales forecast or independent validation. Bull implies **19.4% NI margin**, above the older **13–17% 2028 CMD range** (Nokia **2025-11-19**, before July's perimeter change), and is flagged as a stretch. No capex or working-capital forecast is added.

## Catalysts / what to watch

- **2026-10-22 — Q3 results:** Optical/IP deliveries and new orders, supply constraints and MI software phasing. The July guide calls for revenue **+3–7% sequentially** and EBIT broadly flat on Q2. Track order-to-sales conversion separately. (Nokia **2026-07-23**, S1 pp.2,30.)
- **Q4 2026 — profit and cash conversion:** At Q3 guide midpoints, the base requires Q4 revenue **€6.55bn**, EBIT **€1.23bn**, margin **18.9%**, and H2 FCF **€1.67bn**. These are research requirements. Check software recognition, NI profitability and the cash release. (Research scenario **2026-10-11**, using Nokia **2026-07-23**, S1.)
- **2027 — capacity conversion:** Check San Jose/Pennsylvania output, yield, customer qualification and commercial shipments. Growth without NI margin progress would weaken the base case; a ramp announcement does not close this test. (Nokia ramp plans **2026-07-23**, S1 p.3; research tests **2026-10-11**.)

## Risks

Customer spending, optical supply and integration execution; patent-licensing variability; software recognition; FX; restructuring cash; and dilution. Comparable EBIT excludes charges that can still consume cash. The simplified FCF conversion captures aggregate cash drag but has no working-capital schedule. June net cash and basic shares are held fixed for valuation; EPS uses diluted weighted shares. This is a scoped initiation and scenario model, not an exhaustive two-year filing/transcript or sell-side audit.
