## Snapshot
Okta is the largest independent identity vendor — the **identity system of record for >20,000 customers**, handling **>45 billion authentication events a month and blocking >8 billion threats a month** (Kelleher, Q4 FY26 call, 2026-03-04). Two platforms sold by two specialised sales forces: **Okta** (workforce identity — SSO/MFA, Identity Governance, Privileged Access, Identity Security Posture Management, Identity Threat Protection; sold to security/IT buyers) and **Auth0 / Okta Customer Identity** (developer-facing customer identity for B2B/B2C apps). ~$3.2bn revenue run-rate, ~99% subscription after professional services were pushed to GSIs (Tighe, Q2 FY27, 2026-08-26); Rule-of-40 every year since IPO. In the Morgan Stanley taxonomy it is a **control-point** vendor in the second-fastest cyber segment (Identity & Access $29.7bn 2025E, +17.6% '24-'29 CAGR) — the layer MS argues is defensible against probabilistic models because it *"require[s] low-latency, deterministic responses"* (MS, 2026-04-20). The 2026 thesis is **agentic identity**: *"AI agents are the fastest growing identity in the enterprise but also the least governed"*; McKinnon expects most large enterprises to *"have more agentic identities than human ones"* (Q1 FY27 call, 2026-05-28). The bear is the oldest one in software: a seat-based model in a segment where the largest platform (MSFT Entra, ~31% IAM share) is bundled and the largest cyber consolidator (PANW) just bought its way in via CyberArk.

## Current state (latest quarter — Q2 FY27, quarter ended 2026-07-31, call 2026-08-26)

Stock **+20%** on the night, vs CRWD +11% and CRM +13% the same evening (tape note on [[CRWD]] page).
- **Guide (raised):** Q3 FY27 revenue **+10%**, cRPO **+11–12%**, non-GAAP OM **24–25%**, FCF margin **21–23%**; FY27 revenue **+10–11%** (from +9%), OM **26%** (from 25–26%), FCF margin **28–29%** (from 27–28%). Both include ~1pt of revenue drag from shifting services to GSIs and ~1pt of FCF drag from lower interest income (Tighe, 2026-08-26). ⚠️ *"Closer to the pin"* guidance philosophy unchanged after two ~2–3% beats — UBS called that *"slightly odd"* (UBS · Roger Boyd callback with CFO Brett Tighe, 2026-08-27).
- **CFO callback colour (UBS / Boyd, 2026-08-27):** Q2 gross bookings were a record 2Q, *"bigger than what they typically see in 3Q and close to what they see in 4Q"* — *"everything went right… they ran the table… a special quarter"*; on returning to mid-teens growth: *"whatever number you put in your head… that's what we're shooting for"*; strong **Okta Customer Identity** quarter with **key federal wins** (OCI is fully certified civilian + DoD, Auth0 is not — *"critical to their federal opportunities over the next year"*); the **Permiso** acquisition is *"fairly immaterial… think about it as an acqui-tech"*.
- **AI products — real deals, still immaterial:** *"dozens of AI deals"* in Q2, including a **multimillion-dollar Okta for AI Agents deal with a Fortune 50 healthcare company**, a global consulting firm that chose Okta over an in-house build, and one of the world's largest asset managers with *"thousands of agents from multiple vendors… running in production"*; AI deal sizes remain **bigger than the Okta average** (Tighe). **Agent Gateway** launched — runtime policy enforcement across *"Claude Code, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, Salesforce Agentforce, and any agent pointed at an MCP endpoint"* (McKinnon; transcript renders it "Cloud Code"). **Okta for AI Agents Core** GA for FedRAMP/HIPAA; **IL5** DoD authorisation earned ahead of the DoD 2027 Zero Trust mandate. ⚠️ McKinnon, Q1: the AI products *"didn't materially contribute… a little bit in the guidance, but heavily discounted"* — *"it's not a pipeline problem. It's how can we execute on turning this pipeline into real dollars"* (2026-05-28).
- **Pricing philosophy, stated:** AI agents are priced as *"an uplift to a named user, or… to a monthly active user"* because that is how customers buy and because most agents today act on behalf of a user; per-agent pricing is *"tricky… very hard to define the number of agents"*; *"right now, we're pricing for market share and reducing friction"* (McKinnon, Q1 FY27). ➤ **The seat-based model is being extended, not replaced — which is exactly the wrinkle Wells Fargo flagged (below).**
- **Demand mechanism management describes:** *"conversations that begin with securing AI broaden into identity modernization initiatives"*; a retailer's M&A consolidation onto Okta was *"catalyzed"* by an Okta-for-AI-Agents conversation (Tighe, Q2). On Mythos/Glasswing: cyber buyers are *"all over"* getting access, but *"the world… has extrapolated that from thinking that they're panicking and changing their priorities. That is not true… everyone is reinforcing the fundamentals… 80% of breaches go through identity"* (McKinnon, Q1 FY27). *"I don't think it's necessarily showing up in agentic identity yet. That's still early."*

## Debate / thesis
- **Capstone model:** none — Okta is not on the comp sheet; no house estimates. Consensus only (BBG block below; px $166.50, EV ~$26.9bn, CY26E rev $3,199m / EPS $3.79, CY27E rev $3,518m / EPS $4.34, PT cons $182.4, 38 Buy / 9 Hold / 0 Sell — 2026-09-11). Implied ~**8.4x CY26E / 7.6x CY27E EV/sales**, ~**33x CY26E EV/EBIT** (house arithmetic on the BBG snapshot).
- **Bull:**
  - **Right layer for the agent era, and the multiple has not caught up.** Every agent needs a credential, a scope and a revocation path — a per-identity SKU. MS sizes **agentic identity at ~$60bn+, double the standalone market, *"a positive for PANW, OKTA and SAIL"*** (MS · Marshall/Lountzis, 2026-08-20) and keeps OKTA among *"the four best positioned"* for AI (MS, 2026-04-20/04-27). The Glasswing-partner expert: *"identity is the new control plane for security, especially because of AI. Vendors that can provide real-time identity control… will be the winners"* (BTG Mythos expert call, 2026-06-18 — ⚠️ interested party). Citrini's sovereignty thesis: enterprises *"will not collapse their security architecture into the same model provider that may also enable attacks"* — Okta is on the Glasswing partner list with PANW, Cisco, CRWD, Cloudflare, Rubrik, Netskope (Citrini, 2026-07-20).
  - **The two cleanest demand accelerations in the cyber group are on Okta's tape:** NRR turn after four years (Q1) and cRPO +12% → +14% inside one quarter (Q2), with new products at 30% of bookings and a 40% ACV uplift. MS's relative call: *"more catalysts for PANW, S, OKTA vs. CRWD, SAIL"*; **OW, PT $180** (MS, 2026-08-21). Trailing 3-month move **+45%** vs PANW +38%, large-cap software +22% (same note).
  - **Neutrality is a product feature.** *"Managing and securing an autonomous workforce requires a neutral, independent identity layer that others can't provide"* — customers deploy agents across OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Salesforce and open-source frameworks and pick Okta as *"a no regrets move"* rather than being *"all AgentCore, or… all Agent[force]"* (McKinnon, Q1 FY27). Anthropic itself named Okta the **first identity provider for enterprise-managed auth on MCP connectors** (McKinnon, Q2 FY27).
  - **Federal + customer identity are under-appreciated legs** — IL5, FedRAMP AI-Agents Core, OCI's civilian+DoD certification where Auth0 has none (Tighe via UBS, 2026-08-27).
  - **Sell-side stance:** MS OW $180 (08-21) · JPM Essex OW $120 (05-29) · Barclays EW $120 (05-29) · DB Buy (05-29) · Jefferies "2H Best Ideas" (Gallo) · UBS Boyd positive (*"a more controversial bullish call"*, per Keirstead 08-27). BBG: 38/9/0.
- **Bear:**
  - **Okta is the archetype of the cohort MS says is structurally squeezed.** Hyperscalers are *"an overhang for smaller best-of-breed players"*; **MSFT Entra holds ~31% of IAM**; **OKTA was losing share to platforms in MS's own CIO survey** even as MS kept it OW on valuation (~4x 27e EV/Sales, ~15x 27e EV/FCF at the time) (MS "Forecast as Large Clouds Move In", 2026-01-26). The buying-process tell: *"when CISO organizations report to the CFO, the 'included' value proposition is hard to forego."*
  - **The largest cyber platform is now inside Okta's segment.** [[PANW]] paid $21.1bn for CyberArk and reports ~1,000 cross-org engagements integrating detection with identity (BTG expert relaying PANW, 2026-06-18); [[CRWD]] launched an **Agentic Identity Provider** at Fal.Con (2026-09-03). Kelleher: *"we're not seeing any material change in the competitive behavior in our transactions yet"* (Q4 FY26) — *yet* is the operative word.
  - **Weak AI-security scorecard.** MS's proprietary AI Security Tracker (DSPM, non-human identity, AppSec testing, CNAPP, MDR): **OKTA 1.5/5** vs PANW 4.5, CRWD 4.5, MSFT 4.0 — the same score as SentinelOne and Tenable (MS "Revisiting Cybersecurity 101", 2026-03-20).
  - **Seats.** *"The big wrinkle is seats… this is a very seat-based model versus SailPoint has more of like an ELA. How do we get monetization out of this? How do we get a situation where the seats isn't serving as an offset?"* — Wells Fargo prefers SAIL over OKTA because *"if both win in the AI world, it's going to show up earlier and more significantly for SailPoint"* (WFC software-conference recap, 2026-04-13). Management's own Q1 answer — agents priced as a *user uplift*, per-agent pricing deferred — confirms the model has not moved.
  - **"A special quarter", guided "closer to the pin".** After a record 2Q and a +20% print, the CFO reverted to conservative framing; the bar has moved faster than the guide philosophy (UBS, 2026-08-27). Startups are winning early evaluations in new categories generally (CSO survey, investor webinar 2026-08-20) — McKinnon's counter is that the agent-identity startups are *"pivots… quite immature… end-to-end"* and CISOs *"sure don't want to have to buy five of them"* (Q2 FY27).
  - **Growth is still ~10%.** The whole re-rating rests on a 2-point acceleration from a 9% base; FY27 guide is +10–11% *including* the deliberate services drag. A $3.2bn business needs the AI SKUs to be *"accretive to growth for FY 2028, FY 2029"* (Tighe, Q4 FY26) — i.e. the bull case is dated two fiscal years out.
- **Where the sell-side/buy-side stands:** Constructive and getting more so — MS moved OKTA into its preferred trio with PANW and S (08-21); no Sell on the BBG tape. The debate is not direction but **conversion**: whether *"record pipeline"* for Okta/Auth0 for AI Agents turns into bookings before PANW/CyberArk, CRWD and MSFT bundle agent identity into platforms customers already own. ## Risks
- **Platform bundling / hyperscaler encroachment** — MSFT Entra ~31% IAM share; MS's January note names smaller best-of-breed as the structurally challenged cohort and records Okta share loss in its CIO survey (MS, 2026-01-26).
- **Consolidator entry** — PANW/CyberArk, CRWD Agentic IdP; Okta's 10-K lists Palo Alto Networks among companies it both partners and competes with (FY26 10-K, 2026-03-05).
- **Seat-based monetisation** in an era where agents may replace seats; per-agent pricing undefined (McKinnon, Q1 FY27; WFC, 2026-04-13).
- **Execution on conversion** — record pipeline vs *"how can we execute on turning this pipeline into real dollars"* (McKinnon).
- **Guide/expectations mismatch** — conservative *"closer to the pin"* philosophy against a stock +45% in three months (MS 08-21; UBS 08-27).
- **Security-vendor breach risk** — the 2022–23 support-system incidents are the reference; as an identity provider Okta is a high-value target (10-K risk factors).
- **Revenue-basis drag** — ~1pt FY27 revenue and ~1pt FCF margin from the deliberate services shift and lower interest income (Tighe).
