## Snapshot
Palantir builds AI-powered data-integration and decision-intelligence platforms for government and commercial customers, anchored by Foundry (commercial) and Gotham (government). The bull thesis centers on Palantir's ontology layer — a proprietary data model brokers assert AI labs cannot effectively replicate — marketed as an "action engine" with a claimed 5-year moat. AIPCon (its customer AI conference) has been the primary narrative catalyst in May–June 2026. The stock hit a 12-month relative low in mid-May 2026 (~$146, down from ~$207 in Nov 2025) before recovering through June. Consensus is overwhelmingly constructive on momentum but split on valuation.

## Risks
- Valuation / multiple compression in an AI-software-ROI debate.
- Competitive-encroachment fears from AI labs into the data/ontology layer.
- HF crowding (concentrated AI-software allocations); disclosed short interest.
- Government-revenue and contract-timing lumpiness.
- **🆕 Capability risk to the DELIVERY model, distinct from the competitive risk already listed above:** if continual learning works, the FDE role "narrows to cold start — connecting systems, scoping permissions, and building the evaluation loop," deployment goes "from a team per client for six months to a few weeks of cold start plus model self-learning," and **marginal deployment cost falls sharply** (FUNDA "Deep|LLM: RSI Is the Most Important Variable, and Compute Is the Deepest Moat", 2026-08-05). ⚠️ **This is not the "AI labs copy the ontology" risk the page already carries — it is the risk that the services intensity Palantir monetises stops being scarce for reasons that have nothing to do with any competitor's product. It is on a capability clock (FUNDA puts continual learning as "the acknowledged core research focus today," ahead of full RSI on its roadmap), and the first place it would surface is gross margin and deployment mix, which is the same soft spot the Q2 print opened (GM 86.3% vs 86.9% expected; "lighter FCF" per MS desk, 08-04).** Offsetting, from the same source: a sharply lower cost-to-deploy also opens "the long tail of enterprises that were previously uneconomic to serve," and the deployment experience accumulated across client sites is itself the training data for autonomous deployment — so the same mechanism can expand the TAM and reprice the delivery margin at once. (FUNDA, 2026-08-05)
