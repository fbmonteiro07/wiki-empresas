01 · THE TWO PHASES

Prefill is compute, decode is memory

Prefill runs every prompt token at once and is compute-bound; decode makes one token at a time and is memory-bound, because every new token re-reads the whole KV cache plus the active weights from HBM. Bernstein, 2026-08-28 · Stratechery, 2026-05-11 The best decode batch is only ~1-5 per sequence slot. SemiAnalysis · Bennett, 2026-09-21

02 · WHY ONE RACK

Experts are spread, tokens hop

>95% of an MoE’s weight memory sits in routed experts, which “divide naturally among sockets”: ~15,000 experts, 256 per layer, so each of 72 GPUs holds 3-4 per layer and there is “a lot of all-to-all”. SemiAnalysis · Bennett, 2026-09-21 GB200 NVL72 runs a 64-GPU all-to-all 18x faster than 64 H100s; H100 utilisation on DeepSeek is 16.6% vs 54.5% on a dense model. SemiAnalysis, 2025-08-20

03 · WHY 576

Range, not speed

Local HBM is ~21-22 TB/s vs NVLink 6 at ~3.6 TB/s: “what NPO changes is not SPEED but RANGE”. DAMNANG, 2026-08-10 The per-GPU HBM cut in Rubin Ultra “is absorbed by a scale-up domain that ties 576 of them together”. DAMNANG, 2026-09-04 Scale-up is ~10% of system cost and buys room for 10-20T-parameter models or 2-4M-token contexts. SemiAnalysis (Jalapeño), 2026-08-25

The bandwidth ladder a token climbs

Per GPU, one direction, Rubin generation: FundaAI, 2026-09-01 (HBM4 local 176 Tb/s · NVLink scale-up 14.4 Tb/s · scale-out 1.6 Tb/s). NVLink 6 = 3.6 TB/s both ways = 14.4 Tb/s one way (unit trap flagged on NVDA page). Bars are log-scaled.

01 · THE PRODUCT

What an EML is

An electro-absorption modulated laser: a DFB laser (grating-defined single wavelength) and an electro-absorption modulator on the same InP chip. The DSP’s electrical signal drives the modulator, which switches the laser light on and off at 100G or 200G per lane. 200G per lane is “about 2x ASP” with “very little competition… Broadcom being the other main supplier”. LITE @ DB conference, 2026-08-27

02 · THE CONSTRAINT

Substrate and epi, not demand

Lasers need sulfur-doped InP, which yields worse than the iron-doped InP used for photodetectors. LITE IR, 2026-09-11 AXT ~40% / Sumitomo ~40% / JX ~10% of substrates, on allocation for “>18 months”; third price hike this year, +10% more in 4Q26. AXT, 2026-08-31; UDN via Jefferies, 08-17 Aixtron has ~90-100% of InP MOCVD, slots reserved into 2027. AIXA Q3/25 call; Jefferies, 2026-06-19

03 · THE MIX SHIFT

EML share falls as 1.6T rises

EML-based transceivers drop from 70-80% at 800G to 40-50% at 1.6T as silicon photonics with CW lasers gains. LITE, 2026-08-27 So the same fabs are qualifying CW flows: ~15% of output to CW “given strong pricing” (Citi, 09-22) vs “doing it as a favour” (Jefferies, 09-18) disputed; JPM sees 10-20% of chip output in 2027 (08-14). NVIDIA’s ELS is ~$425 with 8 chips. LITE IR, 2026-09-11
