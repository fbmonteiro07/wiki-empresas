To estimate annual purchases: closing capacity − opening capacity + retirements − net capacity transfers into the service + change in uninstalled inventory. That requires a deployment schedule and reuse assumptions. Capacity is not consumed again each year, and bytes written are not installed NAND capacity.

Logical VM isolation does not establish physical resource reservation. Shared model inference, HBM, host overhead, storage tiering, OS/database/log copies and flash overprovisioning need separate treatment. This model does not establish incremental demand beyond existing forecasts or directly forecast supplier revenue.
