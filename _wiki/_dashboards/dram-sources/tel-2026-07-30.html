## Business environment [official transcript, Kawai]

- **WFE market outlook raised from the April view:** **CY2026 ≥ $150bn** and **CY2027 ≥ $190bn** — **+20%+ YoY in both years**. (April guide was $150-170bn/yr across CY2026-27.)
- **"WFE for AI data centers are growing to exceed 50% of total."** From CY2023 — which TEL calls "the inaugural year of AI" — through CY2027, sales to AI data centres are expected to **grow by five times or more**.
- AI data centres need a broad device range — **"not only GPU and HBM but also DDR, CPU and 3DNAND"** — and investment plans for advanced devices are accelerating.
- **Technology roadmap, all within ~2 years:** CPU/GPU 2-3nm → beyond 2nm **+ backside PDN**; **DRAM 1c → 1d**; **HBM stack 12hi → 16hi**; **NAND 2yy-3xxL → 4xxL**. Fab-space limitation was the conventional bottleneck; "quite a few large-scale fabs to manufacture leading-edge semiconductors are now being constructed."

## Medium-term plan status (targets to March 2027)
| Target | Status |
|---|---|
| Net sales ≥ ¥3tn | **On Track** |
| OPM ≥ 35% | **In Progress** (challenging) |
| ROE ≥ 30% | **On Track** |

- Kawai: with H1 above ¥1.6tn and stronger H2 inquiries, TEL is "getting more confident to achieve 3 trillion yen or more." ROE 30%+ expected on higher net income and capital-efficiency improvement.
- **The OPM target is the one that slips.** Kawai names the causes explicitly: export controls from US-China trade conflict, tariffs, Middle East conflicts and the prolonged Russia-Ukraine war, global inflation and FX — "these emerging developments were not expected when we set up the Medium-term Management Plan." But **operating profit of ¥1tn is "within reach"** (Q&A A6 confirms: for FY2027).

### Five profitability initiatives (new framing this quarter)
1. Next-generation systems with higher added value; 2. productivity gains through system upgrades; 3. AI- and robotics-enabled service + **Epsira** (the digital field-productivity infrastructure announced Dec-2025; **expanded NVIDIA collaboration announced July 2026** for agentic-AI/robotics solutions); 4. **faster equipment start-up** (TEL ships **4,000-6,000 units/yr**; next-gen equipment designed to halve setup time); 5. **pricing optimisation** against inflation.
- **Goal: gross margin ≥50% "within around two years to come."**

## Q&A — 2026-07-30 briefing [Q&A — summarized by issuer, document dated 2026-08-03]

> TEL's own note: "The content above is a summary of the Q&A session." Questioners not identified. **Q14 and Q15 were received in text during the briefing and left unanswered on the call due to time constraints** — TEL answered them in this document.

**Q1 — WFE growth rates by application, and H1-to-H2 shape?**
CY2025 split ~**65% logic / 35% memory**; CY2026 expected **60% logic / 40% memory**. No material FY-vs-CY difference. **CY2026 $150bn+ breakdown: DRAM low-$40bn, NAND mid-teens $bn, logic mid-$80bn, WLP ~$10bn.** Growth from CY2025: **DRAM +30%+, NAND +40%+, logic +15%+.** CY2026→CY2027 ~+20% overall with no major mix change. "The semiconductor market is being driven by agentic AI."

**Q2 — Is "$150bn+" a bearish framing, a capacity constraint, or an H2 risk flag?**
Neither. TEL says "$150 billion+" **because there is sufficient upside in growth potential** — the figure reflects a view of global equipment-maker capacity and parts/component supply. On CY2027, "$190bn+" means **at least** $190bn: **"We are aware that some in the capital market expect the CY2027 WFE market to reach $200 billion, and we feel that there is nothing particularly wrong with that interpretation."**

**Q3 — Timeline and magnitude of the five profitability initiatives?**
1 & 2: high-value-added next-gen tools and upgrades on a **two-to-three-year cycle**; TEL's broad lineup lets it propose both scaling and advanced packaging, with pricing set against delivered productivity value. 3: AI-based solutions and infrastructure to lift service value — higher yields (fewer defects/particles), longer maintenance intervals, shorter downtime. 4: TEL **ships 4,000-6,000 units/yr**; improving start-up efficiency from delivery to acceptance cuts time and cost materially, with next-gen equipment designed to **halve setup time**. 5: pricing optimisation against inflation and ramp costs — **"an issue which we need to address in the near term."**

**Q4 — Progress on price optimisation; does it hit H2 FY2027? Current models too?**
Costs have been affected by inflation and production increases. **"Customers already understand the importance of price optimization, including for current models."** Contributions begin **H2 FY2027, with effects becoming visible in Q4**.

**Q5 — Why do implied Q2 GPM and OPM fall vs Q1?**
Product mix plus **increases in fixed costs such as labour expenses**.

**Q6 — Is the ¥1tn operating profit for FY2027 or later?**
**"It is for FY2027."**

**Q7 — With CY2027 WFE +20%+ and price optimisation, is FY2028 a big revenue-up year?**
Revenue: "we are in a situation where strong growth can be expected." Profitability to improve through the five initiatives.

**Q8 — Can the 50% GPM target be hit on a quarterly basis in FY2028?**
**"We are carrying out activities with a focus on achieving this target in early FY2028."**

**Q9 — Does operating leverage take FY2028 OPM near the 35% target? What OPM in the next mid-term plan?**
**35% OPM "is a target that we are aiming for in the near term. In the next Medium-term Management Plan, we will aim beyond that."** Pursuing world-class profitability via growth in existing businesses, efficiency investment, and SAM expansion by combining existing technologies.

**Q10 — TEL aims to outgrow WFE, but share has been declining. Why can it expand now?**
**"The decline in market share over the past few years has been driven primarily by foreign exchange fluctuations."** TEL will regain share by **optimising pricing** for FX and inflation together.

**Q11 — Key drivers of TEL outgrowing the market in FY2027 and FY2028?**
AI-server demand strong and TEL is concentrated in leading-edge domains. **Coater/developer share >90%**, growing with lithography overall (EUV, immersion, KrF). In etch, a strong position in **capacitor etch** as DRAM grows; the **interconnect-process five-year cumulative target moves from the ¥500bn+ guided in October 2025 to ¥1tn**; plus share-expansion opportunity in **cryogenic etch for 400-layer NAND entering mass production in CY2027**. In advanced packaging, bonders/debonders to grow significantly and **probers are seeing "an extraordinary number of inquiries."**

**Q12 — If WFE hits $250bn CY2027 / $300bn CY2028, can TEL supply enough?** *(the capacity-ceiling question)*
**Customers have been providing investment plans about two years ahead.** Strength lies in steady preparation with TEL's Japan supply chain. **"While we cannot say that we currently possess the production capacity to meet a market scale of $300 billion,"** TEL is raising productivity via smart fabs starting with Miyagi to be able to respond.

**Q13 — How much does Miyagi change production capacity?**
**Etch system production capacity to roughly 3x the current level**, ultimately; TEL intends to accelerate toward maximum capacity in line with market conditions.

**Q14 — Why can coater/developer grow 50% YoY, far ahead of the exposure-system market it docks to?** *(answered in writing)*
**Sales volume matters more to coater/developer revenue than the type of light source.** FY2027 growth is driven by a wide demand range — not only EUV but immersion, KrF and packaging. There may also be **a slight timing discrepancy between exposure-system and coater/developer sales recognition**.

**Q15 — Why is Q2 Field Solutions guided slightly down vs Q1?** *(answered in writing)*
Fab utilisation for leading-edge applications is **extremely high** and parts/services sales are expected to increase steadily. **Used equipment and modifications have short lead times and are difficult to forecast**, so TEL presents only figures it considers reliable at this point.
