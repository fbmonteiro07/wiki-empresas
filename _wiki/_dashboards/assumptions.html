Every figure must carry (a) its rack denominator (racks/GW and the kW/rack behind it), (b) its GW basis (compute-TDP / IT-load vs facility), and (c) whether it is logic-only (foundry) or all-wafer (logic + DRAM + NAND).

Canonical: Rack denominator for VR200 is FIRM at ~4,440-4,545 racks/GW on an IT-load/compute-TDP basis — TrendForce ~225kW/rack (2026-06-28) and Bernstein 220kW compute-TDP (2026-07-20) agree independently. On that denominator, 1 GW of Vera Rubin ≈ 230-295k total 300mm wafers: DRAM 141-205k (house bottom-up; central ~155-186k) and foundry logic 25-30k (house bottom-up) vs Patel's 61k. The DRAM leg is well-anchored; the LOGIC leg is the live dispute — Patel's 55k N3 + 6k N5 is ~2x a die-area bottom-up of the NVL144 rack BOM and ~2.9x Fubon's Blackwell card (33k TSMC wafers/GW at 7,200 NVL72 racks = 4.6 wafers/rack).

⚠️ Scope: THREE traps. (1) Rack denominator: Blackwell GB300 ~150kW => 7,200 racks/GW (Fubon) vs VR200 ~220-225kW => ~4,440-4,545 racks/GW vs Redburn's FACILITY-GW frame (PUE 1.15, 90% to GPU racks, 250kW/rack) => 3,130 racks/GW. Wafers/GW scales inversely with kW/rack. (2) JPM's VR200 'rack design power' of 330-444kW is NAMEPLATE, not draw — do not build a rack count on it. (3) logic-only vs all-wafer: Fubon's 33k is foundry wafers only; Bernstein's 65 wafers/rack and the AlphaSense 350k are all-wafer.
