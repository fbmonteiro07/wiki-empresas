Explore the cell

Capacitor · stores chargeWord line / gate · controls accessBit line · reads / writesIllustrative conduction pathChannel / substrate

The capacitor is already vertical in both cells. The architectural change shown here is the access transistor. A vertical transistor is distinct from stacking many DRAM cell tiers.

The gate controls the channel through a dielectric. Charge does not flow through the gate. Geometry follows a simplified VCT concept, not a vendor mask layout. [1]

Vertical-channel footprint
4F² conceptually 2F × 2F

At the same F, the architecture occupies less cell area. This alone does not specify whole-die size. [1]

Two routes to a denser DRAM array

What changes for EUV and DUV?

Patterning routeWhat survives the transitionWhat remains uncertain
DUV immersionRemains in the process mix. Changing transistor geometry does not make every layer require EUV.Layer selection and multipatterning loops depend on the chosen process. ASML expects immersion intensity to rise. [2]
Low-NA EUVStill used for critical patterns; can replace complex DUV multipatterning.First-node exposure intensity is disputed: UBS allows a pause; ASML expects an increase. [2,3]
High-NA EUVA separate patterning decision, not an automatic consequence of a vertical transistor.UBS's September checks associate staying at 6F² with a stronger case for earlier High-NA. Later 4F² shrink also brings it back into consideration. [3,4]

Interpretation: lithography intensity is not the same metric as scanner shipments. Exposure requirements, wafer starts, dose, availability and throughput all matter. No equipment-unit forecast is implied.

Evidence, not a blended consensus

The architectural mechanism is clear. The equipment impact is contested.
