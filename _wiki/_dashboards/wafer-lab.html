Cell architecture

Maker

A DRAM cell is one transistor and one capacitor. The capacitor is the tall pillar; shrinking the node packs the pillars closer while keeping their charge, so the etch gets deeper and narrower. The vertical-channel transistor (4F²) puts the transistor directly under the capacitor.
