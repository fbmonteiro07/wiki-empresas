2 · Send it to an output

An OCS is a permutation: every input is lit to exactly one output. Re-pointing one fibre swaps it with whichever input held that output. Two tilting MEMS mirrors steer each beam.

Move the optics toward the chip

PluggableNPOCPO
Copper loses signal fast at 200G per lane. The shorter the copper between the switch ASIC and the optics, the less power is spent pushing electrons, and the DSP that cleans up a long trace can go.
01 · COLLIMATE

Fibre in, beam out

Each input fibre ends in a tiny lens that turns the light into a free-space beam. From here until the output fibre, the signal never becomes electrical.

02 · STEER

Two mirrors, two tilts

The beam hits its own MEMS mirror, which tilts to aim at the right mirror on the second array; that one tilts to aim down the chosen output fibre. Re-pointing = re-tilting. No O-E-O conversion, so no transceivers inside the switch: the power and latency of a packet-switch hop disappear. Lumentum mgmt via MS, 2026-05-07

03 · THE CATCH

Loss, and circuits not packets

Every pass through an OCS costs light (~2 dB Palomar; 1.5–3 dB LITE R300), which eats the link budget and pulls coherent-lite optics forward. It also reconnects fibres rather than routing packets, so it fits links whose pattern changes slowly: TPU torus wrap-arounds, failover, and the spine. FUNDA, 2026-08-12 / 08-19

01 · THE PROBLEM

Copper runs out of reach

A front-panel pluggable sits 15–30 cm from the ASIC, so each lane needs a long-reach SerDes plus a DSP to clean the signal. Copper reach falls from ~10 m at 800G to ~2 m at 1.6T. SemiAnalysis CPO Book, 2026-01-01 · LITE @ DB, 2026-08-27

02 · THE FIX

Put the light next to the silicon

CPO mounts optical engines (photonic chip + driver) on the switch package and brings fibre straight to them through a fibre-attach unit. The DSP goes, cutting optics energy by more than 50% (some target 80%). The laser moves out to a hot-swappable external laser source (ELS) at the front panel. SemiAnalysis CPO Book, 2026-01-01

03 · THE CATCH

Yield, service, timing

Fibre-attach yield is the gate (FOCI ~30%, others 50–60%; NVDA wants 70–80% before June 2027), a failed engine means opening the chassis, and at cluster level the saving is only ~2%. NPO keeps the engine socketable as a middle step. Fubon · Shang, 2026-09-26 · SemiAnalysis CPO Book

01 · SCALE-UP

Make many GPUs act as one

NVLink ties GPUs into one domain. Inside an NVL72 rack that is copper. NVDA's next step joins racks: "the NV576 Rubin Ultra in 2027 may use CPO switch trays to connect eight NVL72 server racks". MS · Charlie Chan, 2026-09-06 Copper stays inside the rack; the new layer between racks is optical, CPO or NPO undecided. Fubon · Shang via Jefferies desk, 2026-09-26

02 · SCALE-OUT

Join the domains into a cluster

Every GPU tray has NICs; each NIC port needs a transceiver to reach a leaf switch, and every leaf-to-spine link needs one at both ends. That is why AI transceivers run ~180M units in C27 (90M 800G + 80M+ 1.6T) and ~7x per XPU by C28, while TPUs sit at 1.5x. Jefferies · Curtis, 2026-09-24

03 · WHERE NPO FITS

The socketable middle step

CPO lands in scale-out switches first (Quantum-X / Spectrum-X Photonics; COHR sees the Dec-26 quarter). Scale-up optics timing is the open fight: COHR 2H CY27 for scale-up CPO + NPO, four houses at ECOC see NPO volume in 2028, Jefferies C29. Innolight says NPO; the Taiwan chain says CPO. COHR via JPM, 09-24/25 · ECOC 09-23/24 · Jefferies 09-24 · Fubon 09-26

The open debates

When does CPO ramp? disputed

COHR says scale-up CPO in 2H CY27. Four houses at ECOC agree NPO volume lands in 2028. Jefferies says 2029 (Curtis, 2026-09-24); Barclays says "turn of the decade (if not later)" (O'Malley, 2026-09-23). TSMC's COUPE inflection is "already a year late" (Susquehanna, 2026-09-09).

How much content per switch? disputed

Redburn implies ~$19 per CW laser chip and $4.8–6.4k of non-laser optics per switch; the SemiAnalysis CPO Book implies ~$82 per chip and ~$36k. They are 4–7x apart. COHR's "up to $15,000 per system" (2026-09-21) argues against the low end, but has basis traps.

Is the OCS TAM circular?

Redburn calibrated its OCS model to LITE/COHR disclosures, so its Google-derived $1.27bn 2027 figure matching LITE's >$1bn is not independent confirmation. The non-circular part points the TAM up: Google would need to buy ~40%+ of its OCS outside, vs Redburn's 25% (house bridge, 2026-08-17).

Who wins the value?

Follow the light source: an ELS is common to both CPO and NPO, so "track ELS, not the optical engine" (LITE @ DB, 2026-08-27). Transceiver assemblers lose leverage, since customers find it "much easier to squeeze" them than switch vendors (SemiAnalysis CPO Book). Gross margins: LITE 50.4% > COHR 40.2% > Fabrinet 12.2% (Q4FY26 prints).

Is CPO reliable enough?

Meta/AVGO measured 2.6M device-hours MTBF vs 0.5–1M for pluggables, but the "15M hours" are port-hours across only 15 switches, and one failure takes out 64 × 800G ports. Google is "most hesitant" to adopt (SemiAnalysis CPO Book).

MEMS vs liquid crystal in OCS

Lumentum: MEMS is "the lowest loss" and the only high-volume approach; piezo was dropped (LITE mgmt to Capstone, 2026-09-14). Coherent ships liquid-crystal OCS (320×320 per desk commentary, 2026-09-14, not research). A corpus rumour says Google rejected COHR's LC-OCS on insertion loss rumour.
