Start here · the same wafer returns to lithography many times

A wafer is the canvas. EUV patterns selected levels on it.

WaferThe silicon disk carrying many repeated chip designs (dies). Building more layers on it does not create more wafers.
Physical film / layerA material added or formed on the wafer: insulator, metal, semiconductor or temporary resist. Many films do not need their own lithography step.
Patterned level / “EUV layer”A device or interconnect pattern defined using lithography. “EUV layers” usually means levels assigned to EUV, not every physical film in the chip. Check each source’s counting convention.
Exposure passA patterning pass using a mask. One final level may require multiple passes. Within a pass, the scanner exposes successive fields across the same wafer.

How this creates demand: more wafers means more disks to process. More EUV-patterned levels means more EUV work on each disk. More passes on one level also adds work. Higher dose can take longer, while scanner productivity improvements can offset these pressures. These are separate drivers—not interchangeable meanings of “more EUV layers.”

Time axis · production milestones, not an equally spaced scale

How the lithography mix evolved

2018N7

Original N7 uses DUV. Fine patterns rely on resolution enhancement and multipatterning where needed.

2019 · bridgeN7+

TSMC’s first high-volume EUV process. Do not label original N7 as EUV simply because N7+ uses it.

2020 · bridgeN5

EUV becomes part of the advanced-node platform. Less critical patterns still use DUV.

2022N3

EUV is central to critical patterning. The transistor remains a FinFET. N3 variants need not have identical mask flows.

Q4 2025N2

GAA nanosheets replace fins. Selective etch and conformal films become more demanding; EUV layer growth must be established separately.

One pattern is not the whole wafer

Why EUV can grow while patterning steps disappear

DUV route · schematic split pattern

Print subpattern A→Transfer / intermediate steps→Print subpattern B→Combine pattern

Separate simpler patterns can form a denser final pattern. Another route uses deposited spacers and etch to multiply pitch; a spacer cycle is not an exposure. Extra alignment and process-control work can accumulate.

EUV route · selected critical pattern

Print finer pattern→Develop→Transfer / etch

Shorter wavelength enables finer optical patterning. A qualifying EUV exposure can replace a more elaborate DUV sequence. At tighter pitches, EUV can itself need multipatterning; do not assume it for every N2 layer.

[3, 4] ASML technology and patterning explanations. The animation uses two DUV subpatterns versus one EUV pattern only to explain the mechanism, not as a disclosed N7/N3/N2 mask recipe or an exposure-ratio estimate.

Evidence boundary: N2 is not “N3 plus more of everything”

What is established

TSMC identifies N7 and N3 as FinFET platforms and N2 as its first-generation nanosheet platform. ASML describes a mixed EUV / DUV fleet, chosen by layer requirements. Lam and ASM explain the selective-removal and conformal-film challenges of GAA. [1, 3–6]

High-NA is not assumed for TSMC N2. A scanner advertised for a node class does not establish a particular foundry’s production insertion. Backside power is also not included as a baseline N2 feature in this sketch.

The wiki’s counterweight to a steep N3 → N2 arrow

Arete / Jim Fontanelli, 21 Jul 2026, as summarized in the ASML wiki, places the next material EUV-layer step-up after N3 at A14. This is broker analysis, not a TSMC-disclosed mask list. Fubon’s September estimate points to a larger N2 step-up; both views are now preserved in the source selector and ledger.

UBS / Gaudois et al., 22 Sep 2026, via the same wiki, does not put High-NA into TSMC’s A14 base case. Neither view is converted into tool demand here. ASML research page.
