Methodology & fidelity
  • Chain: total AI capex (row 6) → split into chips / networking+CPU / hard assets (rows 7–9, share rules rows 13–14) → NVDA chips revenue = share of chips capex (row 53) → + networking attach (25% of chips revenue from CY27) + other segments (frozen at CY28 $31.6bn, linked to the NVDA model in the workbook) → gross margin fade → opex % → 18% tax → earnings × payout = distribution → NPV at Ke.
  • Fidelity: the engine reproduces the saved sheet bit-for-bit on 14 checkpoints (NPV D85, value D88, P/E D90/E90, TV AA79, capex CY29/31/45, revenue CY29/45, earnings CY45, distribution CY26, EPS CY27/28). Base preset = the sheet as saved 27-Aug-2026.
  • Excel NPV convention preserved: =NPV(Ke, G79:AA79) discounts the CY26 distribution one full period (valuation point ≈ end-CY25) and the terminal value 21 periods — one period beyond the last cash flow. That is the sheet's own convention, kept as-is.
  • Terminal value: final-year (CY45) distribution at 100% payout × (1+g)/(Ke−g).
  • This is a distributed-earnings (dividend-style) DCF — no explicit reinvestment/working-capital below the payout ratio.
  • CY23–25 are fixed history. CY26–28 are anchored to the AI Model bottom-up (hyperscalers $1,425bn + others $1,488bn = $2,913bn total in CY28); the realization dial scales those three years and flows through to revenue — it is an extension, not a sheet cell (marked ext).
  • Bull / Bear presets are illustrative dial settings, not house calls.
  • The model of record remains Template DCF.xlsx on P:. This page never writes back to it.
