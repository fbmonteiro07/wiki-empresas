01 · THE PRODUCT

What an EML is

An electro-absorption modulated laser: a DFB laser (grating-defined single wavelength) and an electro-absorption modulator on the same InP chip. The DSP’s electrical signal drives the modulator, which switches the laser light on and off at 100G or 200G per lane. 200G per lane is “about 2x ASP” with “very little competition… Broadcom being the other main supplier”. LITE @ DB conference, 2026-08-27

02 · THE CONSTRAINT

Substrate and epi, not demand

Lasers need sulfur-doped InP, which yields worse than the iron-doped InP used for photodetectors. LITE IR, 2026-09-11 AXT ~40% / Sumitomo ~40% / JX ~10% of substrates, on allocation for “>18 months”; third price hike this year, +10% more in 4Q26. AXT, 2026-08-31; UDN via Jefferies, 08-17 Aixtron has ~90-100% of InP MOCVD, slots reserved into 2027. AIXA Q3/25 call; Jefferies, 2026-06-19

03 · THE MIX SHIFT

EML share falls as 1.6T rises

EML-based transceivers drop from 70-80% at 800G to 40-50% at 1.6T as silicon photonics with CW lasers gains. LITE, 2026-08-27 So the same fabs are qualifying CW flows: ~15% of output to CW “given strong pricing” (Citi, 09-22) vs “doing it as a favour” (Jefferies, 09-18) disputed; JPM sees 10-20% of chip output in 2027 (08-14). NVIDIA’s ELS is ~$425 with 8 chips. LITE IR, 2026-09-11
