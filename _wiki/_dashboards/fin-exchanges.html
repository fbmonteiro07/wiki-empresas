How to read it

A strong positive correlation means the year-on-year volume change mostly tracks the change in volatility, which supports the "volatility spike" case. Points in the upper-left quadrant (volume up while volatility fell year on year) support the structural case. A correlation on 21–33 monthly points is descriptive, not a forecast, and one market event can dominate it.

Method & sources

Monthly exchange statistics from public company releases and OCC. OCC counts cleared US listed-options contracts (equity + index), so exchange-group shares are of OCC volume. Cboe RPC is the company's rolling three-month average, reported with a one-month lag. ICE monthly releases give y/y % only. Industry ADV = OCC monthly total / Cboe US trading days (Capstone arithmetic).

  • OCC: exchange-volume report, market view, monthly. Refetched every run.
  • Cboe: monthly volume & RPC data sheet (latest file), also linked from cboe.com.
  • CME: monthly volume releases on PR Newswire (CME's own monthly-volume page refuses scripted requests). Latest: CME Group Reports Record October ADV of 30 Million Contracts, up 15% Year Over Year CME Group, the world's leading derivatives marketplace, today reported its a.
  • ICE: monthly statistics releases (PDF). Latest: Oct-26. Levels sit in ICE's Monthly Statistics Tracking spreadsheet, which refuses scripted requests.
  • Nasdaq: the monthly volume data sheet on ir.nasdaq.com refuses scripted requests; options share comes from OCC and the rest from the quarterly results on the NDAQ page.
  • Volatility: Cboe VIX and OVX daily history. MOVE (rates volatility) needs Bloomberg and is not shown.
  • Trading days: Cboe's published count where available; otherwise the NYSE holiday calendar. Disagreements: none.

Refresh: fetch_exchange_volumes.py (network, public sources) then build_fin_exchanges.py. The 18:15 chain rebuilds the page from the saved JSON.
