AI infrastructure financing: platforms, project bonds and chip leases

Blackstone, KKR, Apollo, BlackRock and Blue Owl now sit on both sides of the AI build-out: they own data-center platforms, lead chip and GPU financings, and sponsor the joint ventures behind Meta's campuses. The debate on the Financials theme is whether this becomes recurring, fee-paying capital or a run of episodic, concentrated deals. This page keeps each deal on its stated basis, keyed to the compute-deal timeline IDs, next to how the market prices the managers' project bonds in the official AI-credit workbook.
