The one thing to agree on before we start: this is multiple compression

Split the book by 4-week NTM EPS revision and the rotation is unambiguous:

Estimates UP, price DOWN (de-rating) ASML +17.1% est / −5.5% pxGOOG +10.7% / −1.9%TSM +9.6% / −7.7%GEV +4.6% / −6.4%
Estimates FLAT, price UP (re-rating) AAPL +0.4% est / +18.5% pxMETA −0.9% / +10.5%MSFT −0.1% / +5.1%NOW −0.9% / +4.7%

The fundamental record from last week backs the estimate side, not the price side: INTC beat and raised hard (rev $16.1bn vs ~$14.4bn, EPS $0.42 vs ~$0.22, stock +8–9%, 2026 capex raised to $18–20bn with a "significant increase" flagged for 2027); TSMC lifted its FY26 revenue guide to "slightly above 40%" and capex to $60–64bn; DISCO beat and raised; AMD raised all three CY30 TAMs at once. Nothing in that set says cut estimates.

The honest bear rebuttal: revisions are a lagging, sell-side-supplied series — they tell you what analysts did after the prints, not what the marginal buyer will pay. The 2026 SOX drawdown (~−23% at the 07-17 low) is still inside the historical median (−25%) and average (−29%) peak-to-trough, so "it's already washed out" is not established. And two funding facts got worse, not better, last week: Google posted its first negative FCF since going public (−$5.9bn), and Oracle sits −65% off its high with CDS at records. Multiple compression is what a rotation looks like on the way through, not proof that it is over.
