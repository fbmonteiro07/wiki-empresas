The Harder Three

Of the twelve DRAM construction steps, three get harder from 1a to 1d: more EUV layers, a narrower and deeper capacitor hole, a thinner high-k film on a taller wall. Walk through them in 3D, see whose tool is core at each one, and read the scoreboard the equipment map produces. Companion to The DRAM Process Lab (its equipment map is the one scored here).

06 · EXPOSE — MORE EUV LAYERS

The stencil gets drawn with EUV more often

EUV layers per DRAM node climb ~2 at 1a → 3 at 1b → 5 at 1c → 8 at 1d, into the low teens at 0b/0c; total DRAM EUV exposures 3.4 MWPM (2025) → 26.3 MWPM (2030), lifting DRAM from 26% to 44% of all EUV exposures. Bernstein · Dai, 2026-07-30 The variant ladder: 1a ~0-1 → 1b ~3-4 → 1c ~6-7 → 1d ~10 by about 2028. BofA ASML callback, 2026-04-15 ASML: “for any of the three DRAM customers we do expect for the next few nodes the number of EUV layers to continue to grow”; 1d “about 2028”. ASML management call, 2026-04-21

Core here: ASML (scanner, the only EUV source) and Tokyo Electron (coat / develop track, 91% world share). EUV does not drill the hole: more exposures do not mean more deposited material, and EUV can remove DUV multi-patterning steps.

07 · ETCH THE OPENING — NARROWER, DEEPER

The hole the capacitor lives in gets a higher aspect ratio

A narrow deep opening is harder to keep straight: bowing, incomplete etch and bottom clearing all get more demanding, and the mask has to survive longer (the Process Lab’s etch delta card; geometry qualitative). 4F² later makes it an etch story in the Z direction: “higher pillars… will bring about challenges for tilt/leaning that will require higher aspect ratio etching as well as conductor etching”. UBS · Gaudois / Arcuri, 4F² note, 2026-01-14

Core here: Lam (Flex dielectric etch, listed first in UBS’s key DRAM etch vendors with Tokyo Electron and AMAT) and Tokyo Electron (capacitor-etch PORs at all leading customers; FY27 etch guided +25-30%, >50% dielectric-etch share). TEL FY26 results, 2026-04-30; Kawai, Q1 FY27 call, 2026-07-30

09 · ADD THE DIELECTRIC — THINNER FILM, TALLER WALL

ALD has to coat the whole wall evenly

The high-k film is deposited by repeated surface reactions down a taller, narrower cup; pinholes or thickness variation mean leakage, and a thinner film on a taller wall leaves less margin (the Process Lab’s deposition delta card). Incomplete conformality in high-aspect-ratio ALD is a modelled failure mode, not a slogan. arXiv 2210.00749 (2022), via the Process Lab

Core here: Tokyo Electron (batch high-k capacitor deposition on its DRAM roadmap) TEL Investors’ Guide, 2025-05-09 and ASM International (ALD high-k / MIM capacitor portfolio; DRAM ALD and epi qualifications reported, with qualified-layer benefits expected in two to three years while growth today is capacity-led). ASM management, BofA-hosted call, 2026-08-20
