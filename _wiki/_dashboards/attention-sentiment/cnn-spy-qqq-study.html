Sources, alignment and limitations

CNN official methodology: seven equally weighted market indicators, including S&P 500 relative to its 125-day average and stocks versus bonds over 20 sessions. Official CNN historical endpoint. ETF returns: Bloomberg Desktop API, gross dividend-reinvested total-return indices.

Pearson and Spearman; primary predictive entry is next-session close, then +1/+5/+20/+60 ETF sessions. Same-day correlation is not predictive. Circular moving-block bootstrap, 500 repetitions, block=max(20,horizon). Separate years and 2021–2024 / 2025–2026 windows; no threshold optimization. One CNN date is missing (2026-03-12); it remains missing. The duplicated latest-day observation was resolved by timestamp. Historical UTC-midnight stamps are treated as date labels, not shifted to the preceding US date.

  • CNN is partly constructed from stock momentum, breadth, volatility and stocks-versus-bonds; same-day association is endogenous.
  • Retrospectively downloaded, potentially revised history; immutable publication-time CNN vintages are unavailable.
  • Current day excluded from the historical study even after regular close; latest CNN displayed separately.
  • Price-level correlation can be misleading because prices trend while the index is bounded. It is diagnostic only.
  • Overlapping forward returns reduce independent observations; block intervals and non-overlapping checks are provided.
  • Multiple horizons and regimes are exploratory; confidence intervals are not adjusted for multiple testing.
  • QQQ/SPY means the ratio of gross total-return indices (relative performance), not the P&L of a dollar-neutral long/short portfolio.
  • No transaction costs, trading strategy or causal claim. Sample starts February 2021 and excludes the 2020 crash.

Missing values are never filled with zero; current-day CNN is shown only as context. Local raw snapshots, date-level calculations and input hashes are retained for audit.
