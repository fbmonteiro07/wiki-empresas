## Snapshot

MSCI is a toll on the global investment process: index licences and asset-based fees on ETF and non-ETF assets linked to its benchmarks, plus subscription analytics (risk, factor models, portfolio tools), sustainability and climate data, and private-assets data (Private Capital Solutions, ex-Burgiss, and Real Assets). The thesis is that Index and private-assets subscriptions compound at or above high single digits while asset-based fees add market-linked upside. The open tests are whether the 2026 subscription acceleration holds through lumpy large deals, how much of asset-based-fee growth is market beta, fee-rate mix and BlackRock concentration, and whether management's choice to reinvest rather than expand margins, while buybacks are partly debt-funded, still compounds value per share. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm); [Henry Fernandez / Andy Wiechmann, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md); Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

**Segment split: Index and Private Capital Solutions carry the acceleration; Analytics revenue lags its sales; Sustainability and Climate net new sales turned negative.** Index revenue was $532.6m (+18.1%) at a 77.4% adjusted EBITDA margin and 97.1% retention. Analytics revenue grew only 4.8% (organic 5.2%) and its adjusted EBITDA margin fell to 44.9% from 49.4%, even as net new recurring sales rose 63.6% to $19.8m. Sustainability and Climate posted -$1.1m net new recurring sales and 91.0% retention (93.6% a year earlier). Private Assets revenue was $74.7m (+6.7%) with margin down to 24.8% from 27.6%. [Company release, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm). On the call, CFO Andy Wiechmann put Private Capital Solutions subscription Run Rate growth at 19% (16%+ in Q2), Real Assets organic subscription growth at 2%, Index hedge-fund subscription growth at 32%, asset-manager organic subscription growth at ~6% and LTM recurring net new sales at +26%. These are management figures from the call, not release tables. [Wiechmann, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md).

**Asset-based fees are volume-led with a falling fee rate and a concentrated client.** Q3 revenue from ETFs linked to MSCI equity indexes rose 28.2% and from non-ETF indexed funds 20.7%, driven by higher average AUM and partly offset by lower average basis-point fees. BlackRock was 11.8% of consolidated revenue and 19.8% of Index revenue in 9M 2026 (10.6% / 18.5% in 9M 2025). [Q3 10-Q, filed 2026-10-20](../MSCI/MSCI_10-Q_2026-10-20_0001408198-26-000056.html). Roughly three-fifths of the underlying AUM is in non-USD securities, so currency moves flow into asset-based fees in a way that the ex-FX figures do not remove. [Company release, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm). Fernandez cited "nearly $23 trillion" benchmarked to MSCI indexes (~$15tn active, ~$8tn indexed); this is a management estimate, not a reported line item. [Q3 call](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md).

**Costs and cash: spending is at the top of guidance, and FCF growth is modest this year.** Adjusted EBITDA expenses grew 12.8% (+13.4% ex-FX) while headcount was flat at 6,259. Management expects the top end of the expense ranges because of ~$20m from acquisitions, $15–20m of non-cash performance-option accruals and $10–15m of higher severance. Q3 FCF fell because cash interest payments were $50m+ higher. [Release](../MSCI/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm); [Wiechmann, Q3 call](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md). The FY2026 FCF guide ($1,485–1,545m) compares with $1,458.6m in FY2025. In January, management attributed the limited growth to ~$100m of higher cash taxes and a ~$90m step-up in cash interest after the 2025 bond issues. [Q4 2025 release, 2026-01-28](../MSCI/earnings/2026-01-28_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm); [Wiechmann, Q4 2025 call, 2026-01-28](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-01-28.md).

**Bull case — a reference-standard toll with new growth layers.** Indexes are models that clients cannot easily replace, and AI raises the value of proprietary benchmarks and datasets more than that of distribution layers. Index subscriptions are accelerating through hedge funds, traders and custom indexes. Private Capital Solutions is accelerating through asset owners, the UBS wealth-channel partnership and Vantager-based diligence tools. Asset-based fees capture passive/ETF growth wherever assets go. Management says AI shortens model-building from weeks to hours. Fernandez also argues that agentic distribution could take MSCI content from tens of thousands of users to hundreds of thousands; that is an aspiration, not a disclosed revenue stream. [Fernandez / Munari / Wiechmann, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/transcripts/MSCI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md); [Redburn research summary, 2026-06-18](../MSCI/commentary/2026-06-18_R-Co-US-Riser-155g-Info-Services-Report---SPGI-MSCI-Both-Buy-LSEG-MCO-MORN-VRSK-All-Neutral-FDS-Sell_16cf711e.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — beta, mix and reinvestment hide a slower core.** Asset-based fees ride equity markets and FX at a declining fee rate, with one client a fifth of Index revenue. Asset managers, the core client segment, grow ~6% and face secular pressure. Sustainability and Climate retention is falling. Analytics margins compressed in Q3. Management has explicitly ruled out targeting margin expansion, so subscription upside is reinvested rather than reaching EPS, and leverage-funded buybacks carry more of the per-share growth. On the Q3 call, MSCI said clients currently prefer its own applications over third-party LLM connectors; that is early evidence, not proof of immunity to AI disintermediation. [Company release and Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../MSCI/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit991earningsrelease-.htm); [Q3 10-Q](../MSCI/MSCI_10-Q_2026-10-20_0001408198-26-000056.html); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** a strong Q4 net new quarter with retention ≥95% and Analytics revenue catching up to its Run Rate; or, on the downside, a 2027 expense outlook that again absorbs subscription upside, a further step-down in the basis-point fee, or renewed Sustainability and Climate cancels after First Street. Compare index economics with S&P Dow Jones Indices inside [SPGI](SPGI.md) and analytics retention with Moody's Analytics in [MCO](MCO.md), on matched definitions only.
