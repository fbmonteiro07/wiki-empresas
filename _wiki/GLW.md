## Snapshot
Corning is a materials-science compounder (175 years old as of 2026) that has re-rated into an AI infrastructure name. Five reporting segments as of Q1 2026: **Optical Communications** (~$1.8B/qtr, the growth engine — datacenter fiber/cable/connectivity for AI plus carrier fiber-to-the-home/DCI), **Glass Innovations** (newly combined Display + Specialty Materials; Gorilla Glass, semiconductor/EUV optics), **Automotive** (emission control + auto glass), **Solar** (newly carved out — polysilicon/wafers/modules), and **Life Sciences + Emerging Growth**. The whole equity story now runs through the "Springboard" plan and the optical/AI ramp. Q1 2026 sales $4.35B; FY2025 sales $16.4B (Q4-2025 call, 2026-01-28).

## Risks
- **Adoption/timing risk** on scale-up + CPO (internal CPO-penetration range 10–50% by 2030) — the bull case is back-end-loaded. **Sharpened, and by the same MS analyst who wrote the constructive 07-13 scale-up primer: on the 07-28 preview webcast Marshall says her optical bias "has been maybe a little bit more cautious, just because of concerns around CPO timing… we don't lean on there being DELAYS in terms of what we've seen [in] CPO, but just the AMOUNT OF SCALE that we think that it will have is just a little bit LOWER IN 2028 versus where expectations had been. That's created a little bit of a breather on the space in general."** Read precisely: this is **not** a slip in the calendar (management's 2028 start and the primer's "meaningful adoption at Feynman" both stand) — it is a **downshift in the 2028 penetration rate**, which is exactly the variable the $10bn Photonics MAP and the FY28 EPS sensitivity grid (~$5.08-$5.97 across 4-20% scale-up × 0-20% scale-out CPO adoption) are most levered to. (Morgan Stanley webcast "2Q26 memory, Intel implications and ARM preview" · Meta Marshall, 2026-07-28)
- **Fiber commoditization:** a segment of investors sees fiber as a low-differentiation product, capping margin upside even as adoption rises (JPM bear tenet, 2025-12-15) — consistent with mgmt's reluctance to raise commodity fiber price.
- **Capital intensity:** with capacity constraints already showing, a significant capex ramp may be needed to deliver incremental capacity, pressuring the "grow into AI without a FCF dip" narrative (JPM, 2025-12-15).
- **Input/utility/single-supplier exposure:** raw materials, precious metals, energy/water and limited-source equipment; solar wafer ramp already hit by utility-build delays (10-K Item 1A risk factors, 2026-02-12; Weeks, Q1-2026).
- **China / trade restrictions** explicitly cited as a supply-chain and demand risk (10-K Item 1A, 2026-02-12). Preform sourcing also politically exposed — hyperscalers reluctant to use China-made preform (Jefferies Techknowledge, 2025-12-17).
- **Yen / FX:** Display USD net income depends on hedging in a weak-yen environment (hedged 2026–2030; Schlesinger, Q4-2025).
- **Cyclicality + customer concentration:** Display/auto/consumer-electronics demand swings; large LTAs concentrate optical exposure on a handful of hyperscalers + Nvidia.
- **Valuation:** ~30x CY2028 leaves little margin for an adoption-timing slip (Marshall, 2026-05-07).
- **⚠️🔴 EXPECTATION-SETTING / COMMUNICATION RISK — now conceded by management rather than merely alleged: Corning *"admitted to us that they were pretty sloppy on expectation setting"*, and the 3Q midpoint of $4.95bn was back-solved by splitting the difference between the June print and the ~$5bn 4Q figure implied by Springboard's $20bn exiting-CY26 run-rate — which mechanically implies a near-flat 4Q** (Wolfe Research "Q2 earnings-season takeaways call", 2026-08-14). ⚠️ **Second-hand: a broker's account of a private follow-up call, in no release, call or filing. Logged as a RISK because of what it costs even when fundamentals are fine — a >15% single-day drawdown on a beat-every-line quarter — and because the underlying practice is structural, not a one-off: while Springboard is updated *"only when there's a material change,"* quarterly guides will keep being interpolated from a potentially stale anchor.** ➜ **It is the one risk on this list that is entirely self-inflicted and entirely fixable, which is also why it is the one most likely to recur unaddressed.**
