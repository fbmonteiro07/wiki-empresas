## Snapshot
ASML is the sole supplier of EUV lithography and the dominant DUV/immersion vendor — the binding constraint of the leading-edge semiconductor supply chain. Revenue splits across system sales (EUV Low-NA + High-NA, plus non-EUV DUV/dry/immersion and Metrology & Inspection) and a high-margin recurring Installed Base Management (service + upgrades) business. FY25 net sales €32.7B: EUV €11.6B (48 systems, +39% y/y), DUV €12.0B (-6%), M&I €0.825B (+28%), IBM €8.2B (+26%); by segment Logic €16.1B (+22%), Memory €8.4B (-2%) (Q4/FY25, 2026-01-28). The franchise is a near-monopoly levered directly to the AI capex cycle via rising litho intensity in advanced Logic (3nm/2nm/A14) and DRAM (1c→1d EUV layer adoption). The installed base underpins a structural annuity: ASML "measures a machine's life in decades, not years," with >5,000 scanners running in fabs end-2022 and ~95% of the ~1,800 PAS 5500 DUV tools ever sold still in use; fab depreciation runs 5–7yr (TSMC 5yr, Micron 7yr, Intel extended to 8yr in 2023, cutting 2023 D&A ~$4.2B) but physical/economic life is 15–30+yr, so the field grows monotonically and IBM service+upgrade revenue compounds (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).

- **🆕 Bull — read-through only: the first physical measurement of what EUV denial is actually producing, and it is more DUV immersion per node, not less (SemiAnalysis / STEEL Team "Is SMIC N+3's Metal Pitch Smaller than Intel 18A's?", 2026-06-14).** ⚠️ **The report says nothing about ASML's own numbers** — it is a teardown of Huawei's Kirin 9030 on SMIC N+3, and everything below is inference from measured process detail. It is nonetheless the best independent read on file for the **China DUV-intensity debate** that sits under the ~20%-of-sales China line and the MATCH Act / immersion-ban wildcard. The finding: **without EUV, SMIC leans harder on DUV immersion, SAQP multi-patterning and DTCO** — reaching **TSMC N6-class logic density (113.4 vs 107.7 MTr/mm²)** but paying for it in complexity, efficiency and process control, so N+3 **"does not match N6 on process maturity or cost."** The measured cost of that route is explicit: M0 at 32.5nm "is below what a single DUV-defined spacer (SADP) can resolve, so SMIC cascades a second spacer step (SAQP)," and the cross-sections show the price (visibly re-entrant M0 trenches, a barrier-rich foot at the etch-stop, and a **liner-bottom blowout at the alumina etch-stop layer** that deviates from the big-three foundries) — whereas **TSMC N6 gets to comparable density with M0/M2/M3 at a relaxed ~40nm on SADP-class double patterning, needing no quadruple patterning anywhere.** **The forward-looking passage is the one that matters for DUV demand, verbatim:** *"Future nodes will be tougher. N+3 still had room to tighten local metals and reduce cell height and CGP. Further scaling without EUV leaves fewer levers. More aggressive multi-patterning adds masks and overlay error. SMIC can keep pushing DUV, but each step will get more expensive and less forgiving."* Read as tool demand: **each additional node of China-domestic scaling consumes disproportionately more immersion tool-time, more masks and tighter overlay** — a theoretical N+4 would need M2 into SAQP territory and M1 to ~36nm (also SAQP), and a theoretical **N+5 with backside contacts would additionally require entirely new backside-alignment, wafer-thinning, contact-reveal and backside-metallisation flows**. That is the mechanical reason the page's China resilience checks (FundaAI: ASML China revenue +20% in 2026, sustainable through 2027-28 on SMIC/Hua Hong quad-patterning) are structurally underpinned rather than a one-off restock, and it also explains the **N+2/N+3 process licensing to HLMC/Hua Hong at government direction** — capacity diffusion means more fabs buying the same DUV-intensive flow. **The one caveat that cuts the other way, and it is not small:** Huawei's answer to the litho ceiling is to route around it entirely via **τ (tau) scaling / LogicFolding** — 3D stacking of *active* logic, i.e. **buying density with packaging rather than with patterning** — and its own 2031 roadmap density jump to 295 MTr/mm² per package implies "either a third active layer, **partial EUV insertion** or aggressive planar DUV scaling." ## Installed-base economics (service / IBM annuity)
- **Depreciation ≠ end-of-life.** Fabs depreciate scanners over 5–7yr (TSMC 5yr; Micron 7yr; Intel moved 5→8yr in 2023, "better reflects demonstrated economic value," cutting D&A ~$4.2B that year) but tools physically run far longer (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).
- **DUV:** >97% production uptime (e.g. NXT:1980Di); proven 15–20+yr front-line life, 30–40yr total with refurbishment. ASML refurbishes ~30 systems/yr (~20–25 PAS 5500), and ~95% of ~1,800 PAS 5500 ever sold are still in fabs — a self-reinforcing service/upgrade base.
- **EUV:** early NXE (~2018) uptime 70–80%, now approaching 90%+ as the source/optics mature; collector mirror swapped ~annually. Official design-life target ~7yr, but expected effective life 15+yr (5–7yr leading-edge + ~a decade in trailing roles) as a refurb/secondary market develops late-2020s. Implication: each EUV cohort keeps generating IBM revenue long after the system sale, and the field never shrinks — the recurring-revenue floor under the cyclicality (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).
