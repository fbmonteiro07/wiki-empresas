## Snapshot
ASML is the sole supplier of EUV lithography and the dominant DUV/immersion vendor — the binding constraint of the leading-edge semiconductor supply chain. Revenue splits across system sales (EUV Low-NA + High-NA, plus non-EUV DUV/dry/immersion and Metrology & Inspection) and a high-margin recurring Installed Base Management (service + upgrades) business. FY25 net sales €32.7B: EUV €11.6B (48 systems, +39% y/y), DUV €12.0B (-6%), M&I €0.825B (+28%), IBM €8.2B (+26%); by segment Logic €16.1B (+22%), Memory €8.4B (-2%) (Q4/FY25, 2026-01-28). The franchise is a near-monopoly levered directly to the AI capex cycle via rising litho intensity in advanced Logic (3nm/2nm/A14) and DRAM (1c→1d EUV layer adoption). The installed base underpins a structural annuity: ASML "measures a machine's life in decades, not years," with >5,000 scanners running in fabs end-2022 and ~95% of the ~1,800 PAS 5500 DUV tools ever sold still in use; fab depreciation runs 5–7yr (TSMC 5yr, Micron 7yr, Intel extended to 8yr in 2023, cutting 2023 D&A ~$4.2B) but physical/economic life is 15–30+yr, so the field grows monotonically and IBM service+upgrade revenue compounds (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).

  - **Musk "has signalled a potential intent to use Free Electron Lasers (FEL) as the light source for powering ASML EUV systems in Terafab's major fab project in Texas."**

  - **🔴 THE ASML P&L MECHANISM, stated plainly: "If the project is successful, it could potentially REDUCE THE NUMBER OF EUV SYSTEMS REQUIRED, while also REDUCING THE ASP PER ASML SYSTEM, as it would not need the light source."** ⚠️ **Both legs of that hit ASML: fewer units AND lower ASP per unit. ## Installed-base economics (service / IBM annuity)
- **Depreciation ≠ end-of-life.** Fabs depreciate scanners over 5–7yr (TSMC 5yr; Micron 7yr; Intel moved 5→8yr in 2023, "better reflects demonstrated economic value," cutting D&A ~$4.2B that year) but tools physically run far longer (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).
- **DUV:** >97% production uptime (e.g. NXT:1980Di); proven 15–20+yr front-line life, 30–40yr total with refurbishment. ASML refurbishes ~30 systems/yr (~20–25 PAS 5500), and ~95% of ~1,800 PAS 5500 ever sold are still in fabs — a self-reinforcing service/upgrade base.
- **EUV:** early NXE (~2018) uptime 70–80%, now approaching 90%+ as the source/optics mature; collector mirror swapped ~annually. Official design-life target ~7yr, but expected effective life 15+yr (5–7yr leading-edge + ~a decade in trailing roles) as a refurb/secondary market develops late-2020s. Implication: each EUV cohort keeps generating IBM revenue long after the system sale, and the field never shrinks — the recurring-revenue floor under the cyclicality (Useful/Economic Life report, 2026).

## Risks
- **Customer concentration:** sells to "a relatively small number of customers"; 3 largest = 35.4% of receivables; heightened by industry consolidation (20-F, 2026-02-25).
- **Single-source supply:** Carl Zeiss SMT is sole supplier of optics under an exclusive arrangement; many EUV parts single-sourced / not economically multi-sourceable (20-F, 2026-02-25).
- **Export controls / geopolitics:** China policy (immersion/DUV) directly governs ~20% of sales; outcomes embedded in FY26 guide bandwidth (20-F, 2026-02-25; Q1 2026).
- **Cyclicality + concentrated demand:** WFE cyclicality; AI-capex dependence — a hyperscaler capex air-pocket would propagate to DRAM/Logic litho orders.
- **2028 DRAM 4F² transition** air-pocket; High-NA HVM timing slippage; FCF lumpiness on down-payment timing.
- **Technology disruption (long-dated):** Substrate XRL / xLight FEL could undercut EUV economics at end-of-decade if industrialized; low-probability but the only structural threat to the monopoly (SemiAnalysis, 2025-10-29).
- **🆕 China domestic litho substitution — first production-stage evidence, and it is a schedule, not yet a volume (The Information · Staff "China Starts Mass-Producing Homegrown DUV Chipmaking Tools", 2026-07-27; single-outlet, "two people familiar," not a confirmed disclosure).** A Shanghai-based state-backed firm has begun **mass-producing immersion DUV**, with **~5 units in 2026 / ~20 in 2027 against the 131 immersion systems ASML shipped last year**, first deliveries slated to [[SMIC]], Hua Hong and CXMT. Immaterial to near-term volume, but it moves "China domestic litho" from a research programme (prototypes sent to some chipmakers last year) to a delivery schedule with named customers — the article states encouraging prototype test results are what triggered production this year. Structural backdrop: China made lithography a national priority in **2002**, and the **2022 US controls pushed Beijing from state-led research toward commercial execution, with Huawei coordinating industry resources**. **The policy leg compounds the risk rather than offsetting it:** the bipartisan **Match Act would both widen immersion-DUV restrictions and curb servicing of tools at some Chinese fabs**, so the same China revenue line is exposed from two independent directions (substitution or prohibition) and tighter restrictions are explicitly framed as the accelerant of the shift to local tools. What bounds it for now: the tools "still trail ASML's products in performance and build quality," mass-production qualification "could take many months or longer," **some key parts still come from Japan**, local-supplier delays already held back 2026 output, and the **domestic EUV effort remains prototype-stage, "likely years away"** from working chips.
