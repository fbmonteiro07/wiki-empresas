## Snapshot

S&P Global combines credit ratings, benchmarks, financial information and commodity intelligence. The investment question is how much durable growth comes from proprietary data and embedded workflows, versus cyclical issuance and market levels. The portfolio is changing: Mobility was spun off in July and Capital IQ and associated data are under strategic review. Treat the remaining company and the old consolidated perimeter separately. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-27](../SPGI/earnings/2026-10-27_spgi3q2026-earningsrelease.htm); Capstone synthesis, 2026-10-31.

Is Market Intelligence reacceleration durable enough to offset ratings mix? | The October Jefferies readout sees better-than-feared organic and subscription growth in MI. | A single improved quarter is not a sustained acceleration, while Ratings can disappoint despite healthy headline issuance. | MI subscriptions, retention and margin on a constant perimeter; Ratings transaction mix and non-transaction growth. | [Jefferies / Panzer, 2026-10-27](../SPGI/commentary/2026-10-27_OUTLOOK_c0139937eeca.md); [Jefferies / Panzer + Thind, 2026-09-02](../SPGI/commentary/2026-09-02_OUTLOOK_88014760505a.md) |

**Bull case — owned data can gain distribution as AI changes the interface.** Ratings, benchmarks and proprietary datasets remain inputs into financial decisions even when customers access them through AI tools. Management described normalizing AI usage rights and expanding integrations and data access. A more focused portfolio could improve execution and capital allocation. The required evidence is paid consumption, retention and organic growth, rather than the number of AI partnerships. [Cheung / Aboaf, 2026-10-27 call](../SPGI/transcripts/SPGI_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-10-31.

**Bear case — distribution changes can weaken workflow pricing, while a disposal crystallizes value below expectations.** A buyer must underwrite both the durability of Capital IQ economics and separation costs. Ratings remains sensitive to issuance mix, indices to markets and asset flows, and buybacks cannot repair a persistently weaker operating franchise. The Mobility separation also makes naïve comparisons with old group totals misleading. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-27](../SPGI/earnings/2026-10-27_spgi3q2026-earningsrelease.htm); Capstone interpretation, 2026-10-31.

**What would change the view:** sustained improvement in organic Market Intelligence growth and renewal economics; disclosed transaction terms that preserve value after stranded costs; or, on the downside, weaker retention/pricing despite growing AI distribution. Compare issuance-sensitive revenue with [MCO](MCO.md), without treating their different business mixes as interchangeable.
