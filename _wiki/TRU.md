## Snapshot

TransUnion is a consumer credit bureau that has broadened into fraud, marketing/identity (largely the Neustar assets) and consumer products, with a growing international footprint (India, Latin America including Trans Union de Mexico, the U.K., Canada). The thesis rests on a proprietary data franchise compounding high-single-digit organic growth while the OneTru platform migration ends, margins expand and free cash flow moves to buybacks. The bear case is mortgage: rates, FICO's score-pricing changes, VantageScore adoption and a possible FHFA move from tri-merge to bi-merge all hit that vertical. **Reported growth is flattered by no-margin FICO royalties.** In Q3, organic constant-currency revenue grew 8%, or about 5.5% excluding them. [Company Q3 release](../TRU/earnings/2026-10-27_exhibit99109302026.htm) and [supplement](../TRU/earnings/2026-10-27_exhibit9929302026.htm), 2026-10-27; Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

**The mortgage arithmetic carries most of the reported growth.** Management guides 2026 mortgage revenue to about $750m, +28%. About $315m of that is FICO royalty cost (2025: $183m), leaving about $435m excluding FICO. The company states that as +9%; its rounded bars ($405m to ~$435m) imply about +7%. Meanwhile full-year inquiries fall about 10% and Q4 inquiries fall by more than 20%. Sensitivity: each 10% rise in mortgage volume adds about $43m of adjusted EBITDA and $0.17 of adjusted EPS. A full return to 2019 levels adds about $240m and $0.90. [Supplement, 2026-10-27](../TRU/earnings/2026-10-27_exhibit9929302026.htm); [Todd Cello, Q3 call, 2026-10-27](../TRU/transcripts/TRU_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md).

**The quality of the margin weakened.** Management now expects underlying 2026 margin expansion of flat to +20bp, versus 50–70bp previously. The causes given were reinvestment in technology and solutions, plus higher legal and regulatory costs from individual consumer (FCRA) cases. Higher SOFR and the swap replacement will add about $60m of net interest expense in 2027. The Q4 guide implies revenue and adjusted EBITDA excluding FICO roughly flat on Q3 ($465m Q3 actual versus the $468m high end). [Cello / Chris Cartwright, Q3 call, 2026-10-27](../TRU/transcripts/TRU_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md); [supplement](../TRU/earnings/2026-10-27_exhibit9929302026.htm).

**Bull case — a data franchise past its investment hump.** The platform transformation is complete, and transformation add-backs ended with 2025. The majority of U.S. credit online and batch volume already runs on OneTru, with migration "largely" done by year-end. Management expects about $350m of three-year revenue from 2026 launches; this is a company estimate. Trusted Call Solutions is expected to reach about $200m in 2026, from $27m in 2021. Insurance revenue should exceed $430m. In mortgage, management argues tri-merge persists even without a mandate (pre-qualification, FHA/VA loans, rate shopping), and VantageScore adds revenue on top. [Cartwright, Q3 call, 2026-10-27](../TRU/transcripts/TRU_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md); [Cello, Q1 call, 2026-04-28](../TRU/transcripts/TRU_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-04-28.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — U.S. credit decelerates while mortgage policy caps the multiple.** Comparisons are getting harder. Tenant/employment screening and media are soft, the latter partly because customers moving to the new TruAudience platform are using it less in the interim. The underlying margin target has been cut, and the new 2027 interest headwind is already disclosed. Score competition and FHFA headlines keep bureau valuations volatile: the UBS desk relayed a Bloomberg-implied move of about 5.2% for TRU, against a ~7.5% average. Jefferies' desk asked what multiple is right once the tri-merge "genie" is out of the bottle. [Cartwright, Q3 call](../TRU/transcripts/TRU_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-27.md); [Jefferies desk, 2026-03-25 — Outlook](https://outlook.office365.com/owa/?ItemID=AAMkADllY2NkMmU1LTc3YTEtNGRkMC1hMjBkLWU1NjIzOTY5OTZhMQBGAAAAAAC2MTk9L2QgQLGbcPk%2FhCdiBwDBpLwblpT0TZ%2Bt%2Fvz7Ao6sAAAAAAEMAADBpLwblpT0TZ%2Bt%2Fvz7Ao6sAAUSFbHtAAA%3D&exvsurl=1&viewmodel=ReadMessageItem); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** a 2027 guide with organic growth excluding FICO at or above 6% and underlying margin back to about +50bp would support the bull case. So would a formal FHFA decision that keeps tri-merge, or one with the limited direct impact management shows. Conversely, a bi-merge order plus bureau-pricing pressure, or Financial Services excluding mortgage staying near 5% into easier comparisons, would support the bear case. Compare with [EFX](EFX.md) only after excluding FICO royalties, and remember EFX's Workforce Solutions has no TRU equivalent.

## Risks / monitoring

- **Mortgage and rates:** the most rate-sensitive vertical. Auto comes next; card and personal loans are less sensitive.
- **Score competition and FICO pass-through:** distorts reported growth and margins.
- **Regulation:** the FHFA/GSE evaluation of fewer than three credit reports is flagged in the FY2025 10-K. A new cautionary item, first appearing in the Q3 release and 10-Q, covers government actions on mortgage reporting requirements and on "the pricing of our services". The CFPB lawsuit was dismissed with prejudice on 2025-03-21.
- **Litigation:** rising individual FCRA litigation (Cello, Q3 call).
- **Operations:** data security and breach exposure; India and LatAm macro conditions (the Q3 Brazil growth included one-time revenue); integration of Mexico and the RealNetworks mobile division.
- **Floating-rate debt:** exposure beyond the hedged ~70%.
