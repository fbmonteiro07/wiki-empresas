## Snapshot

Cboe is the toll on US proprietary index options. SPX (including 0DTE, short-dated contracts expiring the same day), Mini-SPX (XSP) and VIX options trade under exclusive licences, and since the 2026-09-29 extension with S&P Dow Jones Indices that exclusivity runs through 2051. Around this franchise sit multi-listed options, US and European cash equities, VIX futures, FX and Data Vantage, which sells access, capacity and market data. The thesis is that retail and international demand for defined-risk, short-dated index options keeps compounding volume. In this view, Cboe can reprice an SPX complex it has not repriced in years, while the 2025–26 strategic realignment narrows the company to that core. The open tests are three. Will record 0DTE and retail volumes, and pricing, absorb the 2027 S&P royalty step-up? Do event contracts (Cboe Predicts, company-KPI contracts) become a revenue line, or are they a defensive response to perpetual futures and prediction-market platforms? Is the near-flat 2026 expense base structural or a volume-driven peak margin? [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-30](../CBOE/earnings/2026-10-30_cboe-20261030xex991.htm); [Craig Donohue / Jill Griebenow, Q3 call, 2026-10-30](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-30.md); [Q3 10-Q, filed 2026-10-30](../CBOE/CBOE_10-Q_2026-10-30_0001628280-26-068447.html); Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

The 9M organic pace (+24%) runs above the high-teens full-year guide, which leaves room for a slower Q4 against a record Q4 2025 base ($671.1m, +28%). That is a Capstone inference, not guidance.

**The portfolio is shrinking to the core.** Management announced sale processes for Cboe Australia and Cboe Canada on 2025-10-31. It also announced exits from US and European corporate listings and cost cuts in ETP listings, CEDX and smaller analytics businesses. Management expected the combined annualized effect, including the Japan wind-down, to be ~3% lower net revenue and 8–10% lower adjusted opex against the 2025 guided ranges ([Donohue / Griebenow, Q3 2025 call, 2025-10-31](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q3-2025-earnings_2025-10-31.md)). On 2026-04-22 Cboe announced an agreement to sell both businesses to TMX Group for approximately $300m. Australia closed 2026-07-31; Canada awaits approvals. The 10-Q lists Cboe Digital, Cboe Japan and CEDX as wind-downs ([Q3 10-Q](../CBOE/CBOE_10-Q_2026-10-30_0001628280-26-068447.html)). Together with those exits, a second realignment phase announced 2026-05-01 is expected to reduce the workforce by ~20% ([Q1 release, 2026-05-01](../CBOE/earnings/2026-05-01_cboe-20260501xex991.htm)). Management estimates that Australia contributed ~$20m of 2026 net revenue to closing (~$17m in Data Vantage). It still guides total 2026 net revenue growth to the high teens ([Griebenow, Q3 call](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-30.md)).

**Bull case — a licensed, defined-risk toll with a long runway and new adjacencies.** SPX is the global reference for equity-index risk, and the licence now runs to 2051. Short-dated, capped-risk use keeps widening across retail, international (e.g., Korean and Taiwanese brokers) and institutional users. Cboe has pricing room the SPX complex has not used in years and a cost base reset by the realignment. Data Vantage compounds on connectivity and international data sales. Clearing at CCUS and Cboe Clear Europe, extended hours (single-stock options from October; 23x5 equities targeted for December) and KPI contracts could add volume pools that Cboe controls end to end. Hocking frames KPI adoption as following 0DTE: retail first, then institutions once datasets build. That is an analogy, not evidence. [Q3 call, 2026-10-30](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-30.md); [Hocking, Q4 2025 call, 2026-02-06](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-02-06.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — peak volumes, a higher licence bill and a contested retail channel.** 2026 combines record SPX activity, a one-time retail regulatory tailwind (the June pattern-day-trader repeal) and bursts of event volatility. The royalty reset starts in 2027 with undisclosed terms, so the bull case depends on unproven repricing elasticity. Retail volume is concentrated in few accounts ([Redburn deep dive, 2026-06-11](../CBOE/commentary/2026-06-11_Exchanges-deep-dive-CME-u-g-to-Buy-top-pick-ICE-Nasdaq-DB1-all-Buy-MarketAxess-d-g-to-Neutral-CBOE-T_a03cddca.md)). Brokers that own those accounts (Robinhood with Rothera) and CFTC venues (Kalshi perps and binaries) are building substitutes outside Cboe's licence. Cboe argues these are securities that belong under the SEC, which is a regulatory bet rather than a moat. [Donohue, Q1 call, 2026-05-01](../CBOE/transcripts/CBOE_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-05-01.md); [Redburn / Bendit via sales, 2026-10-26](../HOOD/commentary/2026-10-26_Prediction-Markets-New-deep-dive-sizes-the-opportunity-for-Kalshi-Polymarket-Robinhood-Sell-IBKR-Buy_2aeda09f.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** on the upside, a 2027 guide showing organic growth despite the royalty step-up, steady index RPC after any SPX fee changes, and KPI contracts drawing brokers beyond Robinhood/IBKR; on the downside, an index-options volume reversal after a regime change, multi-listed share sliding further, or SEC/CFTC rulings that let S&P-referenced binaries or perps trade outside Cboe's licence. Compare index-derivatives economics with CME equity-index futures ([CME](CME.md)), multi-listed options share with [NDAQ](NDAQ.md), clearing ambitions with [ICE](ICE.md), and the royalty from the licensor's side in [SPGI](SPGI.md) — on matched definitions only.

## Risks / monitoring

Track licence dependence: options and futures generated ~68% of FY2025 net revenue, mostly from exclusively licensed indices and VIX products, now under a licence extended to 2051 with an undisclosed 2027 royalty step-up. Track concentration: one customer is ~10% of revenue and the top ten ~57%; three clearing members accounted for ~71% of transaction and other fees collected through OCC, and ~69% of net transaction and clearing fees came from products cleared through OCC. Also monitor volume and volatility cyclicality and the concentration of retail 0DTE activity; competition from multi-listed options, perpetual futures and prediction-market venues offering SPX-like exposure; the SEC/CFTC split on event contracts and rules on retail options trading; operating risk at CCUS as a new securities clearinghouse (Subpart C DCO) and at Cboe Clear Europe; collectability of the Consolidated Audit Trail funding notes; AI-driven technology cost inflation; and execution of the Canada sale and wind-downs.
