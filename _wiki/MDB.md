## Snapshot
MongoDB is a document (JSON) operational database sold two ways: **Atlas** (cloud DBaaS, consumption-billed, ~74–75% of revenue) and **Enterprise Advanced / "EA & other"** (self-managed licence, term-revenue under ASC 606, now the "run anywhere" leg). Search, vector search and — since the Feb-2025 Voyage AI acquisition ($220M, $20M cash; Q4 FY25 call, 2025-03-05) — embedding/re-ranking models are built into the platform. FY25 revenue crossed $2B (+19%) (Q4 FY25 call, 2025-03-05); FY27 is guided to $2.99–3.03B (+21–23%) (Q2 FY27 call, 2026-09-01). Positioning in the AI stack: the operational layer ("OLTP is the high ground") — agent memory, session state and retrieval — which is also why it is the public name that wears the **Postgres-for-new-AI-apps** bear (theme §2.4/§2.5). Leadership turned over completely inside the window: CFO Michael Gordon left (announced 2024-12-09), Mike Berry joined ~late May 2025 (Q1 FY26 call, 2025-06-04); **CEO Dev Ittycheria → CJ Desai** (ex-ServiceNow) — Desai's first call was Q3 FY26 on 2025-12-01, "day 28 on the job", i.e. he started ~2025-11-03; Ittycheria moved to the board.
