Method & definitions

Prices: Bloomberg daily OHLC (split-adjusted, local currency, primary listing) via the local Desktop API — fetch_technicals_bbg.py → build_technicals.py. Technical read only; it carries no Capstone or broker view.

  • RSI(14), Wilder smoothing. Signal = 9-day simple average of RSI; “RSI ↑ signal” = RSI crossed above its signal today. Oversold < 30, overbought > 70.
  • vs SMA = % distance of the last close from its 20/50/200-day simple average. 200D pct = where today's distance sits in the name's own 3-year history (0 = most stretched below trend ever seen in the window).
  • Trend = SMA50 above/below SMA200; golden/death cross flagged for 20 sessions.
  • Support / resistance = clusters of swing lows/highs (a bar whose low/high is the extreme of a ±5-day window) over the last 2 years. Swings join a level when within a tolerance of 0.75×ATR(14)% (floor 1.5%, cap 4%) and ≥10 sessions apart. 2 touches = double bottom, 3+ = triple. “At support” = within max(2.5%, 0.6×ATR) (price may sit up to one tolerance below it) of the nearest 2x+ level below price.
  • Broken support = a 2x+ swing-low level that price closed below in the last 10 sessions (now resistance-in-waiting).
  • Bull divergence = the latest touch of that support printed a lower-or-equal low than the prior touch but with a higher RSI (selling momentum fading into the retest).
  • The last 5 sessions cannot confirm a swing yet, so a fresh low is visible on the chart before it counts as a touch.
