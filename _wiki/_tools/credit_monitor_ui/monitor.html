Reading the monitor

CDS levels and changes are in basis points. Bond levels are yield to maturity in percent; yield changes are in basis points. Coupon rates identify bonds and are never used as missing yields.

1D compares with the prior available session, at most five calendar days back. 1W and 1M use the observation on or before seven and thirty calendar days before the latest quote, with four days of tolerance. Hover a change for its actual dates.

The comparison window ends today in São Paulo. Change mode uses a common start date and each series’ observation on or before it, within four days; new or sparse series without that baseline stay excluded. Gaps over seven days break the line.

Coverage & freshness

Quotes more than four calendar days old, or retained after a failed fetch, are labeled stale. A refresh timestamp is not a quote date.

The main bond follows the configured headline, preferring USD straight debt and the shorter maturity when several headlines exist. Select another bond inside a company. Convertible and foreign-currency bonds retain their labels; convertibles enter comparison only when explicitly included.
