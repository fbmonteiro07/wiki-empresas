Research design

Explicit formula

T = mean of cross-sectional percentile ranks: RSI14, 21-session return, 63-session return, price / SMA50 − 1 E = mean of percentile ranks: fixed-FY EPS 28-day change, fixed-FY sales 28-day change S = mean of percentile ranks: count-weighted X tone over 7 days, change vs prior 28 days TE = (T + E) / 2 TES = (T + E + S) / 3 EPS change = 2 × (new − old) / (|new| + |old|) Sales change = new / old − 1 Attention = log(1 + mean daily posts, 7d) − log(1 + mean daily posts, preceding 28d) Solve x + cost × Σ|x × target weight − prior weight| = 1 Net period factor = x × gross factor Final exit also pays liquidation cost.

Fixed fiscal year = signal calendar year + 1. Both endpoints use the same target. Horizons differ across fiscal calendars. Analyst additions/removals and vendor corrections remain in the aggregate consensus series.

What this version can and cannot establish

This experiment tests transparent historical rules. It does not prove that original observations were available at the vendor's marked date, that historical watchlist membership was unbiased, or that execution costs can be achieved. It cannot establish the value of an unavailable full-year specialist-sales or curated-X history.

Short interest is displayed as context only because publication-time alignment is unverified. Options, borrow fees, real fund flows, independent author weighting and full-text thesis-change classification are not scored. Curated-X backtests use archived bull/bear counts without retrospective engagement weights. Desk reviews are an evidence trail, not a historical predictor.

Sector exposures, beta and volatility can still explain relative returns. Equal-weight watchlist and QQQ comparisons are benchmarks, not risk-neutral portfolios. The block intervals are exploratory, cross-section-preserving, and unadjusted for multiple comparisons. No rule or weight is tuned after looking at this run.
