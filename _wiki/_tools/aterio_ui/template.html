What the Aterio data holds, and what it says

Overview

Every building Aterio tracks, by stage. All power figures are facility power (the building's total draw). To get IT load, divide by the facility/IT ratio shown on each tile.

    Which "GW under construction"? Four numbers, four bases

    Quoted figures differ because of what they count: building or campus, stage, date window, facility power or IT load.

    Files and fields

    What is in the folder, how the files join, and how complete each field is. Fill rates are for US rows; "UC" is the fill rate among buildings under construction.

    Field guide Level comes from Aterio's dictionary: Campus-level fields repeat on every building of the campus, so never sum them across buildings.

    When does it come online?

    GW by the activation date on each building: the go-live date for active buildings, Aterio's or the public estimate for the rest. The pipeline is lumpy: a few multi-GW campuses fill the out-years.

    How the census grew

    Two ways to read history. Snapshots show what Aterio knew at each month-end. The reconstruction applies today's knowledge back in time (from the weekly report). The gap between them is coverage Aterio added later, not projects that started later.

    Recorded then vs known today

    Month-end snapshots by stage

    Point-in-time GW in each monthly-history file (plus the current file).

    Quarterly flows from the event log

    GW of buildings with an event of that type in the quarter, at each building's current size. Aterio backfills dates, so recent quarters keep filling in. The last quarter is partial (to the as-of date).

    Execution: do projects arrive when Aterio says?

    Compares the same buildings across two snapshots. The base month selects the earlier snapshot.
