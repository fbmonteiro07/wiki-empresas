## Snapshot

Equifax pairs a U.S./international consumer credit bureau with The Work Number (TWN), a proprietary income-and-employment verification database inside Workforce Solutions. The thesis rests on Workforce Solutions (government, talent, consumer lending) and the non-mortgage "Diversified Markets" businesses compounding while AI-driven cost savings expand margins. The swing factors are the U.S. mortgage cycle and regulation of mortgage credit reporting: FICO score pricing, VantageScore adoption and a possible move from tri-merge to bi-merge credit reports. U.S. mortgage was about 20% of Equifax revenue in Q4 2025 ([company Q4 2025 call, 2026-02-04](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-02-04.md)). Reported mortgage growth is inflated by FICO royalties that Equifax passes through at zero margin. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-20](../EFX/earnings/2026-10-20_exhibit99120260930.htm); Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

**Mortgage drove the guide-down.** CFO John Gamble said mortgage volumes fell from late August as 30-year rates reached about 7.5%, against 6.6% when July guidance was set. Management put the cost versus plan at about $20m of Q3 revenue, about $60m in Q4 and about $80m for FY2026. FY guidance assumes U.S. originations down an estimated 6.5–7.5%, with mortgage revenue ex-FICO slightly up. On current run-rates, management expects 2027 mortgage transactions down high single digits on average, mostly in the first eight months. Q3 U.S. mortgage revenue rose 15% reported but only 1% ex-FICO, so most headline mortgage growth is royalty pass-through. [Gamble / Mark Begor, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md).

**Diversified Markets and cost carried the quarter.** Management reported Talent Solutions up more than 20%, consumer lending up high double digits and government up mid-single digits. TWN had 223m total active records (+12%), 125m current active records (+11%) and 110m unique SSNs, against about 250m income-producing Americans. Year-to-date government contracts total more than $550m of annual contract value: about $150m new and $400m renewals, mostly benefiting 2027. EBITDA margin ex-FICO rose 130bp. The FY ex-FICO margin-expansion guide rose to +100bp from +75bp, and the USIS ex-FICO margin was 42.4%. [Begor / Gamble, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md). The 2026–28 AI cost-reduction target was doubled to $150m in July. [Company Q2 release, 2026-07-21](../EFX/earnings/2026-07-21_exhibit99120260630.htm).

**USIS mortgage economics (management disclosure, not guidance).** Management broke expected 2026 USIS mortgage revenue down as follows: ~45% FICO royalty pass-through and ~10% resale of TransUnion/Experian files into tri-merge, both at zero margin; ~30% Equifax trended files (hard pulls); ~15% soft-pull pre-qualification/pre-approval. Management sized a GSE move to bi-merge at about $80m of revenue and $38m of margin, noting GSE loans are about 40% of originations and that FHA/VA (about 25%) are expected to keep tri-merge. Its other estimates were about $35m of extra margin from full VantageScore conversion and about $1bn revenue / $700m margin upside from a full mortgage recovery. [Begor, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md).

**Bull case — proprietary data outgrows the mortgage cycle.** TWN gives Equifax a data asset that peers lack. Government social-services verification (management's $5bn TAM, under 20% penetrated) and talent and lending verifications can compound as records grow. Vitality above 17% points to new products leaving the cloud platform. Once FICO pass-through fades, mortgage revenue becomes smaller but higher-margin, and any rate relief adds operating leverage. Redburn's August upgrade argues that 2027 combines easier mortgage comparisons, government wins and Mexico. [Begor, Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md); [Redburn / Simon Clinch research summary, 2026-08-24](../EFX/commentary/2026-08-24_Best-of-Rothschild-Co-Redburn-Apple-Equifax-u-g-to-BUY-Hershey-Sell-Sherwin-Deutsche-Borse-BUYs-Resm_f2f42112.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — the two growth engines both carry policy risk.** Government revenue depends on state budgets, procurement timing and federal verification tools that can bypass TWN. Mortgage economics hinge on FHFA and FICO decisions that Equifax does not control. Workforce Solutions also faces a CFPB investigation into data accuracy and dispute handling, plus an antitrust class action over verification pricing. If 2027 originations fall high single digits as management modeled, margin expansion must come from cost cuts rather than mix. [Q3 10-Q, 2026-10-20](../EFX/EFX_10-Q_2026-10-20_0000033185-26-000044.html); [Jefferies sales, 2026-07-21](../EFX/commentary/2026-07-21_Fins-First-Looks------MSCI-EFX-SCHW-SYF-ALLY-KEY-MRSH_c7eb3a95.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** government growth re-accelerating to double digits as signed contracts go live in 1H27; an FHFA outcome keeping tri-merge (or optional bi-merge with limited uptake); paid VantageScore conversion lifting USIS ex-FICO margin; or, negatively, state losses to consent-based or new-entrant verification and a 2027 guide implying flat ex-FICO margins. Compare [TRU](TRU.md) on matched mortgage definitions (with/without FICO royalties) and organic local-currency growth.

## Risks / monitoring

**Mortgage rates and score economics.** Mortgage volumes move with rates. FICO royalty increases raise reported revenue while diluting margin, and any FHFA change to tri-merge or to score choice reshapes USIS and EWS mortgage revenue.

**Regulatory and legal exposure in Workforce Solutions.** The CFPB has issued three civil investigative demands (2023–24) on Workforce Solutions data accuracy and dispute handling. A putative antitrust class action over verification services was filed in May 2024. Obligations from the 2017 cybersecurity incident continue.

**Government, data access and integration.** Government revenue depends on state budgets, contract timing and competing verification methods. Data-furnisher access, FX and Argentine inflation, cloud completion in Australia and integration of Círculo (with leverage near 3x per management) also matter. [FY2025 10-K, 2026-02-19](../EFX/EFX_10-K_2026-02-19_0000033185-26-000010.html); [Q3 2026 10-Q, filed 2026-10-20](../EFX/EFX_10-Q_2026-10-20_0000033185-26-000044.html); [Q3 call, 2026-10-20](../EFX/transcripts/EFX_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-20.md).

**Consumer credit read-through.** Management saw some slowing in financial-institution and auto volumes and in hiring late in Q3, but no tightening of lender portfolio reviews or credit lines. Watch this as an early read for card and auto lenders and for [JPM](JPM.md) consumer trends.
