## Snapshot
Sanmina is a US-HQ electronics-manufacturing-services (EMS) / contract manufacturer serving communications, cloud, industrial, defence, and medical end markets, competing with [FLEX](FLEX.md) and Celestica. The acquisition of ZT Systems (AI rack assembly) became the primary revenue driver and re-rating catalyst: ZT contributed $1.88B in F2Q26, well ahead of plan, driven by AMD MI300/MI450 accelerated-compute solutions. Management guides FY27 total revenue to $16B+ (~2x FY25). The Street is re-rating SANM from a legacy-EMS discount toward a ZT-enabled AI-infra play, though all three covering analysts (JPM, BofA, SIG) hold Neutral on ZT-margin-sustainability and rack-ramp-timing uncertainty. SANM and SMTC were added to the S&P MidCap 400 (2026-06-08).

## Current state (latest quarter)
**F3Q26 (Jun quarter, reported 2026-07-27 AMC) — beat & raise on the P&L, but the ZT/Helios ramp slipped a quarter.** Revenue at the **high end of the $3.2–3.5B guide**; **operating margin 8.0%** and **non-GAAP EPS $3.31**, both above the high end of guidance (BofA · Ruplu Bhattacharya / Wamsi Mohan, 2026-07-28).
- **Core Sanmina $2.4B, +17% y/y** — well ahead, with growth across *all* end markets. JPM flags the acceleration explicitly: **legacy growth went +8% y/y in F2Q26 → +17% y/y in F3Q**, driven by a broader **Industrial** acceleration (the same signal [[TEL]] showed) plus continued AI-linked demand in legacy **Communications Networks and Cloud Infrastructure**, which together grew **+33% y/y** (JPM · Manmohanpreet Singh, 2026-07-28; BofA, 2026-07-28).
- **ZT Systems $1.1B — at the midpoint of guide**, i.e. the one line that did not beat.
- **Margin quality flagged:** the operating-margin upside reflects strong execution, **favourable mix (higher legacy Sanmina)** and a **~100bps positive contribution from non-recurring engineering (NRE) services** in F3Q — services that continue into F4Q but **moderate as production ramps** (BofA, 2026-07-28).

⚠️ **The guide is where the news is. ZT F4Q26 is guided to $0.8–1.0B (~$0.9B midpoint) — modestly below the prior implied guide — and full-year ZT to $4.8–5.0B.** Two distinct causes, and the market conflated them: (1) **lower revenue from a few legacy storage and general-purpose compute programs**, and (2) **management does not expect to recognise ANY revenue from [[AMD]] Helios racks in F4Q26** — a **revenue-recognition timing** issue, since Sanmina can only recognise revenue when customers physically receive the racks in their datacenter. **Helios revenue therefore starts F1Q27**, and ZT revenue overall is **weighted to F2H27** (BofA, 2026-07-28; JPM, 2026-07-28). **Management reiterated confidence in $16B+ of FY27 revenue** — so this is a phasing change, not a demand change, but it pushes the proof point out by roughly two quarters.

**F2Q26 (Mar quarter, reported 2026-04-27 AMC):**
- Revenue $4,013M (+25.8% q/q, +102.3% y/y) vs. guide midpoint $3,250M / consensus ~$3,285M.
  - ZT Systems: $1,880M (vs. implied guide ~$1,100M); majority MI450 platform shipments — **no Blackwell or Rubin contribution** (SIG).
  - Core SANM: +7.3% y/y, in line.
- Non-GAAP EPS $3.16 vs. guide/consensus $2.40; non-GAAP GM/OM 8.97% / 6.40% (above guided OM 5.7–6.2%).
- Book-to-bill >1.1x across all end markets; board authorized an additional $600M buyback.

**Guides:** F3Q26 (Jun qtr) revenue $3.2B–$3.5B (core $2.2–$2.3B; ZT $1.0–$1.2B), EPS $2.55–$2.85 — ZT step-down reflects pull-forward normalization. FY26 revenue $13.7B–$14.3B, EPS $10.75–$11.35. FY27 outlook $16B+ (BofA models ZT $7B + core $9.5B). Pre-quiet-period check-in: ZT test-unit shipments on track (JPM / Samik Chatterjee, 2026-06-21).

## Debate / thesis
- **Bull — ZT + AMD rack integration = structural re-rating:** ZT is executing ahead of plan on AMD MI300/MI450 rack assembly; FY27 $16B+ implies a near-doubling within two years. Book-to-bill >1.1x; next-gen MI450 wins announced with multiple customers ramping FY27. Longer term, PCB vertical integration is the next margin vector beyond MI450/MI500.
- **Bear — ZT margin risk + pull-forward distortion:** F2Q26 ZT revenue was a pull-forward from F3Q/F4Q; the F3Q guide reflects normalization. ZT operating margins are only in-line with core SANM (~6.4%), not a premium. As more competitors build AMD Helios racks, SANM may face price competition; management characterized current MI300 business as "project-based."
- **AMD opportunity cadence:** MI300 (minimal, late-cycle/project-based) → MI400/MI450 (full rack integration, early involvement, FY27 ramp) → PCB vertical integration (next vector).
- **The F3Q26 print sharpened the bear into something narrower and more testable.** The old bear was *"ZT margins are only in line with core, and competition will compress them."* The new one is **timing**: Helios revenue recognition is gated on physical rack delivery to the customer's datacenter, so **zero Helios revenue lands in F4Q26 and the ramp is skewed to F2H27** — while **core Sanmina simultaneously accelerated to +17% y/y**. That inverts the composition of the story for at least two quarters: the "legacy EMS discount" business is currently doing the growing, and the AI-rack business is the one being pushed out. Hosseini's read is that **the new-platform ramp is skewed toward 2H27, with NPO (new-product-opportunity) representing revenue upside** to that base (Mehdi Hosseini, 2026-07-28).
- **Where the sell-side stands (still all Neutral, but the PTs moved in opposite directions on 2026-07-28):** **JPM (Manmohanpreet Singh / Samik Chatterjee) — Neutral, PT CUT to $260 (from $275, Dec-27)**; **BofA (Ruplu Bhattacharya / Wamsi Mohan) — Neutral, PT RAISED to $270 (from $234)**, 21x C27E EPS; **Hosseini — Neutral, PT RAISED to $210 (from $185)**, price $208.90, downside risk $175, mkt cap $11.2bn. UBS (Robert Ruple) and Jefferies (Rankie Wong) cover via sales commentary only (no formal rating). ⚠️ **Note a gap in this page's history: JPM's PT went $210 → $275 → $260, and the intermediate $275 mark was never captured here** — logged rather than back-filled. _Prior marks superseded: JPM $210 (post-F2Q26); BofA $234 (18x C27E); Hosseini $185 — see Changelog._ **The dispersion is now narrow ($260 / $270 / $210) but the direction of travel is not — JPM cut on the ZT phasing while BofA raised on the core-business acceleration and margin quality, which is precisely the trade-off the print created.**

## Catalysts / what to watch
- ✅ **RESOLVED — F3Q26 (Jun) print, 2026-07-27 AMC.** Revenue at the high end of the $3.2–3.5B guide, OM 8.0%, EPS $3.31 above the high end. Outcome: **mixed** — P&L beat (core +17% y/y), but ZT came in only at the guide midpoint and the F4Q ZT guide slipped. See `## Current state`.
- **F1Q27 — the first quarter with [[AMD]] Helios rack revenue.** This is now the single most important date on the page: management expects **zero Helios revenue in F4Q26** on rev-rec timing and the ZT ramp weighted to **F2H27**. Watch whether F1Q27 recognises what F4Q26 deferred, or whether the slip extends.
- **F4Q26 print** vs. the ZT $0.8–1.0B guide and the FY26 ZT $4.8–5.0B envelope; also whether the **~100bps NRE-services margin contribution** moderates as guided.
- **Does $16B+ FY27 survive contact with the phasing?** Management reiterated it at F3Q26 — the reiteration is the thing to re-test each quarter, since it now depends on a 2H27-weighted ZT ramp.
- **ZT margin sustainability** (~6.4% OM) as AMD Helios competition increases.
- **PCB vertical integration** timing and incremental margin (beyond MI450, into MI500).
- Any S&P MidCap 400 rebalancing flow / re-initiation.

## Risks
- ZT margin compression from mix, competition, and potential consignment.
- Pull-forward distortion in quarterly ZT revenue.
- AMD rack-ramp timing and concentration on a single key customer.
- Memory (DDR4/5) availability cited as a F2Q26 Communications-segment limiter.
