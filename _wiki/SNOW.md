## Snapshot
Cloud data platform (the "AI Data Cloud") sold on **consumption**, not seats: customers buy capacity, and Snowflake recognizes revenue as compute/storage/AI tokens are used — AI is "not a SKU … it's on their existing spend" (Ramaswamy, Q1 FY26, 2025-05-21). Product revenue is ~95% of the total; the core is the analytics warehouse, with data engineering (Snowpark, Dynamic Tables, OpenFlow), collaboration/data sharing (43% of customers share with at least one "stable edge"), applications (Streamlit, Snowflake Postgres, Hybrid Tables) and — since FY26 — a first-party AI layer: Cortex AI → Cortex Analyst / Search → Snowflake Intelligence (business-user agent surface, now presented as **CoWork**) → **Cortex Code / "CoCo"** (agentic coding harness, GA 2026-02-05) → Cortex AI Gateway (model router) (Q2 FY27, 2026-09-02). Management's framing has moved in two years from "easiest, most cost-effective data platform" (Q3 FY25, 2024-11-20) to "the governed control plane for the agentic enterprise" (Q2 FY27). In the AI stack it is the **analytical-platform / AI-data-repository** layer — where enterprise context lands so agents can reason on it (theme page §2.1). 14,554 customers, 829 Forbes G2K (41% of the G2K), 828 customers >$1M TTM, 65 >$10M (Q2 FY27).
