## Snapshot
Eaton is a global power-management company supplying electrical components, switchgear, busway, and power-distribution equipment — increasingly an "AI-factory power" name as hyperscaler buildouts require grid interconnection and in-facility power distribution. ETN sits in the datacenter-electrical basket alongside [VRT](VRT.md), [GEV](GEV.md), and [PWR](PWR.md), and is routinely tagged a beneficiary whenever datacenter-capex estimates are revised higher. A 2026-06-11 Reverse Morris Trust merging ETN's Mobility Group into Dana (DAN) sharpens the company toward higher-margin Electrical/Aerospace.

## Current state (latest quarter)
Q1 2026 (reported ~2026-05-05) was characterized as mixed-to-negative: full-year margin guide trimmed and Q2 guidance below Street (Vital Knowledge, 2026-05-05) — ETN grouped with CMI on margin shortfall, vs. DD/ROK upside. No specific revenue/EPS figures in the ported corpus (characterization only). Q4 2025 was also flagged a negative print that "dominated the conversation" alongside PYPL (Vital Knowledge, 2026-02-03).

**Corporate event — ETN/DAN Reverse Morris Trust (2026-06-11):** ETN's Mobility Group to merge into Dana via RMT; deal value ~$5.1B / 8.3x 2026E pro forma EBITDA (5.9x including ~$250MM run-rate synergies). ETN receives a $1.1B cash distribution; ETN holders own ≥50.1% of the combined ~$10B-EV entity. Described as "immediately accretive to Eaton's organic growth rate and operating margins upon closing." (Vital Knowledge, 2026-06-11; JPM / Rajat Gupta — DAN coverage, 2026-06-11)

## Debate / thesis
- **Bull:** AI-capex exposure is the core driver — MS Capex Tracker (2026-05-18) shows NTM capex +38% y/y (hyperscalers +126%) and named ETN among "most-supported" names (ETN, ROK, VRT, FPS, HUBB). 800V HVDC transition pulled ahead: NVDA Vera Rubin and GOOGL next-gen AI DCs adopting 800V HVDC with small-volume shipments from Q3 2026; ETN named beneficiary alongside Delta Electronics, [VRT](VRT.md), Schneider, Siemens (@aleabitoreddit, 2026-06-15). META capex (~$10B+ '26/'27) flagged positive for VRT/ETN/PWR/GEV (2026-05-18). Mobility divestiture sharpens portfolio toward higher-margin, more AI-levered Electrical/Aerospace segments.
- **Bear:** Q1 2026 print was the soft spot — full-year margin guide trimmed and Q2 below Street, which needs to reconcile with the otherwise-bullish AI-capex narrative (Vital Knowledge, 2026-05-05). Competitive pressure vs. Hubbell (HUBB), Regal Rexnord, and [VRT](VRT.md) in DC switchgear. RMT deployment of the $1.1B cash distribution (buyback vs. debt vs. M&A) undecided.
- **Modular / content-per-MW read-through — the first hard content number on this page, and the first full map of ETN's modular platform stack (SemiAnalysis "The Wild Wild West Of LEGO Datacenters", 2026-07-29):** a 75-page bottom-up study of datacenter modular construction mapping **>80 vendors**, tracking **>61 GW of modular capacity across 1,000+ sites**, and estimating **modular penetration reaches 30%+ of total live capacity by end-2028**. "**Eaton has one of the broadest portfolios within modular power.**" The ETN-specific reads:
  - **Content per MW, net-new to this page:** "**Management estimates its addressable datacenter content at approximately $2.9 million per MW, increasing to $3.4 million per MW with the addition of liquid cooling.**" For context in the same note: Schneider ~$1.2-3.3mn/MW, nVent ~$1mn/MW, Modine ~$0.6→0.7mn/MW, Vertiv ~$3.5mn discrete → ~$7mn/MW full-stack; Quanta frames the whole DC construction opportunity at ~$13mn/MW. ETN therefore screens near the top of the electrical-OEM content ladder, second only to Vertiv's full-stack case.
  - **Three stacked modular platforms, sized.** **NordicEPOD** is "the most standardized part of the portfolio," packaging **medium-voltage switchgear, transformers, UPS systems, batteries, low-voltage distribution and cooling into transportable power modules of roughly 1.7 MW to 2.0 MW, with configurations reaching as high as 3.1 MW.** **Fibrebond** "expands that model into **larger pre-integrated electrical buildings in North America**." **Flexnode** — a separate, privately held modular-datahall partner, **not to be confused with Flex Ltd (FLEX US)** — "extends Eaton into **turnkey modular data halls ranging from approximately 3.5 MW to 35 MW**," into which "**Eaton supplies critical-power backup, racks, cable management, and 800 VDC-ready infrastructure… which the companies say can reduce deployment schedules by roughly 35%.**" Together these "give Eaton coverage from the power enclosure through the data hall."
  - **The NVIDIA hook — "Eaton Beam Rubin DSX."** SemiAnalysis describes this as "the company's attempt to bring those modular assets together": a platform **combining Fibrebond enclosures, NordicEPOD power skids, Flexnode modular halls, 800 VDC distribution and Boyd liquid cooling around NVIDIA's Vera Rubin reference architecture.** _(Boyd Thermal is Eaton's own — the acquisition Barclays re-based its SoTP on in Mar-2026 — so this is an internally-sourced cooling leg, not a third-party dependency.)_ Eaton is separately listed in NVIDIA's broader **DSX ecosystem** alongside Cadence, Dassault Systèmes, Jacobs, Nscale, Phaidra, Procore, PTC, Schneider Electric, Siemens, Switch, Trane and Vertiv. NVIDIA formalized the **Vera Rubin DSX reference design in March 2026** (first unveiled as an Omniverse digital-twin blueprint at GTC Washington in October 2025).
  - **The sector-structure point that matters for ETN's order book.** Big OEMs — "not only Vertiv but also companies like Schneider and Siemens" — are sold out and now **selective on capacity-slot allocation, requiring capacity minimums and favouring bigger projects**, pushing smaller developers toward system integrators. Set against the bottom-up economics SemiAnalysis derives for a 50 MW liquid-cooled US hall (**~36% shorter construction window / 7-9 months, ~8% lower all-in cost at ~$13.5mn vs ~$14.6mn per MW**), where the schedule payoff concentrates specifically in **the power block — ~26% of the build by content, delivering IT-ready ~22% faster (~13 vs ~16.7 months) and ~5% cheaper per MW** — that is Eaton's own scope. (SemiAnalysis "The Wild Wild West Of LEGO Datacenters", 2026-07-29)

## Catalysts / what to watch
- **ETN/DAN RMT close** — pro forma standalone Electrical/Aerospace margin and growth profile; use of the $1.1B cash distribution.
- **Q2 2026 results** (expected ~late July/early Aug 2026) — can the margin trend recover after the Q1 guide trim?
- **800V HVDC ramp** — small-volume Q3 2026 start at NVDA/GOOGL; ETN content per the BofA Datacloud '26 note (full body not in corpus).
- **Order/backlog cadence** for electrical products into hyperscaler customers; competitive positioning vs. [VRT](VRT.md).
- **Modular platform execution** — whether "Eaton Beam Rubin DSX" (Fibrebond enclosures + NordicEPOD skids + Flexnode halls + 800 VDC distribution + Boyd liquid cooling, around NVIDIA's Vera Rubin reference architecture) converts into repeat-production orders, and whether the ~$2.9mn/MW content estimate migrates toward the ~$3.4mn/MW liquid-cooling case (SemiAnalysis, 2026-07-29). Watch the Flexnode partnership's claimed **~35% deployment-schedule reduction** for a customer proof point.

## Risks
- Margin trajectory post-Q1 guide trim; reconciling cyclical electrical demand with the AI-capex narrative.
- Competition in DC switchgear/power distribution (HUBB, Regal Rexnord, [VRT](VRT.md)).
- RMT execution and capital-allocation choices.
- Any China / export-control exposure (not surfaced in the ported corpus).

**➜ 08-12 UPDATE (/run-inbox).** Bernstein initiates the labor-ceiling frame and it **adds a second, independent reason to own the modular platform — one that does not depend on DC demand being bigger.** The argument is that demand is already past what the trades can staff (~35 GW/yr ceiling vs a 359.5 GW pipeline), so **the marginal GW gets built by whoever moves work off the field and into the factory**, and ETN is named first on that list. That is a **share-shift** thesis rather than a volume thesis, and it is the first item on this page that would still work if DC capex growth decelerated. ⚠️ **But it arrives with a live measurement conflict against the 07-29 SemiAnalysis work already on this page — a ~7x gap in man-hours/MW, on the exact input Bernstein's ceiling is computed from. Until that is resolved, treat the 35 GW ceiling as a framework, not a number.** Still silent, three months on, on the trimmed FY margin guide.

**Síntese do quarter:** the flow since the Q1 print is **one-sided in ETN's favour on demand and content, and silent on the thing that actually broke the stock in May.** The 07-29 SemiAnalysis work is the first hard content-per-MW number this page has ever carried (~$2.9mn → ~$3.4mn/MW) and the first full map of the modular platform stack, and it places ETN near the top of the electrical-OEM ladder with the OEM tier **sold out and rationing slots** — an unambiguously positive order-book backdrop. But **none of it addresses the trimmed FY margin guide**, which remains the live bear case and gets its test at the Q2 print (~late July/early Aug 2026), not from third-party thematic work. The one genuine nuance is on 800V: ETN is a named beneficiary of a transition whose **facility-level architecture is explicitly "not well baked," being worked out design by design** — which both delays standardisation and, read honestly, cuts against modular's core repeatability pitch. Watch the Flexnode **~35% deployment-schedule reduction** claim for a customer proof point.
