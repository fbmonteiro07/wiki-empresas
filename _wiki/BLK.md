## Snapshot

BlackRock is the largest asset manager, with $15.5tn of AUM at 2026-09-30. Its businesses are iShares ETFs, active and index mandates, cash management, technology and data (Aladdin, eFront, Preqin) and, after the GIP (October 2024), Preqin (March 2025) and HPS (July 2025) acquisitions, what management calls a "top five" alternatives platform. The thesis is that a scaled public-markets franchise, plus higher-fee private markets and technology, can keep organic base-fee growth above the 5% target and expand margins. Three tests remain open. First, how much of the 2026 acceleration comes from market beta and the timing of large mandates. Second, whether private-markets fundraising, especially HPS's wealth channel, holds up through private-credit redemption stress. Third, whether AI-infrastructure financing (the AI Infrastructure Partnership's Aligned deal and the Meta Texas venture) becomes recurring fee-paying AUM. [Company Q3 release, 2026-10-14](../BLK/earnings/2026-10-14_blk-ex99_1.htm); [Fink / Small, Q3 call, 2026-10-14](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md); [Small, Q4 2025 call, 2026-01-15](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-01-15.md); Capstone synthesis, 2026-11-02.

**Strong flows, but most of the year's AUM growth came from markets.** Over the last twelve months, AUM grew from $13,464bn to $15,488bn. Net inflows contributed $868.6bn, market moves $1,272.0bn, FX -$81.5bn and realizations -$34.4bn ([release](../BLK/earnings/2026-10-14_blk-ex99_1.htm)). The fee mix is improving. Small sorted roughly $524bn of year-to-date flows (the release shows $526.9bn of total net inflows) into fee bands: about $220bn at 5–10bp, about $270bn at 10–60bp, and the rest above 60bp in private markets and liquid alternatives. On an equivalent day count, the effective fee rate was "approximately flat" quarter on quarter. ETFs posted a sixth straight quarter of double-digit organic base-fee growth: index bond ETFs took in $61bn, precision ETFs $41bn, active ETFs $22bn and digital-asset ETPs $8bn. Institutional index outflows of $27bn were concentrated in low-fee index equity. [Small, Q3 call, 2026-10-14](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md).

**Technology is still growing at double digits, but ACV growth has slowed.** ACV grew 16% organically in FY2025 (31% including Preqin), then 14% in Q1 2026, 15% in Q2 and 12% in Q3, against a long-term commitment of "low- to mid-teens" ([Q4 2025 call](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q4-2025-earnings_2026-01-15.md); [Q1 call](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-04-14.md); [Q2 release, 2026-07-15](../BLK/earnings/2026-07-15_blk-ex99_1.htm); [Q3 call](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md)). Preqin closed on 2025-03-03 for £2.5bn (about $3.2bn) in cash ([FY2025 10-K](../BLK/BLK_10-K_2026-02-25_0001193125-26-071966.html)). Management pitches Aladdin, eFront and Preqin together as the risk and data layer for private credit ("the language of private credit portfolios," Fink, [Q1 call, 2026-04-14](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q1-2026-earnings_2026-04-14.md)).

**Private markets: fundraising is strong, while realizations and wealth liquidity are the offsets.** Q3 private-markets net inflows of $15.3bn came mainly from deployment in infrastructure and private credit, plus the onboarding of a real-estate mandate. Year to date, net inflows were $39.8bn against $20.0bn of realizations. BlackRock raised $25bn in private credit in its first full year with HPS, and HPS earned $110m of performance fees in Q3. On private markets' $20bn of evergreen funds, Small said "we still have redemptions in the queue" and expects the funds to "turn a corner" in Q4 and 1H27 ([Fink / Small, Q3 call](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md)). Private-markets performance fees fell to $191m from $298m. Liquid-alternatives performance fees rose to $409m, an increase the release attributes largely to a single hedge fund whose annual measurement period ends in Q3 ([release](../BLK/earnings/2026-10-14_blk-ex99_1.htm)).

**AI infrastructure has moved from partnership to deployment.** The release says the AI Infrastructure Partnership (AIP) "made its first deployment this quarter." On the call, Fink identified that deployment as the close of the Aligned Data Centers transaction, at "approximately $40 billion enterprise value." In July he had described the deal as bringing together AIP, GIP and MGX in "the largest data center infrastructure transaction ever announced" ([Q2 call, 2026-07-15](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q2-2026-earnings_2026-07-15.md)). Fink also cited the partnership "with Meta on the financing of its planned $14 billion data center in Texas," which combined GIP infrastructure equity with HPS private financing. That matches the Texas Venture terms Meta filed, already recorded in the [compute-deal timeline](themes/ai-compute-deals.md) and on [META](META.md); no new terms were given. Small said AIP deployment contributed to Q3 organic base-fee growth, and that syndication revenue is "incremental," with the goal being fee-paying AUM. Fink said BlackRock is looking at "two more platforms" and has "a very large transaction" in the market. He gave "about $50 billion" of enterprise cost per 1 GW US data center, with 40% chips and 60% power, grid, core and shell (GW basis not specified; do not mix with IT-load GW). [Fink / Small, Q3 call, 2026-10-14](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md). As of 2026-11-02, Aligned has no deal ID in the compute-deal timeline.

**Bull case — the scale franchise compounds while the mix moves up the fee curve.** iShares keeps taking share: active ETF AUM has doubled in a year to more than $160bn, ahead of the $500m revenue ambition, and systematic equity is approaching $500bn of AUM. Q4 typically brings 30–40% of iShares' annual flows. Private markets and technology lift fee rates and recurring revenue, and management targets more than 30% of revenue from those two by 2030. The recurring margin rose 230bp year over year, buybacks were stepped up to at least $550m a quarter, and BofA's Craig Siegenthaler (Buy, $1,320 PO on 17.5x 2028E EPS, relayed by sales) sees the stock de-rated despite above-target growth. [Fink / Small, Q3 call, 2026-10-14](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md); [BofA US 10, sales, 2026-10-01 — Outlook](https://outlook.office365.com/owa/?ItemID=AAMkADllY2NkMmU1LTc3YTEtNGRkMC1hMjBkLWU1NjIzOTY5OTZhMQBGAAAAAAC2MTk9L2QgQLGbcPk%2FhCdiBwDBpLwblpT0TZ%2Bt%2Fvz7Ao6sAAAAAAEMAADBpLwblpT0TZ%2Bt%2Fvz7Ao6sAAWP7JkVAAA%3D&exvsurl=1&viewmodel=ReadMessageItem); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**Bear case — beta, acquisition cost and private-credit liquidity.** About 94% of base fees are long-term AUM that moves with markets. The record margin partly reflects the performance-fee mix (management's own caveat) and a strong tape. The acquisitions carry a visible cost: $564m of Q3 GAAP charges for contingent consideration (rising with the share price), Subco-unit dilution, and $829m of intangibles amortization year to date. Technology ACV has slowed from 15% to 12%. The private-credit wealth channel, the reason HPS was bought, is in a redemption cycle. AI financing also concentrates exposure to a handful of hyperscaler tenants. [Release](../BLK/earnings/2026-10-14_blk-ex99_1.htm); [Q3 call](../BLK/transcripts/BLK_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-14.md); Capstone interpretation, 2026-11-02.

**What would change the view:** to the upside, organic base-fee growth staying at or above 8% through a flat or down market quarter, with a stable fee rate, or evergreen and HLEND net flows turning positive; to the downside, organic growth falling back toward 5% as large mandates finish funding, ACV growth below 12%, or AIP deployments that add assets without disclosed fee-paying economics. For private-markets read-across, compare [BX](BX.md), [KKR](KKR.md) and [APO](APO.md) only after reconciling FRE and AUM definitions.

## Risks / monitoring

Watch market and FX sensitivity of base fees and the effective fee rate. Other risks include institutional index outflows; performance-fee mix volatility in margins; and GAAP volatility from contingent consideration tied to BlackRock's share price. Watch private-credit and evergreen liquidity (HLEND), plus valuation and credit risk in private-markets assets. AI-infrastructure exposure is concentrated in a few large tenants. The 10-K flags reliance on "a relatively concentrated group of third-party index providers" under long-term contracts, which limits renegotiation (see [MSCI](MSCI.md): BlackRock was 10.8% of MSCI's FY2025 revenue and 19.8% of its Index revenue in 9M 2026, on MSCI's own figures; also [SPGI](SPGI.md)/[NDAQ](NDAQ.md) index licensing). Political and legal risks include state restrictions on firms deemed to be "boycotting" industries, and a lawsuit by thirteen state Attorneys General in the Eastern District of Texas alleging that BlackRock, State Street and Vanguard suppressed coal supply. Also monitor cyber, AI and operational risk on Aladdin, and dilution from the HPS Subco Units.
