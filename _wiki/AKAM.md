## Snapshot
Akamai is the original CDN, now mid-pivot from a legacy content-**Delivery** business (in secular decline) toward two growth pillars: **Security** (Web App Firewall, API Security, Guardicore Segmentation / micro-segmentation, Zero Trust) and **Cloud Compute / Cloud Infrastructure Services (CIS)** built on the Linode acquisition plus a new Akamai Inference Cloud (distributed CPU + NVIDIA GPU at the edge). The platform spans 4,300 locations across 700 cities in 130 countries — the distributed footprint is the central value proposition for both security (intercepting attacks at the edge) and low-latency AI inference (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07). FY2025 revenue $4.208B, +5% YoY; by solution: Security $2.243B (~53%), Delivery $1.257B (~30%, declining), Cloud computing $708M (~17%, of which CIS $314M growing ~36%) (AKAM Q4 2025 PR, 2026-02-19). The core debate: can Security + CIS growth (and now AI-infrastructure mega-deals) outrun the structural erosion of legacy Delivery and re-rate a business that has long been valued as ex-growth.

First UBS mark on this page: Neutral, PT $125. Sentiment 7/10, ~14% implied vol.** **"Once again, 2Q hinges on compute."** The arithmetic of the round trip is laid out precisely: **after the +27% T+1 move on the 1Q26 print — driven by the $1.8bn, 7-year CIS contract with a frontier-model provider "NOW KNOWN TO BE [[ANTHROPIC]]" — shares have pulled back 24%**, which Boyd attributes to investor concern about the CIS ramp trajectory plus the absence of any incremental compute-deal announcements since. ⚠️ **THE ESTIMATE CUT IS THE ACTIONABLE ITEM AND IT IS SHAPE, NOT LEVEL: consensus models CIS c/c growth of 40% / 47% / 70% for 2Q / 3Q / 4Q, i.e. a steady ramp through CY26. Boyd instead thinks "that ramp looks MORE 4Q WEIGHTED, based on management tone intraquarter," and TRIMS his own 2Q CIS growth to 40% — flat with 1Q — from 43%.** So the disagreement with the Street is not about whether the Anthropic contract converts, it is about **when in the year the revenue arrives**, which for a name that re-rated 27% on a single deal announcement is the whole near-term P&L. **Offsetting: "we feel better on the Delivery and Security businesses this quarter"** — the legacy base is doing better while the growth engine is back-loaded, the inverse of the mix the 1Q print rewarded. **The binary framing: "similar to 1Q we would expect ANY LARGE DEAL ANNOUNCEMENTS TO OFFSET what seems like a TOUGHER NUMBERS SETUP"** — i.e. the print can be carried by a headline rather than by the model, which is a poor basis for owning it and is why the rating is Neutral. ⚠️ **A new margin/cost datapoint that bears directly on the CIS unit economics this page has flagged as unproven: Boyd cites a "55% INCREASE IN RETAIL PRICE ON [[NVDA]]'s RTX PRO 6000 in June" as a live profitability concern** — the GPU-cost inflation running through the wider complex lands on Akamai's inference-serving cost base too, and Akamai is a price-taker on it. **Boyd's net is patience rather than a view: "we're OPTIMISTIC on the CIS story EXITING CY26 as new capacity comes online while the rest of the business stabilizes, but with the ramp potentially taking A COUPLE MORE QUARTERS to unfold, and profitability concerns lingering, we're comfortable STAYING PATIENT with shares at 4x CY27E EV/S."** ⚠️ **Note this is the first sell-side source to state on this page that the frontier-model counterparty on the $1.8bn deal is Anthropic as established fact rather than inference — cross-filed to [[ANTHROPIC]].** UBS positioning tracker does not list AKAM in the Software buckets. - **Bull:** The story has shifted from a legacy delivery network to a credible **AI-infrastructure platform**, and the inflection is now visible in real bookings, not narrative. CIS is inflecting at +40–45% YoY with the $1.8B/7-yr and $200M wins validating distributed AI demand; mix-shift (CIS + Security growing, Delivery stabilizing) should drive faster total growth and eventually margins — management guides to **double-digit total growth in 2027** (BofA on AKAM, 2026-05-13; AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07). The distributed-edge architecture (4,300 locations) is a genuine differentiator vs centralized hyperscalers/neoclouds for low-latency inference, and Akamai wins on latency + ability to secure data-center space/power on good credit (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07). Security is a structural beneficiary of AI: CEO Leighton says he's "never seen the CISOs more agitated" — AI-powered attacks and unpatchable legacy devices are driving urgency, with WAF re-accelerating and API/Guardicore still low-penetration (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07). Strong FCF generation funds the buildout and buybacks (FY2025 OCF $1.519B / 36% margin) (AKAM Q4 2025 PR, 2026-02-19).

The CIS mega-deals are lower-margin (management said it's willing to do large deals at potentially **sub-30% operating margin**) and depreciation-heavy, so the model becomes more capital-intensive and reliant on a small number of large frontier-model customers — concentration risk. Legacy **Delivery is in secular decline** (−7% YoY, ~30% of revenue) and management itself frames it as a cash cow to "harvest" to fund compute, not a growth engine — even agentic/AI traffic isn't expected to "make a huge swing in the amount of bits" delivered absent video generation (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07). The compute pivot also pits AKAM directly against hyperscalers and neoclouds ("every deal is competitive") with a far smaller balance sheet, and may eventually require external capital if mega-deals keep landing (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07).

## Risks
From the FY2025 10-K (AKAM 10-K FY2025) plus the debate above:
- **Slowing / flat / limited revenue growth** has hurt and may continue to hurt profitability and the stock (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **Cloud-computing execution dependency** — an increasing share of revenue depends on successfully building the compute platform, developing AI capabilities, and attracting a customer base that traditionally partnered elsewhere; "we may not be successful in our artificial intelligence initiatives" (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **Competition** — hyperscalers/larger rivals can expand points of presence, secure server components (incl. memory), co-location space and power on preferred/priority terms — constraining supply and raising AKAM's costs (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **DIY / insourcing** — large customers shifting to in-house delivery or security would hurt the business (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **Capital intensity & indebtedness** — heavier capex and convertible debt increase vulnerability to adverse conditions and could limit future financing if cash generation weakens (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **New-solution development risk** — failure to develop/acquire attractive new solutions could hurt results (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **Global operations, tax, regulatory and integration (acquisitions)** risks; defects/disruptions in products and IT systems; dependence on third-party software (AKAM 10-K FY2025).
- **Customer concentration** in CIS mega-deals (a few frontier-model customers driving disproportionate compute revenue/capex) (AKAM Q1 2026 call, 2026-05-07).
