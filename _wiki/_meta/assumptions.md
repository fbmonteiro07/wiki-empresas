## EUV tools per fab & leading-edge capex intensity (per 10kwpm)  `euv-fab-intensity`

**Canonical:** N2 logic (Bristlemoon, Jun-2026): ~11 EUV tools per 25kwpm fab and ~US$4.7B TOTAL fab capex per 10kwpm, built on real EUV throughput ~1/3 of nameplate (NXE:3800E 220->165 wph @40mJ dose x 45% uptime -> 34% OEE -> 2,430 wpm/tool) and 25kwpm as the modern module size (no longer ~40kwpm); capex stack: tool $240M -> EUV 80% of litho -> litho 40% of WFE -> WFE 70% of fab total. ## AI compute revenue / rental rate per GW  `rev-per-gw`

_Annualized $ revenue (or rental rate) per GW of AI compute capacity — state chip type (ASIC vs merchant GPU) and whether it is a rental rate or realized revenue. Distinct from facility BUILD cost per GW._

**Canonical:** Rental/revenue per GW of compute: Trainium <$10B/GW (Anthropic/OpenAI); merchant GPU ~$12-13B/GW (neocloud & AWS, pre-2H26 shortage); premium GPU ~$25B/GW (~$25M/MW/yr, SpaceX–Google deal). Colocation POWER-ONLY rent: ~$60/kW-month historically -> ~$120-160 typical now, up to ~$200 (weak-credit tenant + good DC), as low as ~$80-100 (India / mid DC / poor grid). Rev/GW is a data-center-QUALITY metric: credit, utilization, networking and speed-to-market drive a ~2x spread — a top-operator GW ≈ 2x a generic GW. NEW (08-28): lab INFERENCE MONETISATION ~$60-100bn/GW/yr (Baker/a16z) — a distinct, much larger basis than the rental rates; implies ~1yr REVENUE payback on a ~$50bn/GW build. ## Wafers per GW of AI compute — node split (N3 / N5 / DRAM)  `wafers-per-gw`

_300mm wafer starts needed to BUILD 1 GW of AI compute, split by node/type. A one-time wafer BILL per GW, not a standing monthly capacity — never mix with the WSPM cards. Every figure must carry (a) its rack denominator (racks/GW and the kW/rack behind it), (b) its GW basis (compute-TDP / IT-load vs facility), and (c) whether it is logic-only (foundry) or all-wafer (logic + DRAM + NAND)._

**Canonical:** Rack denominator for VR200 is FIRM at ~4,440-4,545 racks/GW on an IT-load/compute-TDP basis — TrendForce ~225kW/rack (2026-06-28) and Bernstein 220kW compute-TDP (2026-07-20) agree independently. On that denominator, 1 GW of Vera Rubin ≈ 230-295k total 300mm wafers: DRAM 141-205k (house bottom-up; central ~155-186k) and foundry logic 25-30k (house bottom-up) vs Patel's 61k. The DRAM leg is well-anchored; the LOGIC leg is the live dispute — Patel's 55k N3 + 6k N5 is ~2x a die-area bottom-up of the NVL144 rack BOM and ~2.9x Fubon's Blackwell card (33k TSMC wafers/GW at 7,200 NVL72 racks = 4.6 wafers/rack).

> ⚠️ **Scope:** THREE traps. (1) Rack denominator: Blackwell GB300 ~150kW => 7,200 racks/GW (Fubon) vs VR200 ~220-225kW => ~4,440-4,545 racks/GW vs Redburn's FACILITY-GW frame (PUE 1.15, 90% to GPU racks, 250kW/rack) => 3,130 racks/GW. Wafers/GW scales inversely with kW/rack. (2) JPM's VR200 'rack design power' of 330-444kW is NAMEPLATE, not draw — do not build a rack count on it.
