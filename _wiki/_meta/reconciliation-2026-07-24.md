# Reconciliation — run-inbox 2026-07-24 (SemiAnalysis batch, 10 files)

Variance pass on the NEW **quantitative** datapoints from this run — a 10-file SemiAnalysis batch (4 genuinely new, 6 re-emitted duplicates handled as a dedup pass) — against **(1)** prior wiki comments, **(2)** Capstone house models (`_data/house.json`, asof 2026-07-24), **(3)** **BBG consensus — Terminal LIVE**, pulled 2026-07-24 (`PX_LAST`, `BEST_TARGET_PRICE`, `BEST_EPS`, `BEST_SALES`, `BEST_CAPEX` with `BEST_FPERIOD_OVERRIDE` 1/2/3FY).

**Character of this batch:** SemiAnalysis is an engineering/supply-chain shop, not a ratings shop — there are **no PTs, no EPS estimates and no ratings in the entire batch**. So the reconcilable quantities are *unit economics, content-per-box, power, financing spreads and deal economics*, and the variance pass is against what consensus **implicitly assumes** rather than against a competing number. That makes several of the divergences below unusually clean: consensus has a number, SemiAnalysis has the mechanism that breaks it, and nobody has yet reconciled the two.
