## 🔭 Deltas

1. **TSM 2027-28 gross margin.** Haas Liu says the Street at ~69-70% is "2 percentage above our expectations", which implies ~67-68%. JPM agrees (67-68%, 10-01). The BBG snapshot is already at 66.5% for CY27, so BofA's "Street" is its own poll, not the BBG mean. The house bridge treats 70% in 2027 as conditional.
2. **TSM revenue.** BofA's base case is a 35% CAGR through 2028 vs the company's 25% guide. The house bridge works out to ~33%, close to BofA.
3. **A14 capacity.** BofA's full phase is 20-25k wpm (10k by 1Q/2Q28), below JPM (30-40k by end-28) and below the house bridge (30/60k at EOY27/28).
4. **N3/N2 peak capacity.** BofA has N3 at 250-300k wpm and N2 at 200-250k wpm in 2028-29, above the house bridge (N3 220k, N2/A16 210k at EOY28).
5. **TPU v9 CoWoS backup is still live.** Haas Liu dates the substrate-yield test to end-1Q27, with a 3-6 month window and "meaningful downside risk" to MediaTek's 2028 TPU v9 shipments. This conflicts with JPM 10-01 ("no CoWoS path") and with MediaTek via MS 09-30.
6. **ASML Foundry/Logic 2026 growth cut from 25% to 10%.** This is Scemama relaying ASML's 10-14 call. ASML's 2026 is memory-led; TSMC is "not the biggest driver" and Intel is ~zero.
7. **TSMC 4Q capex.** $41bn has been spent of the $60-65bn guide, and the ~$20bn remainder is construction-weighted, not WFE (Liu and Arya). Do not read a big 4Q capex print as a 4Q WFE beat.
8. **Tool inflation from the vendor side.** Front-end +10-20% and back-end +5-15% (Haas Liu, SEMICON Taiwan checks).
9. **Intel.** External 14A customers arrive '29-'30 (Arya). The Street's packaging range of $2-3bn → $4-5bn sits below UBS's EMIB-T model.
10. **Neoclouds.** Neocloud capex is "<10-15%" of global AI capex but is the market's "weakest link" (Arya). Broader AI spend grows +40-45% over 2026-30 in BofA's model.
11. **IFX.** 75% of Infineon's automotive division is fabless (Scemama's figure, not a company disclosure). Lagging-edge foundry prices rise 5-10%, which he frames as a pricing pass-through.
12. **BofA's own drift on TSM GM.** In April BofA called for "another round of expansion" in 2027; now it sees a "mild upward trend", below the Street.
