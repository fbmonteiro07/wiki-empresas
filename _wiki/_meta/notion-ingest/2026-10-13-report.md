## 🔭 Deltas
1. **Scale-up optics timing.** He has copper/PCB (MEGTRON, Kyber midplane) "till well into 2032", with optics in-package only from ≥4 reticle dies ('28-'29). That is later than Hurlston (LITE, 10-01: scale-up CPO/NPO in 2027), UBS (GPU-CPO ~2029E), NVDA IR via Citi (Feynman/2028 option) and COHR (scale-up CPO 2H CY27). It agrees with 650 Group (~2031) and MS (Feynman/2029+).
2. **CPO adoption.** He sees CPO as switch-only, with a real ramp in 2H27-'28, top layers first, and broad rollout "well into 2035". He calls the fear that CPO wipes out ~$20bn/yr of transceivers "just not true". That is a slow burn, bullish for pluggables (AAOI, the LITE/COHR transceiver lines) and less bearish for merchant DSPs (MRVL/AVGO/CRDO).
3. **CRDO AEC.** He puts AECs at ">$1,200 / 50 W / 100-200 ns". The wiki's own division of Jefferies' model implies ~$280 average ASP, so his figure is ~4x. His reach is 2 m at 200G and gone at 400G, vs CRDO management's 1.6T "up to 6.5 m" and UBS's 200G/6 m. Not reconciled.
4. **Kyber.** He describes Kyber as live and all-copper. The pages carry cancelled (MS desk 08-18), pushed out (Jefferies 08-20) and "isn't working" (Fubon via Jefferies 09-22). Left unresolved.
5. **APH.** In-rack cables go away, but he names Amphenol, TE and Molex as the PCB-era connector winners. That splits the JPM bear tenet: cable content is at risk, connector content is not.
6. **COHR VCSELs.** He sees a short-reach and storage niche (multimode ~30 m) and "I don't see VCSELs replacing CPO for a long time". This leans against the page's VCSEL scale-up optimism.
7. **NPO.** He gives it a "limited life" at large switch chips (modules the size of "hot dogs"), against the theme's "NPO is additive" framing (08-17).
8. **ANET.** Arista is absent from his list of CPO-switch players, while Cisco is on it. This is one expert's list.
