## Consolidated findings

**Meta (S2).**
- The capex range of $135-145bn repeats the Q3 guide.
- **NEW:** "we expect that investment to grow in 2027". This is the first FY27 capex direction; there is still no number, and it is due at the 4Q26 call.
- Equity is now named as "another complementary source of capital". On 10-21 Li said "no specific financing plans", so this is a change.
- The Muse ceiling is now company-stated: "ARPOs similar to our core business" plus "search monetization markets".
- MTIA Artemis is described as "roughly twice the performance per dollar" (company expectation, no comparator given).
- BlackRock/Blue Owl JVs and Class C were logged as confirmations on ai-compute-deals. No new deal.

**UBS SemiBytes (S3).**
- **SMH math:** at a 21x median, the SMH implies 2028 EPS of $25.23 vs Street $42. Against UBS's 70 GW installed in 2028, the market is pricing 50-52 GW, a ~20 GW cut. The AI-lab 20%/30% compute shares behind that comparison are NVIDIA's, relayed.
- **Server-CPU TAM:** $175bn → $210bn, but it is memory-bound. Unconstrained demand is "like $500bn". He also said "3-4x", which does not reconcile with the $500bn.
- **MU:** PT $1,625. Spoken downside $700-750, below the "$800 floor" he gave on 10-19. "Eventually $2,000" is explicitly not his PT. Book value of $360-400 exiting 2028; gross margin 50-55% even at −80% pricing.
- **Equipment:** LRCX/AMAT are his top equipment buys at 13-14x on a $300bn 2028 WFE.
- **NVDA:** at $215, EPS growing "50%+".
- **AMD:** Dec-Q bogey $17bn.
- **INTC:**
  - Gross margin "sandbagged": pro forma 45-46% vs a 42% guide.
  - 2027 capex $30bn with an upside bias.
  - TSMC may curtail Intel wafers over Terafab.
- **QCOM:** skews to the downside.
- **MCHP/TXN:** prefers TI over MCHP.
- **Credit inbounds:** he took 10-15 calls from credit analysts. He called the AVGO financing vehicle "one-time", and said NVIDIA and the neoclouds are "almost the same company".

**UBS credit (S1).**
- **Keirstead (pricing):** CoreWeave's next-year capacity is "priced out at $15bn per GW", vs "$11bn" currently. AWS is "de facto sold out through end-2027" (relaying Jassy). CoreWeave raised prices +25% in July and again by 10% in September.
- **Keirstead (CoreWeave funding):** prepays, equity and throttling could cut 2027 external debt to "a couple of billion dollars". This matches UBS Scenario 4 from 10-23.
- **Richardson:**
  - Spread moves since June: Google's own bonds +10-20bp, HY project bonds leased to Google-backed tenants +40-200bp.
  - CoreWeave-backed project bonds have widened far less than CoreWeave's unsecured bonds.
- **Sady (desk):** prefers Cipher, Stingray and TeraWulf. Meridian and Tract D were sold by hedge funds in the low-to-mid 6s.
- **Mish:**
  - Sizes 2027 at about $1.8tn of AI capex, $1.3tn of it from hyperscalers.
  - Expects the debt share to rise from the mid-30s% to 40-50%, and issuance of ~$600bn+.
  - Says UBS's US credit strategy is underweight tech and sees a possible Q4/January squeeze.
- **Gravett:** about half of capacity is self-built (20 GW vs 30 GW leased; basis ambiguous). EQIX and DLR each need $3-4bn.
- **Keirstead (ORCL):** a financing plan is expected at Oracle's 10-28 investor day, per the CFO.
- Spoken spread moves were never netted against the AI_Credit_Live workbook. The workbook's own CRWV levels are cited alongside with their basis.
