## Calendar defects & missing catalysts (for the next parser pass)

🔴 **The parser cannot see paragraph-form catalysts.** `build_catalysts.py` only walks lines matching `^\s*[-*]\s+`; a bold paragraph is appended to the *previous* bullet, or dropped when it comes first. **Cost this window: ORCL is absent from the calendar entirely (0 rows) even though its FQ1 FY27 print — the page's own *"single biggest scheduled event"* — is tomorrow.** The same failure hides **KIOXIA 09-14**, **CRM Dreamforce / Investor Day 09-15-17**, **TOKYOELEC 09-17**, **FSLY 09-22**, **QCOM 09-22/24**, and every "CONFERENCE WEEK" header. **Fix: treat a bolded line beginning with an emoji or a date as a bullet.**
