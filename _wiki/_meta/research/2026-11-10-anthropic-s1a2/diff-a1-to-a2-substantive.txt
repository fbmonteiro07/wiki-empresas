Our development of highly advanced models, platforms, and applications and expansion of use cases could further increase the risk that our models cause harm. For example, agentic SI systems are capable of autonomously taking actions on behalf of users, interacting directly with external systems, executing code, making API calls, sending communications, or completing transactions with limited human oversight. These systems are designed to possess enterprise-level permissions, integrations, and contextual awareness and to operate autonomously for extended durations, including sustained multi-step workflows lasting hours or days, which amplifies the potential severity of any errors, misalignments, or security incidents that may occur during such extended autonomous operation.
