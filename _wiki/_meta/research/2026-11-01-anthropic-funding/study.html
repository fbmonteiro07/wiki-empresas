What does another $100bn buy Anthropic?

It covers about 17% of the identified June commitment subtotal, but more than twice the modeled 2027–28 net cash draw. In FUNDA's base case, replacing its $60bn IPO assumption with $100bn leaves a year-end cash trough of approximately $190bn in 2028. A prolonged revenue stall still needs further financing.

Assumption: $100bn of additional net cash proceeds received by December 2026, on top of cash already reported in June. This is a conditional adaptation of an attributed independent-research model, with an independent check of the filing and cash basis. The original excerpt's source remains unconfirmed.

The contract

HARD • Anthropic S-1, p.172 Up to $120.5bn over terms extending into 2029; approximately $3.1bn per month once all capacity is delivered, expected December 2026. Initial non-cancellable periods end in January and March 2027. Each agreement can then be cancelled by either party on 90 days' advance notice.

The three-month calculation is an approximate service-payment window, not a disclosed termination penalty. Full delivery and uniform fees are assumed.

4. Checks and investment interpretation

PASS • basis Stocks compared with stocks; annual cash flows kept separate. Finance-lease principal included once. Restricted cash and undrawn funding excluded from base resources.

PARTIAL • disclosure September cash and the updated annual contractual maturity table are blank. A precise legal obligation coverage ratio through 2030 cannot be established from this filing.

ESTIMATE • growth The base case reaches $438.4bn annual revenue in 2030. This is a FUNDA assumption. The funding adjustment is exact arithmetic conditional on that operating path.

FAIL • stable cost ratio Q2 non-compute adjusted costs calibrate to (10.581 − 5.558 − 1.261) / 11.555 = 32.6% of revenue. The independent Q1 period gives (6.398 − 3.387 − 0.924 − 0.126 noncash charity) / 4.731 = 41.5%, a relative difference above 25%. A fixed future cost ratio is not validated; no such ratio is used to manufacture this cash path.

SKEPTIC • PARTIAL Independent audit: source transcription and arithmetic PASS; forward cash sufficiency remains an unvalidated FUNDA scenario. The mismatched commitment denominator was removed; cancellation savings exclude lost contribution and transition costs; the $100bn replaces $60bn once. Audit.

Google's $20bn and Amazon's $15bn remaining financing capacity are conditional. Broadcom's $42bn note facility is restricted to specified rent payments and subject to revenue/liquidity conditions. The $15bn revolver is facility capacity, not unrestricted cash already in the bank. These sources can help, but are least reliable as unconditional buffers in the stall case.

Investment interpretation: raising $100bn materially reduces Anthropic's financing risk across the published growth scenarios. It does not prefund every compute commitment or prove that the spending earns an attractive return. For SpaceX, a well-funded customer can still exit or renegotiate when cheaper dedicated capacity arrives; customer solvency and contract durability are separate questions.
