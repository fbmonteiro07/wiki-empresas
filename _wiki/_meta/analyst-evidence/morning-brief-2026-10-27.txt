FIVE PRIORITIES

1. CLS — Strong growth, weaker cash conversion: interrogate the gap today.
Company evidence, October 26: Q3 revenue $5.83bn and adjusted EPS $3.30 beat prior guidance. Initial FY27 outlook is $40bn revenue, $21.50 adjusted EPS and 9% adjusted operating margin. Yet FY26 free-cash-flow guidance falls from $600m to $460m. JPM / Joseph Cardoso et al., October 26, flags the absence of FY27 FCF guidance and working-capital demands. [Celestica results](https://corporate.celestica.com/node/17881)
Inference/action: use today’s call to bridge inventory, receivables, customer advances and capex into cash. The release describes FY28 customer forecasts, not firm backlog; do not capitalize them as secured revenue.
Falsifier: growing revenue and EPS without working-capital normalization or adequate funding would undermine the quality-of-growth thesis.
Signal: supp-20261027-cls. Evidence: E, idea_evidence.cls. No CLS company page exists; FLEX is a peer read-through, not a substitute model.

2. META — The funding plan is real; “$95bn cash raised” is not.
Company evidence, October 26: Meta launched approximately $35bn of underwritten offerings and plans a $60bn Class C ATM. Crucially, the $22.5bn common-stock component uses forward sales: no initial proceeds to Meta. The ATM is not yet contracted; supplier purchase indications are nonbinding. [Meta offering announcement](https://investor.atmeta.com/investor-news/press-release-details/2026/Meta-Announces-Launch-of-Offering-of-Depositary-Shares-Representing-Series-A-Mandatory-Convertible-Preferred-Stock-and-Offering-of-Class-A-Common-Stock-in-Connection-with-Forward-Sale-Agreements/default.aspx)
Broker interpretation: BofA / Justin Post and Nitin Bansal, October 26, sees the financing size as a warning of higher capex and estimates roughly 6% EPS dilution under its assumed issuance scenario—not a settled company outcome.
Inference/action: model cash receipts by settlement date separately from dilution, preferred dividends and future capex. Raising equity reduces reliance on debt but does not establish investment returns.
Falsifier: funded proceeds, disciplined capex and monetization sufficient to stabilize cash generation would weaken the continuing-financing-risk thesis.
Signal: supp-20261027-meta. Evidence: E, idea_evidence.meta; R; _wiki/themes/ai-compute-deals.md, META-CLASSC-FIN-20261021.

3. AVGO — Financing progress supports execution, but risk remains with the supplier.
Company/bank evidence, October 26: the $63bn Anthropic financing SPV has committed financing and expected deliveries starting CY27; Broadcom supplies residual-value support equivalent to the first-lien financing. This supports an existing compute agreement, not another $63bn of new demand. Closing and drawdown were not established by the release. [Broadcom announcement](https://investors.broadcom.com/news-releases/news-release-details/bank-america-blackstone-citi-and-morgan-stanley-announce)
Broker estimate: UBS / Timothy Arcuri et al., October 26, puts aggregate exposure across vehicles at approximately $70–75bn. That is not immediate cash outflow, a company-confirmed aggregate liability, or an amount to add to the financing headline.
Inference/action: reconcile each vehicle’s support, amortization, collateral recovery and possible cash-call timing before lifting confidence in backlog conversion.
Falsifier: delayed financing/deliveries or impaired collateral values requiring substantial support payments would defeat the straightforward de-risking thesis.
Signal: supp-20261027-avgo. Evidence: E, idea_evidence.avgo; _wiki/themes/ai-compute-deals.md, ANTH-AVGO-CHIP-FIN-20261005.

4. CDNS — Evidence against AI displacement; not yet proof of a new growth acceleration.
Company evidence, October 26: recurring revenue grew 18% y/y; backlog reached $8.2bn; FY26 non-GAAP EPS guidance rose to $8.40 at midpoint. JPM / Harlan Sur et al., October 27 email, raises PT $405→$420 and models CY27 EPS $9.85. [Cadence results](https://investor.cadence.com/news/news-details/2026/Cadence-Reports-Third-Quarter-2026-Financial-Results/default.aspx)

Inference/action: test paid agent adoption and underlying EDA consumption on an acquisition-normalized basis. Strong total revenue and customer engagements do not by themselves establish incremental recurring monetization.
Falsifier: agent adoption rises while normalized recurring growth and paid engine consumption stagnate.
Signal: supp-20261027-cdns. Evidence: E, idea_evidence.cdns; _wiki/CDNS.md; CDNS/transcripts/CDNS_Q2-2026-earnings_2026-07-27.md.
