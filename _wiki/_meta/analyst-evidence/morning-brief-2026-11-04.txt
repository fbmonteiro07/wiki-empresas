1. AMD — Estimates up does not necessarily mean multiple up.

Hard evidence: AMD’s November 3 release guides Q4 revenue to $17bn ±3% and non-GAAP gross margin to 55%. That clears the newer $16.5–16.9bn desk hurdle in Capstone’s November 2 preview, but only matches the older UBS $17bn bar. [AMD release](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1303/amd-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results)

Broker evidence: UBS / Timothy Arcuri and Seth Cho, November 4, raises CY2028 EPS from $26.94 to $29.83 but cuts its target from $730 to $700 as the multiple falls from 27x to 23x. JPM / Harlan Sur and Apoorva Kumar, November 4, remains Neutral; its quoted Street Q4 gross margin is 55.5%. UBS/JPM relay management’s >80% CY2027 revenue-growth outlook; this is call guidance relayed by brokers, not an independently reviewed transcript.

Inference/action: prioritize the server-CPU/GPU/Client revenue and margin bridge. Do not transplant CPU margins into the GPU ramp or mistake higher EPS for a higher valuation.
Falsifier: Helios/server delivery slippage or mix/input costs undermine the guided margin and operating leverage.
Signal supp-20261104-amd. Evidence: E.amd; AMD/AMD_3Q26_Preview_2026-11-02.html; _wiki/AMD.md.

2. ANET — The initial guide clears the stronger hurdle; now test conversion.

Hard evidence: Arista’s November 3 release guides Q4 revenue to $3.9–4.0bn. JPM / Joseph Cardoso, Marc Vitenzon and Manmohanpreet Singh, November 4, relays management’s initial CY2027 growth guide of 38–40%, approximately $7bn AI-fabric revenue and 62–64% gross margin. That exceeds JPM’s November 2 favorable threshold of at least 35%. [Arista release](https://www.arista.com/en/company/news/press-release/25025-pr-20261103)

Counterevidence: JPM also reports a sequential decline in deferred revenue, which management attributes to healthy conversion. Scale-up trials in 2027 and production in 2028 are not interchangeable revenue dates.

Inference/action: obtain the deferred-revenue bridge—opening balance, releases and new additions—and customer acceptance/supply milestones before upgrading the whole growth curve. Reconcile the call’s $220bn-plus 2030 TAM with JPM’s November 2 $340bn-plus expectation; scope differences must be checked before declaring a market contraction.
Falsifier: releases are not replenished by orders, or component costs break the gross-margin framework.
Signal supp-20261104-anet. Evidence: E.anet; _wiki/ANET.md.

Adjusted revenue was $9.793bn versus GAAP $8.893bn, reflecting the $900m FTC revenue reduction. Do not compare those bases indiscriminately. [Company release, unaltered mirror](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/booking-reports-third-quarter-2026-financial-results-ce785fdedc81f720)

Broker evidence: BofA / Post and Gupta, November 4, keeps Buy, cuts FY2027 EPS from $12.23 to $12.16 and its target from $232 to $219. JPM / Doug Anmuth and Dae K Lee, November 4, says AI referrals remain well below 1% of room nights despite approximately 2x conversion among AI users.

Inference/action: separate macro disruption from distribution-share loss. AI-user conversion is a selected cohort, not proof of incremental bookings or lower acquisition cost.
Falsifier of caution: forward nights recover alongside demonstrably incremental, profitable AI bookings.
Signal supp-20261104-bkng; prior catalyst cata-3309c26d3f2a. Evidence: E.bkng; _wiki/BKNG.md.
