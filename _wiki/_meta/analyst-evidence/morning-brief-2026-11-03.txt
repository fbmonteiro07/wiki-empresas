BofA / Wamsi Mohan, November 3, nonetheless raises FY2027 EPS $9.12→$9.15; UBS / David Vogt’s November 2 note cuts $9.58→$9.55. Hardware strength and margin pressure are pulling forecasts in opposite directions.

Falsifier: sustained Services reacceleration and underlying margin resilience offset those costs.
