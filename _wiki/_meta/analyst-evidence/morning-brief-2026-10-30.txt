FIVE PRIORITIES

1. AMZN — Buy-side expectations missed; longer-term growth visibility improved.
Company evidence, October 29: AWS grew 39.3%, operating income was $28.1bn and trailing-twelve-month FCF was negative $42.4bn. AWS operating margin was 38.7% (also reported by JPM / Doug Anmuth et al., October 30). Against the BofA / Justin Post October 25 benchmarks frozen in yesterday’s brief, AWS missed the 40% model and potential 42%+ investor hurdle; margin missed 39%. CEO Andy Jassy nevertheless projects AWS growth above 40% in both 2027 and 2028. Q4 operating-income guidance is $28.5–33.5bn. [Company release](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results/default.aspx)
Inference/action: investigate a long-term earnings opportunity, but demand a quarterly funding bridge before calling the selloff irrational. Separate cloud consumption, future chip sales, depreciation and financing costs; backlog is not cash collected.
Falsifier: AWS fails to accelerate as capacity arrives, or investment consistently outruns incremental operating cash generation.
Signal: supp-20261030-amzn. Evidence: E.amzn; _wiki/AMZN.md; _wiki/_meta/reconciliation-2026-10-29-inbox.md.

2. INTC — A stronger server market is not enough to rescue the near-term equity case.
Company call, October 29: Q4 revenue guidance $15–16bn and non-GAAP gross margin 38%; management plans for PC unit demand down roughly 10% in 2027 and does not expect positive FCF next year. Wafer/substrate constraints remain tighter in the first half; capacity relief is expected later.
Revision: BofA / Vivek Arya, October 30, downgrades Buy→Neutral, cuts PT $145→$100 and CY27 EPS 16% to $1.69. JPM / Harlan Sur et al., October 30, stays Neutral and cuts PT $85→$80.
Inference/action: distinguish the temporary Fab 28 disruption from PC demand destruction and persistent server supply constraints. Test how much recovery survives without a foundry valuation premium. Do not assume stronger industry demand means Intel gains share.
Falsifier of the cautious view: PC inventory clears faster, server constraints ease earlier, and a named external foundry customer supplies credible revenue milestones.
Signals: supp-20261030-intc; cata-c333d9a79742. Evidence: E.intc; INTC/transcripts/INTC_Q3-2026-earnings_2026-10-29.md.

3. SNDK — Strong delivery, but brokers disagree on earnings durability.
Company release, October 29: F1Q27 revenue $11.031bn and non-GAAP EPS $49.51; F2Q revenue guidance $12.2–12.6bn and EPS $55–57. All EPS figures here are pre-split. The eight-for-one split begins adjusted trading November 20. [Sandisk release](https://www.sandisk.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/2026-10-29-sandisk-reports-fiscal-first-quarter-2027-financial-results)
Real disagreement, October 30: JPM / Harlan Sur et al. raises CY27 EPS $250→$260, maintaining its $2,250 PT. BofA / Wamsi Mohan cuts CY27 EPS $255→$251 and PT $2,500→$2,000, with its multiple falling 10x→8x. These are pre-split broker vintages, not directly comparable to future split-adjusted screens.
Inference/action: reconcile end-market mix, bit allocation and contract pricing—not just the headline beat. JPM says the two NBMs closed this quarter were already announced previously; unchanged commitments are not new wins.
Falsifier: non-datacenter demand destruction or additional supply overwhelms datacenter strength, while the NBM pipeline fails to become incremental signed commitments.
Signal: supp-20261030-sndk. Evidence: E.sndk; _wiki/SNDK.md.

4. AAPL — Prepare for a September beat that fails to protect the December/March outlook.
Broker forecast, not guidance: BofA / Wamsi Mohan, October 30, keeps Buy/$370 but cuts FY27 iPhone units by 9m to 247m versus quoted Street 260m. It estimates March-quarter total-company gross margin of 43.6–44.6% versus Street 47.7%, reflecting memory costs after pricing/mix offsets. Staggered launches also shift revenue between quarters.
Inference/action: prioritize a quarter-by-quarter unit/ASP/margin bridge. Separate tariff refunds and inventory-cost timing from sustainable margins; test the licensing exposure to weaker Google Search. A favorable September print alone would not disprove these forward risks.
Falsifier: pricing, premium mix and procurement protection preserve margins while launch-adjusted demand holds.
Catalyst: November 2 after close, per BofA’s October 30 preview; Apple’s live IR page did not expose the calendar in the retrieved text.
Signals: supp-20261030-aapl; esti-ad66a4ac7201. Evidence: E.aapl; _wiki/AAPL.md; _wiki/_data/house.json and estimates.json. The engine flag uses the June 16 house model, not an overnight upgrade.

5. AIXA — Fresh optical-equipment long candidate; verify conversion and cash quality.
Company release, October 29: total orders EUR274.9m, equipment backlog EUR549.4m, and optoelectronics 68% of equipment orders. FY26 revenue guidance remains EUR560m ± EUR30m. Cash flow benefited materially from customer advances, so it is not a clean recurring-earnings proxy. [Aixtron release](https://aixtron.com/en/investors/news/AI-driven%20demand%20fuels%20strong%20order%20momentum_n6508801)

Inference/action: test backlog shipment dates, cancellation protection, customer concentration and the advance-payment unwind. Improving GaN demand does not establish a broad SiC recovery.
Falsifier: orders fail to convert, customer expansion pauses, or cash conversion reverses materially as advances unwind.
Signal: supp-20261030-aixa. Evidence: E.aixa; _wiki/AIXA.md; _wiki/_meta/outcomes.md. The missing-outcome alert post-e11128f74409 is now resolved.
