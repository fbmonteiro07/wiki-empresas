**5. Theme read-throughs**
- **ai-datacenter-power:** Austin put the DC MEP wallet at "$15 million a meg". PWR's capture was "$3-4mn" a year ago, is "$5-7mn" now and is heading to "$7-10mn". The MW basis is not stated, and this is not generation $/MW. He also said "we can't even get to 25%-30% of what they have in front of us."
- **GEV:** NiSource power-plant scope is now in PWR's backlog. Austin cited GE's supply-chain "criticality" and engine investments "three years out", which ties to the GS/GEV point that the turbine is about one-third of plant cost.
- **ETN:** PWR calls itself a "top five buyer of HV equipment". It is self-manufacturing transformers, breakers and poles across "10 states", which means partial disintermediation of electrical OEMs.
- **VRT:** Austin said "Almost every OEM's expanding" and "Our growth will be dependent on the OEMs." He said AI "learning centers" (training sites) "may slow a little" but are "very little of our work"; cloud and inference are the majority.
- **Theme (transmission):** 765 kV bookings come in 1H27, with Texas and Ohio first.
