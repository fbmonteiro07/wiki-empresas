**6. 🔭 Variant perception.**
- The ">35% CAGR" headline is base-year arithmetic ($225bn on FY26's $67.4bn), not a raise.
- The $20bn cap moves funding to channels outside the cap (vendor financing, customer hardware, prepayments, refinancing). The binding test is <3.5x on management's own lease-inclusive net debt, not the $20bn.
- FY29 FCF of $30bn+ is ~10x GS's +$2.8bn FY29E. The FCF definition is not given; prepayments sit in operating cash flow, and vendor-financed equipment may bypass cash capex.
