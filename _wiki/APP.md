## Snapshot
AppLovin is a mobile-advertising and AI-technology platform operating the AXON AI engine for performance marketing (app-install and eCommerce ads). The investment debate centers on whether APP can sustain its AI-driven ad-growth engine, expand into eCommerce monetization (the "next leg"), and whether the META bear case (META competing directly in non-IDFA mobile inventory) has structurally faded. The stock has repeatedly been flagged a high-beta "dislocated beyond megacap" name squeezing alongside RDDT in May–Jun 2026.

## Current state (latest quarter — Q2 2026, print 2026-08-05 AMC)

**🆕🔴 Q2 2026 PRINT (2026-08-05 AMC) — THE FIRST MISS IN FOREVER. Stock −16% AH / −18–22%. Every house calls it model-release TIMING rather than structure, and every house cut its price target anyway.**
- **Revenue $1.924bn, +53% y/y; Adjusted EBITDA $1.614bn, +58% y/y — both MODESTLY BELOW GUIDANCE**, and revenue missed the **~$2.0bn buy-side bogey** (Deutsche Bank · Benjamin Black, 2026-08-06).
- ⚠️ **The calibration point that makes it a genuine break in pattern: "APP has typically exceeded the high end of revenue guidance by approximately 5.5% over the past two years, making the 2Q shortfall notable"** (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06). JPM: **"APP is plummeting −19% pre-market off the back of a Revenue miss (relative to investor expectations) coupled with a guide that I expect investors will characterize as aspirational… APP is decidedly in the PENALTY BOX with investors until next quarter"** (JPM TECH SKETCH · Mark Schilsky, 2026-08-06). UBS: **"The biggest surprise was APP, which somehow managed to miss the high end of its guide, a sentence I didn't think I'd be writing. Ecom was already expected to be a bit of a work in progress, but the GAMING WEAKNESS caught most people off guard"** (UBS TMI · Christina Dwyer, 2026-08-06).
- **The stated cause — AXON model-release timing, not demand:** "APP did not generate its normal level of model improvement during 2Q, but deployed a more meaningful release shortly after quarter-end" (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06). MS: "Management noted that they were unable to roll out a planned major model improvement until the beginning of 3Q, which drove the weaker than expected result… APP reiterated that it expects to drive **>30% long-term growth** and that it is **not observing demand-based or competitive headwinds**" (Morgan Stanley · Matthew Cost / Brian Nowak, 2026-08-06). GS frames it as "the timing and magnitude of core AXON model improvements fell short of management's internal projections (with the company pointing to timing mismatches as opposed to any structural headwinds)" (Goldman Sachs · Eric Sheridan, 2026-08-06).
- **🔴 The 3Q guide is the counter-evidence and it is unusually strong: revenue $2.055–2.085bn = +7–8% q/q "above the typical 3%–5% sequential progression"**, and **in line with Street estimates**; 3Q revenue and EBITDA each **~1% above MS's prior estimates** (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06; Morgan Stanley · Cost/Nowak, 2026-08-06).
- **Consumer (ex-"eCommerce") — the page's key open item finally gets a number: Q2 consumer ad spend finished 28% ABOVE the seasonally elevated 4Q25 level.** MS's arithmetic: that "implies ~13% ecommerce ad-spend growth q/q in 2H:26, which annualizes to **64% y/y growth**, though the strong 4Q seasonality means the actual annual figure could be materially higher" (Morgan Stanley · Cost/Nowak, 2026-08-06). DB adds that **existing advertisers "remain below their potential spending ceilings"** with a large, fragmented, low-penetration advertiser base (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06).
- ⚠️ **Margins declined sequentially — and for the first time the cost line is INFERENCE:** adj. EBITDA margin still ~80–85% but down q/q on "targeted investments in both **cloud hosting/inference costs** and go-to-market (partnerships & advertiser outreach to support scaling Consumer)" (Goldman Sachs · Sheridan, 2026-08-06). A new structural cost item on a business that has been modelled as near-fixed-cost.
- **MAX / supply side held:** "MAX publisher earnings also increased double digits q/q while publisher share remained stable, supporting the view that demand and APP's competitive position remain healthy" (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06).
- **Management's own rebuttal to the third-party trackers:** APP "noted that some third-party data showing market-level headwinds likely fails to capture the ongoing shift to direct payments within mobile games" (Morgan Stanley · Cost/Nowak, 2026-08-06) — i.e. management is explicitly leaning on this page's existing direct-payments thesis as the reason the external data understates it.

## Debate / thesis
- **eCommerce monetization timing:** Bull — "imagine what the stock might do if eComm really starts to inflect" (JPM / Schilsky, 2026-05-27); DB sees the June GA launch on track. Bear — eComm flat q/q in Q1; "lack of Q1 scaling suggests a more drawn-out ramp" (BofA / Dessouky); "Commerce Ads Remains Key Variable" (GS / Sheridan).
- **META as competitive threat (non-IDFA):** Bull — META backing away from the non-IDFA mobile-ad market is a tailwind; "META unlikely to bid on non-IDFA iOS traffic anytime soon" (Edgewater, 2026-05-27); APP +7–10% on the note; "the Bear Case is slowly eroding" (JPM / Schilsky, "APP Boogeyman Backs Off," 2026-05-28). Bear — if META reverses, APP's addressable market compresses (Bernstein / Seidman flagged "META Ad-Tech ambitions (-APP/U)," 2026-04-27).
- **Direct-payments tailwind:** every 5% reduction in app-store fees drives ~5% EBITDA upside at APP in '27 as developers reinvest savings into paid UA; DTC adoption scaling (PLTK 25%→39% of revenue in 12m) (MS / Cost, Nowak, 2026-06-11).
- **Diversification vs. distraction:** APP launched the 'Gist' social network on iOS (2026-05-22) — strategic diversification vs. distraction from core ad/eComm execution.
- **🆕⚠️ The new bear that is NOT Meta — UNITY. (JPM TECH SKETCH · Mark Schilsky, 2026-08-06.)** APP missed on gaming the same night **Unity beat and raised hard (+15–16%: 2Q revenue/EBITDA beat by 6%/19%, 3Q EBITDA guided 24% above the midpoint)** on its Vector AI ramp, and Schilsky asks the question directly: **"in light of Unity's beat/raise, the question must be asked: is Unity negatively impacting APP's growth? It can't be ruled out entirely."** Independent corroboration that Unity's ramp is real and accelerating: **agency checks put Unity D28 IAA growth at ~75% y/y in 1Q26, >80% in 2Q26 and >100% in July 2026**, with **D28 reaching 30–35% of one expert's Unity budget by July 2026** and IAA becoming the primary component within one quarter of launch (@FundaAI, 2026-08-06). ⚠️ **This reframes the competitive bear on this page: the corpus-era bear was META re-entering non-IDFA inventory (still unrefuted but dormant); the live, evidenced share threat in 2026 is Unity's D28/Vector in the same auction. Management's counter is that market-level third-party data misses the direct-payments shift, not that no share moved — that is a non-answer to the Unity question.** Bernstein's desk logged the pair the same morning as **"U EPS (+), APP EPS (−)"** (Bernstein TMT · Tyler Seidman, 2026-08-06). Note the page's own MS direct-payments note was a **paired U/APP** call — the pair has now diverged.
- **🆕 The valuation defence — "attractive even if you value ecommerce at $0" (Morgan Stanley · Matthew Cost / Brian Nowak, 2026-08-06).** MS keeps **Overweight** and argues that at **12x EBITDA and 18x GAAP P/E**, the analysis shows shares are "arguably attractive even when valuing ecommerce ads at $0" — i.e. the entire Consumer optionality this page has debated since 2025 is now free in the price. Their read on the quarter: **"2Q displayed short-term MISEXECUTION, not structural headwinds… despite that misexecution, APP's gaming ad business continues to significantly outperform the broader market."** ⚠️ **The test is explicit and one quarter away: if the 3Q guide (+7–8% q/q vs a 3–5% norm) is met, the "model timing" explanation is validated and the multiple compression is the opportunity; if 3Q also misses, "timing" was the wrong diagnosis and the Unity/share bear owns the name.**
- **Where the sell-side stands:** ⚠️ **ALL THREE MARKS ON THIS PAGE WERE CUT AT THE Q2 PRINT (old values in `## Changelog`): Goldman Sachs (Sheridan) Neutral, PT $585 → $465; Morgan Stanley (Cost/Nowak) Overweight, PT $720 → $650; Deutsche Bank (Black) Buy, PT $660 → $580** (Goldman Sachs · Sheridan, 2026-08-06; Morgan Stanley · Cost/Nowak, 2026-08-06; Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06). Note the pattern — **nobody downgraded, everybody cut the target**: the diagnosis is uniformly "timing/misexecution," which is a rating-preserving explanation, while the numbers came down. GS is the most explicit about withholding: "we look for greater visibility into how medium-term growth drivers against incremental investments translate into topline growth & margins in the coming quarters… we highlight the possibility of our medium-term estimates proving conservative, but look to our industry channel-check work deeper into the quarter before underwriting improved/sustained outperformance" (Goldman Sachs · Sheridan, 2026-08-06). DB "modestly lower[s] our FY26/FY27 revenue and Adjusted EBITDA estimates as the delayed [model release flows through]" (Deutsche Bank · Black, 2026-08-06).
- **Prior sell-side marks (pre-Q2-print, superseded — kept for the drift record):** **Deutsche Bank (Black/Horowitz/Madhukar) — Buy, $660** (initiated Oct 2025 at $705; cut to $660 on 2026-06-05 mgmt meeting; ~80% supply-side / >55% demand-side share). **Goldman Sachs (Sheridan) — Neutral, $585** (cut $710→$585 post-Q4; raised $535→$585 post-Q1). **StoneX/Benchmark (Hickey) — Buy, $775** ("Catalysts Building Into 2Q"). **MS (Cost/Nowak) — Overweight** (paired U/APP note; APP PT not separately stated). ## Catalysts / what to watch
- **🔴 Q3 2026 print — THE decisive one.** Guide $2.055–2.085bn (+7–8% q/q vs a 3–5% seasonal norm), predicated on the AXON model release that slipped out of 2Q landing in 3Q. **Meeting it validates "timing"; missing it retires "timing" as an explanation and hands the name to the share/competition bear.** Watch the Q4 guide in the same print — JPM: "APP is going to have to demonstrate in Q3, AND THE Q4 GUIDE, that this was truly a mere model hiccup and not something more structural" (JPM · Schilsky, 2026-08-06).
- **🆕 Unity's next print** — whether D28/Vector keeps compounding (agency checks: ~75% 1Q26 → >80% 2Q26 → >100% July) while APP's gaming line stays soft. Divergence in the same auction is the cleanest available share test.
- **🆕 Consumer ad-spend trajectory against MS's implied bar** — Q2 spend +28% vs 4Q25 implies ~13% q/q in 2H26 (~64% y/y annualized). This is now a checkable number, not a narrative.
- **🆕 Inference / cloud-hosting cost line** — first quarter it was cited as a sequential margin drag; watch whether ~80–85% adj. EBITDA margin is a plateau or the start of a step-down.
- **Analyst marketing 08-11 (NYC)** — MS SMID Internet: Matt Cost hosting APP/RBLX/U (MS TMT, 2026-08-06); BofA APP/U callback with Omar Dessouky 08-06 (BofA TMT, 2026-08-06); UBS 2Q Internet EPS callback 08-06 with Stephen Ju (UBS TMI, 2026-08-06).
- Whether **META** re-enters the non-IDFA mobile-ad market (dormant, unrefuted).
- App-store fee changes (direct-payments tailwind) — management is now explicitly invoking this to explain why third-party market data understates it.

## Risks
- **🆕 The guidance-beat streak is broken.** A company that exceeded the high end of its revenue guide by ~5.5% on average for two years just missed it — the "APP always sandbags" prior that supported the multiple no longer holds without re-proof.
- **🆕 Unity/Vector taking share in gaming** — the live, evidenced competitive threat, distinct from the dormant META bear.
- **🆕 Inference/cloud-hosting cost creep** on a business modelled as near-fixed-cost.
- eCommerce/Consumer ramp slower than bulls expect (Q2 gave the first hard datapoint: +28% vs 4Q25).
- META competitive re-entry into non-IDFA inventory.
- High-beta / dislocated-name volatility; valuation vs. RDDT peer group.
- Execution-distraction risk from 'Gist' social launch.
