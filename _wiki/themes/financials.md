| SPGI / MCO | Issuance volumes and mix, transaction/non-transaction ratings revenue, subscription growth, retention, analytics ARR and margins | SPGI's Mobility separation changes the perimeter. ## Cross-company debates and read-throughs

**Capital-market activity connects the banks, ratings agencies and alternative managers.** An open financing and exit market can support JPM/GS underwriting, SPGI/MCO ratings activity and BX/KKR/APO realizations. The link is conditional: refinancing differs from acquisition financing, ratings mix matters, and fundraising does not immediately become fee revenue. Track the operating stages separately rather than treating an issuance headline as an earnings upgrade for every company. _Capstone inference from JPM/GS 2026-10-13, MCO 2026-10-21, BX 2026-10-22, SPGI 2026-10-27, KKR 2026-07-30 and APO 2026-08-04 calls._

**🆕 2027 issuance: another record IG year, but slower and more refinancing-led, and some AI funding bypasses rated bonds (Barclays, 2026-11-02).** Barclays' credit teams' first 2027 forecasts (published 10-30) put US IG gross issuance at **$2,100bn (+11%) after $1,900bn (+32%) in 2026e**, US HY **$400bn (+15%)** and US leveraged loans ex-repricings **$470bn (+8%)**, while Europe falls (EUR IG €790bn, -3%; EUR HY €105bn, -23%) and EM sovereign hard-currency falls to $235bn (-12%); the comparable total is **$4,358bn (+3%) after $4,231bn (+17%)**. ⚠️ The note's text says US HY +13% and loans +7%; its own table (Figure 2) gives +15% / +8%, and the table is used here. S&P's Credit Trends shows the same shape (+2.7% in 2027e vs +12.2% in 2026e), on a different basis from SPGI's billed issuance and from Barclays' forecast. Per Bloomberg as cited by Barclays, AI issuers were ~1/3 of the 2026e increase in bond issuance; MCO put AI at close to 50% of its issuance growth and ~40% of revenue growth. Barclays therefore cuts 2027E ratings revenue growth to **7.4% at MCO (from 12.1%) and 7.6% at SPGI (from 11.9%)**, cuts both EPS paths, keeps both OW and prefers [SPGI](../SPGI.md) ($572 PT) to [MCO](../MCO.md) ($575 PT unchanged); [MSCI](../MSCI.md) is a "core OW" at $760. The mix point is the one this page already makes: refinancing differs from acquisition financing, and the AI funding need is "becoming more fragmented across unsecured bonds, data-center financing, structured products, private credit and equity". That ties the ratings agencies to the [AI infrastructure financing](../_dashboards/fin-ai-financing.html) work below: AI dollars raised through private credit or equity are revenue for the asset managers but not for the raters. _Source: Barclays · Manav Patnaik / Wahid Amin / Ronan Kennedy / Brandon Cohen, "SPGI/MCO/MSCI — 2027e another healthy issuance year, just not as good as 2026e; SPGI > MCO; MSCI OW too", 2026-11-02 — [report](../../relat%C3%B3rios%20bons/Delayed_SPGIMCOMSCI_2027e_another_healthy_issuance_year_just_not_as_good_as_2026e_SPGI_MCO_MSCI_OW_too_SPGIMCOMSCI_2027e.html)._

**AI infrastructure is also a financing question.** The new alternative-manager coverage connects to the existing [AI compute-deal timeline](ai-compute-deals.md), [hyperscaler capex](hyperscaler-capex.md) and [datacenter power](ai-datacenter-power.md) work. KKR's Helix is a capital platform; Apollo's Big Sky exposure has a draw and fee-recognition schedule; Blackstone's Crux discussion is a separate structure. Capital commitments, asset originations, investment-manager fees and delivered computing capacity are distinct quantities. Use the timeline's stable deal IDs and preserve the source's dollar and MW bases. _Sources: Helix/KKR announcement 2026-06-11; KKR call 2026-07-30; Apollo call 2026-08-04; Blackstone call 2026-10-22; later filed/press evidence is preserved in the timeline._

**AI can strengthen distribution while disrupting a workflow.** SPGI/MCO own data and analytical products; the question is whether AI increases usage and pricing or substitutes for paid seats. Visa/Mastercard can supply identity, authorization and fraud services to [agentic commerce](agentic-commerce.md), but current pilots do not establish material payment volumes. _Capstone inference from SPGI/MCO/V/MA October 2026 calls; management claims and broker views are separated on the pages._

**Stablecoins and retail platforms overlap with payment networks, without identical economics.** Coinbase earns stablecoin-related revenue alongside trading fees; Robinhood's product expansion seeks a larger share of customer balances and activity. Visa and Mastercard are also building services around new payment flows. Track distribution, settlement, compliance and funding separately: adoption of one rail does not by itself prove the displacement of a network's entire fee base. _Capstone inference from HOOD 2026-10-27, COIN 2026-10-29 and V/MA October 2026 calls._

**Credit bureaus are an early read on consumer credit and mortgage for the banks.** TransUnion's Q3 showed card and banking +3% and slowing consumer lending with stable delinquencies. Equifax saw "some slowing in FI and auto volumes late in 3Q" and is watching it closely. Both bureaus guide to a weak mortgage market: TransUnion's mortgage inquiries fell about 16% in Q3 and are guided down more than 20% in Q4, and Equifax expects 2026 originations down 6.5–7.5%. These read across to [JPM](../JPM.md) card and mortgage banking and to [V](../V.md)/[MA](../MA.md) volumes. The bureaus' own debate (FICO pricing, VantageScore adoption, an FHFA tri-merge versus bi-merge decision) moves EFX and TRU together. Equifax sizes GSE-related originations moving to bi-merge at roughly $80m of revenue (including FICO scores delivered with each file) and $38m of margin. TransUnion sizes the same move at about $33m of revenue ex-FICO and $0.15 of adjusted EPS, assuming equal bureau share. _Sources: EFX call 2026-10-20 and TRU release/call 2026-10-27, linked on the company pages; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._

**Index fees ride market levels; private-markets data rides the alternative-manager build-out.** MSCI's asset-based fees grew 24.9% in Q3 on record ETF AUM while the basis-point fee fell from 2.41 to 2.28. That is the same passive/custom-index tailwind and fee-mix risk as SPGI's S&P Dow Jones Indices, and it is partly market beta rather than structural share. MSCI's Private Capital Solutions is a data read-across to [BX](../BX.md)/[KKR](../KKR.md)/[APO](../APO.md) private-wealth and LP-transparency demand, and Moody's is MSCI's partner on private-credit assessments. _Sources: MSCI Q3 release/call 2026-10-20; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._

**The exchanges share one volume cycle and one fight over new retail venues.** Q3 activity was strong across all four: CME ADV +16%, ICE futures and options ADV +31%, Cboe index-options ADV +24% and Nasdaq Index revenue +39%. Each page tests whether this is structural hedging demand or a volatility spike. Perpetual futures and prediction markets tie them to [HOOD](../HOOD.md) and [COIN](../COIN.md) as both partners and rivals. CME owns 51% of FanDuel Prediction Markets. Cboe launches its contracts with Robinhood as a partner, while Robinhood routes prediction contracts to its own Rothera exchange. ICE holds a Polymarket stake, and its adjusted EPS excludes the fair-value gains. Index licences run across the group. CME pays Nasdaq for Nasdaq-100 futures and owns 27% of S&P Dow Jones Indices. Cboe's SPX licence, extended to 2051 with a royalty step-up from 2027, pays S&P DJI; SPGI's CFO expects "a couple percentage points" of extra index growth from it in 2027. In fixed income, ICE's ~$6.0bn all-cash MarketAxess purchase, expected to close in Q1 2027, consolidates credit e-trading. _Sources: CME 2026-10-21, NDAQ 2026-10-22, ICE 2026-10-29 and CBOE 2026-10-30 releases/calls and SPGI call 2026-10-27, linked on the company pages; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._

**ICE Mortgage Technology is the cleaner mortgage-volume read for the bureaus.** ICE's Origination Technology revenue grew 1% in Q3, and its Q4 transaction revenue is guided down. Equifax's U.S. mortgage revenue grew 15% reported but about 1% ex-FICO. Bureau mortgage growth is therefore mostly price and FICO pass-through, and ICE's closed-loan revenue is the better volume signal for [EFX](../EFX.md) and [TRU](../TRU.md). _Sources: ICE Q3 release/call 2026-10-29; EFX call 2026-10-20; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._

**The asset managers finance AI infrastructure, and their wealth channels are diverging.** BlackRock's AI Infrastructure Partnership closed the Aligned Data Centers purchase (about $40bn of enterprise value) as its first deployment. Both also finance Meta data-center campuses through joint ventures with Meta, BlackRock in Texas and Blue Owl in Louisiana. Blue Owl now cites "almost 14 GW" of leased and owned data-center capacity, down from 15.3 GW a quarter earlier with no reconciliation and no stated GW basis. The [AI compute-deal timeline](ai-compute-deals.md) keeps these amounts and MW bases separate. In private credit, Blue Owl's OCIC redemption requests fell for a second quarter, while Blackstone's BCRED requests reached 10% of NAV against a 5% quarterly limit. BlackRock is also the largest client of MSCI's index fees (19.8% of MSCI Index revenue in 9M 2026), so iShares flows feed asset-based fees at [MSCI](../MSCI.md), SPGI and Nasdaq. _Sources: BLK call 2026-10-14; OWL Q3 and Q2 calls 2026-10-29 and 2026-07-30; BX call 2026-10-22; MSCI Q3 10-Q 2026-10-20; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._
