## How to compare the businesses

| Group | Operating measures to follow | Comparability guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| JPM / GS | Net interest income, advisory fees, trading revenue, deposits, provisions, expenses, capital and tangible returns | JPM's managed revenue differs from GAAP revenue. GS is more exposed to capital-markets activity. Neither bank's revenue should be compared mechanically with payment-network revenue. |
| SPGI / MCO | Issuance volumes and mix, transaction/non-transaction ratings revenue, subscription growth, retention, analytics ARR and margins | SPGI's Mobility separation changes the perimeter. Moody's Analytics ARR is a recurring revenue measure; it is not ratings issuance volume or reported quarterly revenue. |
| MSCI (index peer: SPGI's S&P DJI) | Organic recurring subscription Run Rate, Retention Rate, net new recurring sales, asset-based-fee Run Rate, ETF AUM/flows and basis-point fee, adjusted EBITDA margin, FCF per share and leverage | Run Rate and Retention Rate are company-defined and are not revenue. The asset-based-fee Run Rate annualizes period-end AUM, so it moves with markets. MSCI retention excludes asset-based fees and is not comparable to Moody's Analytics ARR or SPGI subscription measures. |
| EFX / TRU | Organic constant-currency revenue with and without FICO royalties, mortgage revenue ex-FICO versus inquiries, non-mortgage financial-services growth, EFX Workforce Solutions/government, adjusted EBITDA margin ex-FICO, leverage and FCF conversion | FICO mortgage-score royalties are a near-zero-margin pass-through. In Q3, EFX U.S. mortgage was +15% reported but +1% ex-FICO; FICO adds about 3pt to TRU's FY2026 organic growth and costs about 80bp of margin. Compare the two only ex-FICO. TRU's inquiry counts include pre-qualification pulls. |
| BX / KKR / APO | Fee-paying assets, fundraising, deployments, management fees, fee-related earnings, realizations and liquidity | AUM is not revenue. FRE, DE, ANI, TOE and SRE are company-defined measures. KKR's K-Series reporting/compensation change and Apollo's insurance spreads require explicit adjustments before comparison. |
| V / MA | Payment/purchase volumes, cross-border activity, processed/switched transactions, incentives and services growth | Reported, constant-currency and adjusted growth differ. Cross-border figures can exclude intra-Europe activity. Incentive timing and acquisitions affect revenue growth. |
| HOOD / COIN | Customer assets, net deposits, trading activity, transaction revenue, interest/stablecoin income, subscriptions and expenses | Customer assets are not company cash. Coinbase's total revenue differs from net revenue. Robinhood's adjusted EPS is newly introduced; investment marks and SBC require separate treatment. |

## Cross-company debates and read-throughs

**Capital-market activity connects the banks, ratings agencies and alternative managers.** An open financing and exit market can support JPM/GS underwriting, SPGI/MCO ratings activity and BX/KKR/APO realizations. The link is conditional: refinancing differs from acquisition financing, ratings mix matters, and fundraising does not immediately become fee revenue. Track the operating stages separately rather than treating an issuance headline as an earnings upgrade for every company. _Capstone inference from JPM/GS 2026-10-13, MCO 2026-10-21, BX 2026-10-22, SPGI 2026-10-27, KKR 2026-07-30 and APO 2026-08-04 calls._

**AI infrastructure is also a financing question.** The new alternative-manager coverage connects to the existing [AI compute-deal timeline](ai-compute-deals.md), [hyperscaler capex](hyperscaler-capex.md) and [datacenter power](ai-datacenter-power.md) work. KKR's Helix is a capital platform; Apollo's Big Sky exposure has a draw and fee-recognition schedule; Blackstone's Crux discussion is a separate structure. Capital commitments, asset originations, investment-manager fees and delivered computing capacity are distinct quantities. Use the timeline's stable deal IDs and preserve the source's dollar and MW bases. _Sources: Helix/KKR announcement 2026-06-11; KKR call 2026-07-30; Apollo call 2026-08-04; Blackstone call 2026-10-22; later filed/press evidence is preserved in the timeline._

**AI can strengthen distribution while disrupting a workflow.** SPGI/MCO own data and analytical products; the question is whether AI increases usage and pricing or substitutes for paid seats. Visa/Mastercard can supply identity, authorization and fraud services to [agentic commerce](agentic-commerce.md), but current pilots do not establish material payment volumes. _Capstone inference from SPGI/MCO/V/MA October 2026 calls; management claims and broker views are separated on the pages._

**Stablecoins and retail platforms overlap with payment networks, without identical economics.** Coinbase earns stablecoin-related revenue alongside trading fees; Robinhood's product expansion seeks a larger share of customer balances and activity. Visa and Mastercard are also building services around new payment flows. Track distribution, settlement, compliance and funding separately: adoption of one rail does not by itself prove the displacement of a network's entire fee base. _Capstone inference from HOOD 2026-10-27, COIN 2026-10-29 and V/MA October 2026 calls._

**Credit bureaus are an early read on consumer credit and mortgage for the banks.** TransUnion's Q3 showed card and banking +3% and slowing consumer lending with stable delinquencies. Equifax saw "some slowing in FI and auto volumes late in 3Q" and is watching it closely. Both bureaus guide to a weak mortgage market: TransUnion's mortgage inquiries fell about 16% in Q3 and are guided down more than 20% in Q4, and Equifax expects 2026 originations down 6.5–7.5%. These read across to [JPM](../JPM.md) card and mortgage banking and to [V](../V.md)/[MA](../MA.md) volumes. The bureaus' own debate (FICO pricing, VantageScore adoption, an FHFA tri-merge versus bi-merge decision) moves EFX and TRU together. Equifax sizes GSE-related originations moving to bi-merge at roughly $80m of revenue (including FICO scores delivered with each file) and $38m of margin. TransUnion sizes the same move at about $33m of revenue ex-FICO and $0.15 of adjusted EPS, assuming equal bureau share. _Sources: EFX call 2026-10-20 and TRU release/call 2026-10-27, linked on the company pages; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._

**Index fees ride market levels; private-markets data rides the alternative-manager build-out.** MSCI's asset-based fees grew 24.9% in Q3 on record ETF AUM while the basis-point fee fell from 2.41 to 2.28. That is the same passive/custom-index tailwind and fee-mix risk as SPGI's S&P Dow Jones Indices, and it is partly market beta rather than structural share. MSCI's Private Capital Solutions is a data read-across to [BX](../BX.md)/[KKR](../KKR.md)/[APO](../APO.md) private-wealth and LP-transparency demand, and Moody's is MSCI's partner on private-credit assessments. _Sources: MSCI Q3 release/call 2026-10-20; Capstone inference, 2026-11-02._
