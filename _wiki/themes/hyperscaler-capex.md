## What it is / why it matters
Hyperscaler capex (MSFT, AMZN, GOOGL, META, ORCL) is the single biggest **demand signal** for the entire AI hardware chain — GPUs/ASICs, memory, optical, power, semicap. Tracking the trajectory (and the gap between **consensus**, our **house model**, and the **street-high**) is how we size the cycle and judge whether the supply names are pricing too much or too little. This page is the synthesis layer over the AI_Demand_Capex dashboard.

## Recent signals (Jul 30) — the post-print rule change: reported capex stops being a usable proxy for silicon demand
- **★ THE STRUCTURAL POINT OF THIS PRINT SEASON — both [[MSFT]] and [[META]] now have mechanisms that flatter reported capex optics, so reported capex is becoming a WORSE proxy for silicon demand. Track cash PP&E / gross build instead (Rothschild & Co Redburn · Rowan Adley, 2026-07-30).** MSFT flatters via **lease classification** (useful lives 15 → 25 yrs shifts future DC leases from finance to operating, and only finance leases sit inside MSFT's capex definition); META flatters via **off-balance-sheet JVs** (the BlackRock deal). Adley's exact framing: "both companies now have mechanisms that flatter reported capex optics… so the metric to track from here is **cash PP&E / gross build rather than headline capex**, ie **reported capex is becoming a WORSE proxy for silicon demand**." This is the operative caveat for every row in the State-of-play table above — the 2027E columns mix bases.
- **[[MSFT]] — the desk split flagged on Jul 29 resolves as follows, and the apples-to-apples number is UP, not down (Rothschild/Adley, 2026-07-30; TMTB, 2026-07-30; JPM · Mark Schilsky desk, 2026-07-30).** Management's own words (per the call): CY26 investment expectations are **unchanged outside the useful-life adjustment**, and "the shift from finance to operating leases adjusts our expectation to **approximately $175 billion**." So: **~$175B reported ≈ ~$190B on the prior basis — a reiteration, not a cut** (~$15B moved outside the reported line). Forward:
  - **FY27 apples-to-apples ≈ $235-245B (+60-65% y/y); FY27 as-reported ≈ $205-215B (+40-45%)** — "the **entire headline reduction is classification**, so no evidence of any slowdown in intent" (Rothschild/Adley).
